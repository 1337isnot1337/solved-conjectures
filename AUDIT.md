## Further corrections

- **Blázsik–Lemons.** A hypergraph with a single singleton edge satisfies the pairwise intersection restriction vacuously. Every coloring makes that edge monochromatic. The published statement now requires every edge to have at least two vertices. [Proof comparison](audit/records/results--blazsik-lemons-one-intersection.md).
- **Erdős–Purdy.** A single block containing every point admits a cyclic transitive action but occurs just once. A collinear point set determines only one line. The design statement needs a nontrivial pairwise balanced design, and the geometric statement needs a noncollinear set. [Proof comparison](audit/records/results--erdos-block-size-multiplicity.md).
- **Erdős problem 607 and Kotesovec's asymptotic.** Szemerédi and Trotter had proved the exponential-in-square-root upper bound in 1983. The stronger November 2026 target is Václav Kotesovec's proposed sharp asymptotic for Seiichi Manyama's sequence. The exact counter matched all 10,000 OEIS terms. [1983 paper](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02579194) · [OEIS A401948](https://oeis.org/A401948) · [audit record](audit/records/results--erdos-607-line-sizes.md).
- **Proof status and sources.** Nine partial-result pages explicitly say that independent proof review is pending. The overview now discloses this. The Csordas–Vishnyakova landing page reported conflicting Lean statuses. The Merino–Welsh DOI and Romik–Śniady reference were corrected. [Csordas–Vishnyakova](audit/records/results--csordas-vishnyakova.md) · [Merino–Welsh](audit/records/docs--merino-welsh-research.md) · [Romik–Śniady citation](audit/records/results--pantone-bona-third-largest-terminal.md).

## What was examined

The base is [main at d676ae5](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/tree/d676ae52308b71d65beac020fb2a1a591a9e6d34). It has **196 result directories** including 171 ranked entries, 16 directories under `docs/research-results`, one supporting Németh–Seberry–Shu manuscript directory, 57 standalone or catalogue notes, and two packages expressly linked on the research branch. Records can describe the same project at different stages. The 272 dossiers therefore do not count 272 conjectures or 272 independent proofs. The ledger hashes 903 distinct source-document paths.

For every record, the audit used a title-based Crossref search and a DataCite search targeting Zenodo. It also used 238 targeted web searches, direct retrieval attempts for up to three source links per record and forward-citation searches seeded from up to four cited DOI and four arXiv identifiers per record. The OpenAlex responses retain up to the 100 most recent citing works per indexed source. The ledger preserves returned titles, request statuses, and accessible source links. Some sources appear under more than one identifier. Search results are leads. A removal required comparison of the theorem or counterexample and a dated public source. The external archive comparisons used pinned versions of [Principia Math](https://github.com/PrincipiaMathOrg/math-solutions/tree/47371706c400c79d06e3fb62190fcf6f7a725c85), [The Omega Institute](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/tree/12036e5e3cc8d8d9f92467546faa8e37a61702b4) and [10m-u](https://github.com/10m-u/open-problems-catalog/tree/ca8d4d44ba3db527f19f28928c240168f545ecfe). [Forward-citation responses](audit/evidence/forward-citations.json).

A dated overlap can also support retaining an entry. Omega’s Brown–Mol translated-annulus counterexample came **after** the repository proof. Rather’s earlier order-one counterexample was already credited and leaves the all-orders root classification distinct. The Hegyvári paper resolved the irrational-ratio case, leaving a rational-ratio problem. These comparisons are recorded individually. [Brown–Mol](audit/records/results--brown-mol.md) · [Rather](audit/records/results--rather-book-domination-roots.md) · [Hegyvári](audit/records/results--hegyvari-floor-doubling.md).

## Verification and limits

- The local computational logs record the command, exit status, elapsed time and raw output digest for each completed run. Individual dossiers link applicable logs. [Computational replay logs](audit/evidence/replays).
- The independent priority checks use exact integer and rational arithmetic. They compare small weighted-poset Möbius values directly against the earlier complete-graph formula and compute the earlier rank-eight examples independently. [Executable comparisons](audit/evidence/check_priority_examples.py).
- Ma's version-one PDF has SHA-256 `622436c49f5c3c36801b82217ad444ffc67d0cb374f007cb9cb0efe5edce6a35`. The accompanying ZIP has SHA-256 `ce64229930beb11fb567fcfd9c6310addc34fa0e7ce40c3e970cc50afe5aae9e`. The original `verify_all.py` passed all five stages in 660.37 seconds, including the partition of the compact region. The mathematical reductions are in the paper. [Archive](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23639859) · [replay log](audit/evidence/ma-certificate-replay.txt).
- For 551 historical Lean receipts containing explicit Lean source hashes, the audit compared the file bytes with this frozen tree. Some preserve older, failed or intermediate builds. The comparisons record the status supplied by the receipt and the mismatched paths. An old receipt alone cannot establish the behavior of a later source revision. [Receipt ledger](audit/evidence/lean-receipts.json).
- The corrected catalogue passed its README check, 11 site tests, the Lean documentation check, and a complete site build and validation. The built site contained 189 results, 934 HTML pages, 933 sitemap URLs and 41,400 MathML formulas. [Publication PR](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/pull/891).

**Scope of assurance.** A dossier marked “Screened” records the actual literature and verification work available for that entry. It carries no independent priority certification. Source outlines link the original authors' proof steps. The audit did not independently derive every inference in every manuscript or freshly rebuild the entire Lean project. Searches can miss unindexed work, alternative terminology, private proofs, and older references. Each proof finding, executable check and historical verification receipt is identified separately so the reader can decide what the evidence supports.
