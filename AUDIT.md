## Further corrections

- **Asgarli–Ghioca–Yip.** The logarithmic estimate requires fixed monomial degree at least two. In degree one the projective closure is a line and already meets every projective line. The proof had the restriction, while two summaries omitted it. [Audit record](audit/records/results--asgarli-ghioca-yip-blocking-pencil.md) · [scope correction](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/pull/895).
- **Blázsik–Lemons.** A hypergraph with a single singleton edge satisfies the pairwise intersection restriction vacuously. Every coloring makes that edge monochromatic. The published statement now requires every edge to have at least two vertices. [Proof comparison](audit/records/results--blazsik-lemons-one-intersection.md).
- **Erdős–Purdy.** A single block containing every point admits a cyclic transitive action but occurs just once. A collinear point set determines only one line. The design statement needs a nontrivial pairwise balanced design, and the geometric statement needs a noncollinear set. [Proof comparison](audit/records/results--erdos-block-size-multiplicity.md).
- **Erdős problem 607 and Kotesovec's asymptotic.** Szemerédi and Trotter had proved the exponential-in-square-root upper bound in 1983. The stronger November 2026 target is Václav Kotesovec's proposed sharp asymptotic for Seiichi Manyama's sequence. The exact counter matched all 10,000 OEIS terms. [1983 paper](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02579194) · [OEIS A401948](https://oeis.org/A401948) · [audit record](audit/records/results--erdos-607-line-sizes.md).
- **Proof status and sources.** Nine partial-result pages explicitly say that independent proof review is pending. The overview now discloses this. The Csordas–Vishnyakova landing page reported conflicting Lean statuses. The Merino–Welsh DOI and Romik–Śniady reference were corrected. [Csordas–Vishnyakova](audit/records/results--csordas-vishnyakova.md) · [Merino–Welsh](audit/records/docs--merino-welsh-research.md) · [Romik–Śniady citation](audit/records/results--pantone-bona-third-largest-terminal.md).

## What was examined

It has **196 result directories** including 171 ranked entries, 16 directories under `docs/research-results`, one supporting Németh–Seberry–Shu manuscript directory, 57 standalone or catalogue notes, and two packages expressly linked on the research branch. Records can describe the same project at different stages. The 272 dossiers therefore do not count 272 conjectures or 272 independent proofs. It records the revision, file mode, Git blob, byte count and path for each entry.

For every record, the audit used a title-based Crossref search and a DataCite search targeting Zenodo. It also used 238 targeted web searches, direct retrieval attempts for up to three source links per record and forward-citation searches seeded from up to four cited DOI and four arXiv identifiers per record. The OpenAlex responses retain up to the 100 most recent citing works per indexed source. The ledger preserves returned titles, request statuses, and accessible source links. The [bibliography](audit/evidence/bibliography.json) records authors, publication dates, venues and persistent identifiers for 418 cited works. Public Git author and committer names are recorded separately from the mathematical attribution in the source documents. Some sources appear under more than one identifier. Search results are leads. **Scope of assurance.** A dossier marked “Screened” records the actual literature and verification work available for that entry. It carries no independent priority certification. Source outlines link the original authors' proof steps. The audit did not independently derive every inference in every manuscript or freshly rebuild the entire Lean project. Searches can miss unindexed work, alternative terminology, private proofs, and older references. Sixty-eight dossiers include a targeted mathematical, scope or priority comparison. A further field locates qualifications and prior-work passages from 139 records; these passages retain their original attribution. Each proof finding, executable check and historical verification receipt is identified separately so the reader can decide what the evidence supports.
