# Priority and scope of the fractional Taylor-section result

Principia Math in Progress proved Kovalev’s Conjecture 1.2 for the square root in September and posed fractional powers and sharp segment bounds as further questions. This supplement proves zero-freeness for exponents between zero and one-half and derives the stated segment bounds. [Earlier proof](https://github.com/PrincipiaMathOrg/math-solutions/blob/47371706c400c79d06e3fb62190fcf6f7a725c85/site-backup/mathdb-369171/mathdb-369171-solution.tex). The dated checks below describe the repository work.

[Kovalev’s Conjecture 1.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.04544v1#S1) is the square-root assertion for d>2; the source proves d=2. Our argument treats every length and all powers up to one-half. [Kang’s earlier Conjecture 1.3 counterexample](https://mathdb.com/abs/2610.00766) concerns a stronger and different trigonometric inequality. It is not claimed here; the exact negative sign was independently reproduced.

The audit checked the beta representation, independent gamma-product coupling, Möbius disk mapping under the fractional power, exact binomial weights and endpoint at z=1. The zero-free result is all-degree and purely analytic.

No Lean, external review or absolute priority claim is made.
