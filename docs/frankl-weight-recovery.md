# Frankl arithmetic recovery

The local execution environment became unavailable during two kernel replays.
The interrupted graph prototype and first512-row batch were not recorded as
passed. Earlier completed local receipts are separate from fresh hosted gates.

The arithmetic helper and128-row producer were recovered with their exact
recorded SHA256 values. The recovery workflow regenerates both pilot sources
and requires their recorded hashes. It also regenerates all75 short-period
blocks and requires the pre-outage manifest hash
18246e0188eedf9ca9074b1d8bec301cc70f0ee4570aa20d0ee36789378a2ebc.
That manifest commits to the producer and all150 generated source files.

A fresh g00 gate must pass before the workflow publishes the reconstructed
candidate files. The gate rebuilds all30 project dependencies, audits every
project declaration and all256 private arithmetic proofs, and requires an exact
stock-kernel replay. The other18 groups run on the resulting immutable commit.
Accepted imports, audit requirements, and replay registration are unchanged.

The period-ten producer and independent finite-set replay were reconstructed as
new version2 candidates. Their bytes are not claimed to match the lost
version1 source. Two independent generations must be byte-identical. The replay
checks all9,088 explicit support maps, their generator/core images, injectivity,
and transported natural weights. This replay does not establish representative
arithmetic or replace any required Lean proof.
