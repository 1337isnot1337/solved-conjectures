# Review and priority for independently subdivided decorated trees

The WadeDavis Codex research session developed the [full-box theorem](DECORATED-SUBDIVISION-DENSITY.md) for the existing [Stanley tree campaign](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/315). One contributor derived the proof from the earlier campaign's fringe, minimum-leaf and core reconstruction arguments. A separate internal reviewer independently checked the complete proof, including limiting contractions, integer counting, orientation of equal splits and recovery of the rooted caps. This is an internally reviewed written proof with exact finite checks. Lean verification and external refereeing remain open.

## Independent review

The reviewed manuscript DECORATED-SUBDIVISION-DENSITY.md has SHA-256 `9c6286e84675e6e21044d1b27e17d1b8039d15beb2470a3b6c43d72b90612672`. Its density and bounded-width statement passes review for arbitrary fixed terminal caps and an arbitrary fixed choice of varying internal edges.

1. Two-scale recognition fixes the finite possibilities for the suppressed competing skeleton, its degrees and the cap multiset. The total edge length is the sum of arm orders and internal lengths. The signed endpoint law and that total determine the normalized arclength distance distribution for positive real realizations of both sides. After scaling in a positive rationally independent target direction, collapsing zero-length edges preserves the limiting distance law by sampling each edge with probability proportional to its length. A contracted cluster containing only hubs has boundary degree at least three. Every limiting rival leaf has an original rival skeleton leaf as a preimage. Minimum-leaf uniqueness for the independent target metric tree therefore gives exactly the target shape and all its metric lengths. Each of the original rival leaves survives in a distinct leaf cluster, and a leaf cluster containing even one hub and just one old leaf has degree at least two. No bivalent intermediate cluster remains. This justifies individual edge localization for every mixed choice of fixed and varying internal paths.
2. Partitioning the combined target/rival signed endpoint pairs into equal-distance classes is an exact finite linear description of equality of their signed measures. Requiring each class to balance works when several classes collapse to the same distance as well. The affine offsets from the bounded caps and lower bounds on actual integer stem lengths can all be retained for the real relaxation. Failure for every allowed contraction and bijection is a finite union of closed polyhedra after resolving signs and the minimum of differences. A full-dimensional projected recession cone contains a strictly positive rationally independent direction. One may choose a rival lift separately at each point of a ray. Total length keeps the rescaled parameters bounded, and a common subsequence and fixed combinatorial type suffice. The quantitative target window width divided by the ray parameter has a strictly positive limit, contradicting failed localization. Each remaining rational recession cone then has a rational annihilator and yields one bounded-width slab. The lattice count follows by conditioning on every coordinate except one with nonzero coefficient.
3. In the oriented histogram, a retained-endpoint proper label is an edge partition with one direction on one side and the rest on the other at the unique hub shared by the two connected edge sets. Its sign distinguishes the single direction from its complement, which cannot be another single direction at a vertex of degree at least three. For a contracted internal cut, the omitted bridge uses one boundary edge on each of the two hub subclusters. The degree sum leaves at least two other directions on each side. These labels cannot mask a retained endpoint. Singleton exterior impulses and both endpoints of a retained internal path are therefore fixed exactly. Counting both orientations is essential for a balanced split. When internal path contributions are removed, the sum of exterior stem histograms depends on the separate multisets of orders and cap sizes, so no premature identification of cap assignments is required. Distinct edges in a reduced contracted cluster give different splits of its external directions, and multiplicities recover the exact path lengths.
4. The connected-subset polynomial separates the two needed windows after clearing a bounded denominator and bounded negative powers. A no-hub subset lies wholly within one cap, one arm or one internal path and involves at most one active label. A proper connected hub subset omits a branch leading to a terminal hub with at least two exterior arms. Consequently the full and single-omission exterior windows, with all retained internal labels, can arise only from the entire core. Their ratio isolates the unary-normalized rooted cap factor, including a unit cap. The earlier rooted factor uniqueness then yields the unmarked tree. The additive constants in this separation are controlled because the total of all recovered fixed internal lengths is fixed.

It checked 280 skeleton/mask choices, 5,704 isolated endpoint windows in the oriented first differences and 720 directed contracted-edge splits. It verified full ambient first-difference coefficients and the compact edge's oriented multiplicities for mixed fixed lengths and unequal cap orders. The finite calculation checks this aspect only; the density and against-all-rivals scope rest on the written proof.

## Scope and prior results

The bounded eligibility search on 27 November 2026 compared both permanent branches, the open issue and the active publication queue with the original question, its source aliases, and work on twig lengths, decorated trees, tree reconstruction from distance distributions and asymptotic reconstruction under independent subdivision. The ordinary chromatic symmetric function is the only supplied input. The exceptional count concerns lengths assigned on one arbitrary fixed skeleton with prescribed rooted terminal trees. It gives no verdict for an individual exceptional tree or on the original all-trees conjecture. [Stanley](https://doi.org/10.1006/aima.1995.1020)

Crew determines the size of the subtree spanning the branching vertices and the multiset of twig lengths leading out of it. Martin, Morin and Wagner identify spiders and derive the degree and path data. Wang, Yu and Zhang's connected-subset invariant and two-branch-vertex result are also inputs or comparators. The skeleton and terminal decorations here can each have arbitrarily many branch vertices. [Crew](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.disc.2021.112682) · [Martin, Morin and Wagner](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0609339) · [Wang, Yu and Zhang](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.03980)

Loebl and Sereni treat caterpillars and related weighted families. Zeng studies multiplicity-isolated weights in proper trees, Awan uses a first derivative and structural restrictions on proper trees, and Orellana and Tomba reduce the general question to particular proper trees. The screening found no earlier density estimate for independent full-box lengths on arbitrarily decorated skeletons with any mixture of fixed and varying internal edges. [Loebl and Sereni](https://kam.mff.cuni.cz/~loebl/clanky/LoSe17rev.pdf) · [Zeng](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18179) · [Awan](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13464) · [Orellana and Tomba](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18940)

The campaign's [arbitrary-core theorem](ARBITRARY-CORE.md) gives the earlier balanced-window estimate for arbitrary caps and a full-box estimate with leaf-count assumptions. Its [distance certificate](PATH-DISTANCE-CERTIFICATE.md) treats independent subdivision of all edges of a bare reduced tree. Arbitrary fixed caps and fixed internal paths can impose exact linear relations on those edges. The new proof applies directly to those prescribed faces of length space.

## Reproducible checks

The [arm and cap verifier](verify_decorated_subdivision_density.py) checks 372 assignments on 31 reduced skeletons, including 186 with mixed internal lengths, 169 with a dominant exterior arm, 816 exact internal intervals, 1,308 short oriented cuts and 864 cap-factor ratios. For 124 cases it independently computes the entire connected-subset polynomial evaluated at finite-field points from the actual subdivided unit tree. The [independent cut verifier](verify_decorated_endpoint_windows.py) supplied the counts in the referee report. The asymptotic estimate and the assertion against every competitor follow from the written proof.
