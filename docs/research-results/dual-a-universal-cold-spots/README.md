# Universal Gaussian cold spots of dual type A

For every rank $`n\ge1`$ and every positive Gaussian parameter, the global minima of the Gaussian sum on $`A_n^*`$ occur exactly at its deep holes, the points farthest from the lattice. In the standard realization obtained by projecting $`\mathbb Z^{n+1}`$ onto the sum-zero hyperplane, their coordinates modulo one are equally spaced around the circle. The proof also evaluates the minimum and shows that the Hessian there is positive definite. [Complete proof](PROOF.md)

This answers the dual type-A part of Bachoc, Moustrou, Vallentin and Zimmermann’s §1.6. Rank one is classical, rank two is the hexagonal case of Baernstein, and the checkerboard theorem already gives rank three. Cinna Davis treated sufficiently small and sufficiently large Gaussian parameters for every rank. The advance is the classification at all intermediate parameters in ranks four and above. Universality for arbitrary lattices with stable cold spots remains open. Codex derived the all-parameter proof in the WadeDavis research session. An independent Codex reviewer checked the argument within the project. The proof combines Chan–Liu’s circular summation identity with strict concavity of the logarithm. The verifier checks the product formula with exact integer coefficients and the Hessian with finite numerical lattice sums. There is no Lean formalization or external referee report.
