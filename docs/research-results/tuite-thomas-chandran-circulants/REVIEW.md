# Review of the monophonic circulant order spectrum

Tuite, Thomas and Chandran conjectured existence at every order from eleven onward. The MathDB manuscript of 2 November 2026 ruled out order 15 and reported finite positive examples through order 40. The new assertion is the complete order spectrum, with explicit jump sets in all three residue classes for the unbounded part. The earlier obstruction and examples retain their original credit. [Original Conjecture 4.5](https://oro.open.ac.uk/92086/13/92086final.pdf#page=21) · [Dated prior proof](https://mathdb.com/pdf/2611.00020v1)

I checked that the jump sets are symmetric, exclude triangles, and cover each nonjump as a difference. For an independent triple with private neighbors for two of its pairs, those private neighbors share the middle vertex, so triangle-freeness excludes their mutual edge. This constructs an induced path containing the triple. I tested the endpoint and hole assignments in the short-arc table algebraically, including the least allowed parameters and the condition preventing wraparound. Outside a short arc each cyclic gap must be a short nonjump. Summing the three gaps gives the one exceptional gap multiset in the $`3k+1`$ class and the three listed multisets in the $`3k`$ class. The displayed neighbors and the stated reflections yield private pairs in each case. The finite boundary witnesses, the smaller forbidden orders, and the order-15 twins account for every remaining order. [Proof](README.md)

The supplied verifier passed 317 finite-witness triples, 748,912 uniform-family regression triples and all 128 symmetric jump masks at order 15. I also enumerated all 509 symmetric jump masks at orders one through fifteen independently. For each diameter-two triangle-free graph I extended paths only when its new vertex had no earlier neighbor other than the endpoint, and recorded all triples contained in an induced path. This direct test found precisely orders 4, 5, 8, 11, 12, 13 and 14. The unbounded part follows from the symbolic constructions. I searched the original terminology, the general-position survey cited by the earlier manuscript, and indexed papers and discussions on diameter-two monophonic circulants.
