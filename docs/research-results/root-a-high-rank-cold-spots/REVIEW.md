# Independent mathematical review of the compact-scale high-rank root-A theorem

A separate Codex reviewer in the WadeDavis research session checked the source manuscript (SHA-256 `da8bead18c0c9c27fa5095b0312a4d6cbbae1f6eaec37473cdaaa30ac19bd7a2`) on 27 November 2026 UTC. I found no mathematical gap in the exact equality classification or the uniform leading asymptotic. No numerical estimate or specialist computation is required for this review.

1. **Lattice geometry.** The root inequalities imply every integral zero-sum Voronoi inequality by unit transfers from negative to positive coordinates. The projected cube description follows by translating all coordinates by a common constant. Strict convexity of squared norm restricts its farthest points to projected cube vertices of largest norm. Complementary indices give two distinct common residues for odd $`N`$ and one for even $`N`$.
2. **Single-site inequality.** After Poisson summation, Jacobi gives the real factors $`1+2q^{2r-1}\cos(2\pi y)+q^{4r-2}`$. The log Fourier-series second derivative has coefficient with sign $`(-1)^r`$, so every summand is largest at $`y=1/2`$, and at least the first is strictly positive there. Thus $`v(y)\le v(1/2)`$. The midpoint energy gap is uniformly quadratic by periodic compactness, uniqueness and strict curvature. Absolute convergence of the differentiated series is uniform even if the fixed compact interval has large upper endpoint.
3. **Uniform central probability.** All real tilt centers can be reduced modulo the integers after centering each variable. Adjacent atoms with uniformly positive mass yield a Gaussian envelope on the full inversion interval. On the central interval the real cost of the odd cubic in the log characteristic function is quadratic. The errors $`Nt^4`$, $`N^2t^6`$, and $`B^2t^2`$ each integrate to order $`N^{-1}`$ relative to the leading Gaussian. This justifies the local estimate for arbitrary independent phases and bounded offsets, with no separate identical-distribution hypothesis.
4. **Localization.** On $`\sum_i k_i=\sum_i x_i=0`$, the tilted energy is the original energy plus $`Nu^2`$. Consequently the multiplier on the tilted product and conditional probability is precisely $`e^{+\alpha Nu^2}`$. The global comparison gives three nonnegative terms, including $`\frac12\log(Nv_*/V)`$. The $`O(N^{-1})`$ budget implies $`u=O(N^{-1})`$ and the total squared phase displacement is $`O(N^{-1})`$, which also bounds the possible central level offsets by a fixed constant. These constants depend only on the chosen compact interval.
5. **Pairwise exchange.** A transposition of deviations with the original integer lifts restored is an actual root-lattice translation after permuting coordinates. Along that segment the bounded derivatives of the means and their uniform positive lower bound give an $`O(N^{-1/2})`$ common tilt. Removing the two coordinates changes the variance sum by $`O(1)`$, so for bounded centered values their conditional-to-independent mass ratio is $`1+O_M(N^{-1})`$. For arbitrary values the characteristic-function envelope for the residual sum gives a uniform upper bound on the ratio. Gaussian tails then give uniform convergence of the centered first and second moments and conditional difference variance $`2v_*+o(1)`$. The second derivative of $`\log P`$ along $`e_i-e_j`$ converges to $`-4\alpha+8\alpha^2v_*`$, which is uniformly positive. Strict convexity on a nonzero transposition segment contradicts the equal globally minimal endpoint values. Thus every minimum is a scalar phase.
6. **Neighboring central classes.** Substituting $`\sum_i k_i=N/2+j`$ in the midpoint energy gives the exact multiplier $`e^{+\alpha j^2/N}`$. The Fourier integrand for the offset is the real part of $`\psi(t)^Ne^{-ijt}`$. Half-integral $`j`$ in odd $`N`$ causes no problem for inversion of the integer-valued sum. $`J_0`$ is allowed as a comparison integral even when it is not a lattice probability; its central neighborhood has a positive leading term of order $`N^{-1/2}`$, with a uniformly negligible outer contribution. For bounded $`j,j_0`$ the ratio correction is $`(j^2-j_0^2)(\alpha-1/(2v_*))/N+o(N^{-1})`$. Strict midpoint curvature supplies the uniform positive coefficient and discreteness supplies a unit gap in $`j^2-j_0^2`$. Negation gives equality of the two central levels when applicable.

The rank threshold is existential and compact-interval dependent. The argument proves the claimed uniform minimum $`\Theta_\alpha(1/2)^N/\sqrt{2\pi N v_*(\alpha)}\,[1+O_I(N^{-1})]`$ and precisely which translates attain it.
