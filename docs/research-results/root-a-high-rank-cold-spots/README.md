# Gaussian cold spots of high-rank type-A root lattices

**There is a rank threshold above which the deep holes of $`A_n`$ are exactly its Gaussian minimizers for every positive scale.** In the standard realization by zero-sum integer vectors with $`N=n+1`$ coordinates, all coordinate residues of a minimizer equal $`k/N`$ modulo one, with $`k`$ nearest to $`N/2`$. This gives one class for even $`N`$ and two for odd $`N`$. [Small-scale theorem and combination of the two regimes](SMALL-SCALE.md) · [Positive half-line theorem](HALF-LINE.md)

There are also absolute scales $`0<\alpha_-\le\alpha_+<\infty`$ such that this classification holds in **every rank** for $`0<\alpha\le\alpha_-`$ and for $`\alpha\ge\alpha_+`$. The thresholds are existential in the normalization where the roots have squared length two. Only finitely many ranks at intermediate scales remain in the universality question of Bachoc, Moustrou, Vallentin and Zimmermann. The rank-one and rank-two all-scale cases are classical, and the FCC theorem covers rank three. The dual $`A_n^*`$ has a separate all-rank, all-parameter classification. [Source question, §1.6](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.08819v2#S1.SS6) · [FCC theorem](../../../results/betermin-fcc-bcc-cold-spots/README.md) · [Dual type A](../dual-a-universal-cold-spots/README.md) · [Open issue #967](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/967)

At every positive scale and in **every rank**, the scalar translates with common coordinate residue $`k/N`$ have strictly decreasing Gaussian mass as $`k`$ runs from zero to $`\lfloor N/2\rfloor`$. The deep classes minimize over all translates with at least $`N-1`$ mutually congruent coordinates. Each deep class also minimizes uniquely among displacements whose coordinate residues occur in opposite pairs about that class, and its Hessian is positive definite with an explicit rank-independent lower bound at fixed scale. Any remaining global competitor must lie outside these families. Three Python scripts use exact arithmetic and explicit tail bounds for finite examples. The proof is internally reviewed, with no Lean formalization or external referee report.
