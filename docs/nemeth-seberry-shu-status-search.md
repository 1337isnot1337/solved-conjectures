# NSS status and novelty checks

Checked 2026-11-20. This is a search record, not a guarantee that no unpublished or unindexed proof exists.

## Exact primary statement

- Nemeth, Seberry, Shu, original 1979 paper, title-page abstract and introduction: https://documents.uow.edu.au/~jennie/WEBPDF/071_1979.pdf . All binary circulants, prime order, number of distinct permanent values conjectured of order p.
- Cheon–Wanless 2005, Conjecture 27, pp329–330: https://users.monash.edu.au/~iwanless/papers/permsurveyLAA.pdf . Explicit O(p) statement, all row sums. Also explains Resta–Sburlati fixed-degree rival is conjectural.
- Resta–Sburlati 2003 DOI https://doi.org/10.1016/S0024-3795(03)00649-9 . Fixed-k prediction is not an established asymptotic and does not already resolve NSS.
- Eades–Praeger–Seberry 1983: https://documents.uow.edu.au/~jennie/WEBPDF/092_1983.pdf . Affine support symmetries and class-counting do not establish that all different classes have different permanents.

## Dependency versus proposed contribution

The uniform determinant estimate is not new. It is used from Divoux–Wang arXiv:2610.23755v1, Proposition 3.1, with their attribution to McCullagh2014. New candidate contribution: binary sinusoidal Fourier profiles with cardinality correction, determinant-factor separation uniformly over a linear number of degrees, and use of the prime congruence to obtain V(p)/p→∞. The cardinality/Fourier lemma, concentration inequality, Parseval identity and modular permanent observation are elementary ingredients, not separately asserted novel.

No explicit polynomial growth rate is claimed. No fixed sparse row-sum conjecture is claimed solved. The conclusion addresses the original all-row-sums O(p) conjecture.
