> Complete refutation of Conjecture 21 in the author preprint. An integral dimension-ten lattice has 2-designs on its first two nonempty shells but not its third. Even orthogonally indecomposable lattices can exhibit arbitrarily long initial runs of 2-design shells before a failure.
