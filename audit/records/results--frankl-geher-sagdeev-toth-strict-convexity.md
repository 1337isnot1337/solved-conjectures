# Frankl–Gehér–Sagdeev–Tóth’s strictly convex coloring conjecture

**Screened.** Result directory. [Pinned entry](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/blob/d676ae52308b71d65beac020fb2a1a591a9e6d34/results/frankl-geher-sagdeev-toth-strict-convexity/README.md).

## Recorded scope

The source describes the result as follows.

> Every isometric copy of a prescribed infinite configuration can receive every color infinitely often in a single countable coloring of a strictly convex normed plane, or of a polynomial normed space in any finite dimension.

## Audit finding

The authors’ 2023 preprint, arXiv:2308.08840, distinguishes positive results for polygonal norms from two-color anti-Ramsey results for the specified ℓ_p norms. The repository’s scope and the previously resolved special cases should be read with those norm hypotheses. The source PDF catalogues the authors and is accessible from arXiv despite the university-mirror denial.
