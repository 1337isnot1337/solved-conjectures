## Recorded scope

The source describes the result as follows.

> Complete proposed proof that the diagonal Mittag–Leffler value at minus one strictly increases throughout the unit parameter interval. A classical sine quantile, logarithmic contraction and reciprocal pairing give a bounded pointwise integral comparison. The endpoint and crossing-point consequences are not counted separately.
