## Recorded scope

The source describes the result as follows.

> Proves total nonnegativity of all four Fuss–Narayana triangles and strict total positivity of both squares for every integer parameter at least two.

## Audit finding

Wang and Yang’s paper proves the ordinary Narayana case; Conjecture 4.1 imposes m≥2 and all minors of four triangles and two squares. The older research checkpoint links the November 26 full repository proof. A Hoggatt sum counterexample to infinite log-convexity concerns a different object.
