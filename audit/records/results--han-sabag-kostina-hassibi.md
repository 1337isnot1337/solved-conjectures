## Recorded scope

The source describes the result as follows.

> Complete proposed disproof of the Lyapunov positivity conjecture. A rational feasible encoder has power strictly below the infimum over every encoder with one nonzero per column. All sparse assignments are exhausted, and the same failure holds on an open interval of channel gains. The full matrix assertion is refuted; no separate communication-code theorem is claimed.
