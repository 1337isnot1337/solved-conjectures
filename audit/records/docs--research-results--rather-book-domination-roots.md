## Recorded scope

The source describes the result as follows.

> Let $`B_n`$ consist of $`n`$ four-cycles with a common edge and otherwise disjoint vertices. A set dominates when its closed neighborhood is all of $`V(B_n)`$; $`D_n(x)`$ counts dominating sets by their sizes.

## Audit finding

Omega’s earlier n=1 refutation is exact for the literal Rather conjecture. The record already credits this and derives an all-order classification. Rather’s even-case existence intervals do not give the full number, simplicity and location of all roots.
