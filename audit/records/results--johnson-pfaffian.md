## Recorded scope

The source describes the result as follows.

> Complete proposed proof of Conjecture 7.1 for every positive nonincreasing log-convex distance kernel, every even order, and every perfect matching. A stronger theorem for products of circular interval factors follows from a complement scaling and adjacent-pair deletion. Johnson's endpoint theorem and kernel uncrossing reduction are credited.
