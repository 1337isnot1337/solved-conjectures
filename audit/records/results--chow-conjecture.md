## Recorded scope

The source describes the result as follows.

> Ordinary type-D Eulerian polynomials interlace in consecutive ranks, strictly from rank three onward. The weak assertion is a direct deduction from Savage–Visontai's published refinement theorem; the note supplies an additional strictness argument.
