## Recorded scope

The source describes the result as follows.

> A lattice-preserving transfer gives the same Ehrhart series for opposite polar preorder polytopes, with convex mutations and a finite numerator model.

## Audit finding

Conjecture 6.2 is polar duality for opposite preorders. The matched manuscripts prove 4.2, 4.4, 4.9, 6.3, 7.2 and 8.2 or treat arbor polytopes. Nonpolar duality and ordinal-sum factorization have different targets. The two withdrawn same-paper records do not establish this one.
