## Recorded scope

The source describes the result as follows.

> Complete proposed proof of journal Conjecture 74, for every natural unit interval graph on at least two vertices: the ascent-weighted chromatic quasisymmetric function after identifying the endpoints is the trace of the published forest-triple matrix. Directed edge multiplicities are retained. This trace identity is separate from Ellzey’s positivity and unimodality conjecture.
