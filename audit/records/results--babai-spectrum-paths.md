## Recorded scope

The source describes the result as follows.

> Johnson–Kaya–Matzke, arXiv Conjecture 2.1: for all `n>=2` and `1<=k<=n-1`, the chromatic numbers of the `k`-element distance graphs of the path `P_n` are exactly the integers from `ceil(n/(n-k))` to `k+1`. The missing lower bound comes from the bound `n-k` on independent sets.
