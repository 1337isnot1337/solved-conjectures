## Recorded scope

The source describes the result as follows.

> Work is coordinated in issue 19 and issue 20. The full `k ≥ 15` theorem is checked: in every finite `k`-uniform generating family with exactly three generators through each point and pairwise intersections of size at most one, every point belongs to at least `657/1000` of the distinct unions, including the empty union. The full high-girth bound is also checked for every `k ≥ 3`: every point has frequency at least `518208/1000000` when the incidence graph has no cycle shorter than sixteen. The cyclic span-four theorem remains incomplete. Li remains deferred until the other results are fully verified.
