**Screened.** Research note. [Pinned entry](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/blob/d676ae52308b71d65beac020fb2a1a591a9e6d34/docs/research-results/wang-yang-eulerian-square/README.md).

## Recorded scope

The source describes the result as follows.

> Wang and Yang’s Conjecture 4.3 asserts strict positivity of all minors of $[A(n+k,k)]_{n,k\ge1}$, where $A(m,j)$ counts permutations of $m$ letters with $j-1$ descents. Write $E(a,b)$ for the number of permutations with $a$ ascents and $b$ descents, so they have $a+b+1$ letters. The source matrix has entries $E(n,k-1)$. Reversing a permutation gives $E(a,b)=E(b,a)$. Below the symmetric extension includes $a=0$. Wang–Yang, §4 · Research issue
