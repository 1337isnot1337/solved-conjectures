## Audit finding

Earlier exact resolution. MathDB 374967 gives the same reciprocal identity for the transmuted M-triangle at every finite preorder. It uses an interior translate of a lattice cone and Stanley reciprocity. The full two-variable target and the size exponent agree. Conjecture 8.1 about a realization is a separate target.
