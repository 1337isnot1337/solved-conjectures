## Recorded scope

The source describes the result as follows.

> Oblique root-of-unity growth proves each sign occurs infinitely often on every index progression, for all coprime odd permitted-part steps at least three.

## Audit finding

The source Conjecture 4.3 is nonperiodicity for permitted parts congruent to 1 modulo 3. Principia and Omega refute 4.4 on factorial parts; they use different allowed-part sets and draw different conclusions.
