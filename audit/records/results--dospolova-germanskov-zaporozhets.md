## Recorded scope

The source describes the result as follows.

> Complete proposed disproof of the ultra-log-concave sequence realizability conjecture. Every positive factorial sequence c^k/k! satisfies the conjectured inequalities with equality but cannot be an intrinsic-volume sequence. A strict global-range/local-oscillation comparison and Vitale's published limit theorem provide the obstruction. Long's earlier results also permit a short alternative deduction and are explicitly credited. Three independent mathematical reviews passed.
