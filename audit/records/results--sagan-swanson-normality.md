## Recorded scope

The source describes the result as follows.

> Conjecture 7.8 is proved: all four q-Stirling coefficient distributions are asymptotically normal whenever $k/n$ tends to any value in $(0,1)$, using their actual means and variances.

## Audit finding

The same-source Principia and Omega papers prove bottom-heaviness of coefficient families. A shape inequality is distinct from an asymptotic local limit law. The record credits earlier interior-proportion CLT work and claims the stronger local limit.
