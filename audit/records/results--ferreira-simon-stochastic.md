## Recorded scope

The source describes the result as follows.

> Complete proposed proof of the usual stochastic decrease of the normalized inverse-stable family throughout the full real parameter interval. One Poisson coupling is almost surely strictly decreasing for every parameter pair. The endpoint limits and Mittag–Leffler bounds follow from the same argument.

## Audit finding

The Kilbas–Saigo upper and lower hyperbolic bounds concern a different function/inequality from the stochastic order or stable-infimum statement here.
