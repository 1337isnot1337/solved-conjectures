## Recorded scope

The source describes the result as follows.

> Graphs with every maximal independent set of size at least $`cn`$ can require $`n^{1-o(1)}`$ vertices to meet them all. Excluding any fixed induced biclique gives a sublinear transversal for the large maximal independent sets.
