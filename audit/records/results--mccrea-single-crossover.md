## Recorded scope

The source describes the result as follows.

> Complete proposed proof of the unique linear-density crossover for the two fixed-perimeter Franklin statistics, for every modulus at least three and every permitted density. Positive power-series factorizations give local coefficient asymptotics including the floor; opposite monotonicity of the two growth exponents gives existence and uniqueness. Independently reviewed internally.
