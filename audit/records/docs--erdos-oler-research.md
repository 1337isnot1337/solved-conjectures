## Recorded scope

The source describes the result as follows.

> Use the centre formulation in the research note. Let $n=(k+1)(k+2)/2-1$ centres at pairwise distance at least one fit in an equilateral triangle $\Delta$ of side $L\lt k$. The argument below gives necessary conditions for such a configuration. It uses the area normalisation $B(T)=4\operatorname{area}(T)/\sqrt3$, so a unit equilateral triangle has $B=1$.
