## Recorded scope

The source describes the result as follows.

> Proposed disproof for the standard Macdonald family of the majorization-to-Muirhead-semiring implication in Conjecture 5. A rational functional is nonnegative on an exhaustive generating list of 38 products and seven monomials, but strictly negative on an admissible normalized Macdonald difference. Exact eigenoperator and independent Hall-inner-product constructions identify the polynomials. Rectangular translates and a continuous parameter family give further counterexamples to the same assertion. Ordinary evaluation positivity is not refuted.
