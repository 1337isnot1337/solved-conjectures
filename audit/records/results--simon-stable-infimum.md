**Screened.** Ranked. Rank 64 on the original main table, marker ⬜. [Pinned entry](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/blob/d676ae52308b71d65beac020fb2a1a591a9e6d34/results/simon-stable-infimum/README.md).

## Recorded scope

The source describes the result as follows.

> Complete proposed proof that the mean-normalized fixed-time infima of spectrally negative stable processes increase in convex order throughout the full source parameter interval, including the Brownian endpoint. Increasing logarithmic Lévy measures yield an independent mean-one multiplier.

## Audit finding

The Kilbas–Saigo upper and lower hyperbolic bounds concern a different function/inequality from the stochastic order or stable-infimum statement here.
