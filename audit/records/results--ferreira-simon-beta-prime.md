## Recorded scope

The source describes the result as follows.

> Complete proposed analytic proof of the independent beta-prime self-convolution identity for every positive first shape and every second shape strictly between zero and one half. A quadratic hypergeometric transformation evaluates the mixing factor; a beta integral identifies the Mellin transforms on a nonempty strip. Two independent derivations passed.

## Audit finding

The repository target is Conjecture 1, a distributional identity for a beta-prime convolution. Principia 351688 addresses Conjecture 3 in the different squared Mills-ratio derivative paper. Neither the hypotheses nor the objects coincide.
