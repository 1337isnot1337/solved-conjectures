## Recorded scope

The source describes the result as follows.

> Let $b_p(n)$ be the least $b$ for which $p$ subsets of an $n$-point ground set, each of size at most $b$, cover all pairs. Let $L(p)$ denote the *all-to-all comparison data limit* of Hall, Kelly and Tian. Hall, Horsley and Stinson identify it as the least maximum block weight of a pair covering by $p$ blocks with total point weight one. They prove rational attainment and identify its reciprocal as the maximum fractional matching number of an intersecting hypergraph on $p$ vertices. Hall–Horsley–Stinson, §3, Lemma 3.1
