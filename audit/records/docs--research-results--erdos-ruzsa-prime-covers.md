## Recorded scope

The source describes the result as follows.

> **Theorem.** Fix $C>0$ and $0<\theta<1/2$, and put $y=N^\theta$. Choose one uniform residue independently for every prime $y<p\le N$. There are constants $a,b>0$, depending only on $C,\theta$, such that with probability at least ```math 1-\exp(-bN^\theta\log N), ``` at least $aN$ integers survive **every** choice of a set $P$ of small primes up to $y$ with $\sum_{p\in P}1/p\le C$ and arbitrary classes modulo these primes, in addition to **all** the larger random classes. The conclusion still holds with $aN/2$ survivors if any subset of large classes is discarded and any remaining large classes of total reciprocal cost at most $a/4$ are reassigned adversarially.
