The source describes the result as follows.

> The conjecture says that the chromatic symmetric function $X_T$ of any tree $T$ with a vertex of degree at least four has a negative elementary-basis coefficient. It appears as Conjecture 6.1 of Dahlberg, She, and van Willigenburg. The problem remains open in the sources checked.
