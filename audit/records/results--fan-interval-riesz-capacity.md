## Recorded scope

The source describes the result as follows.

> Proves the full negative-exponent conjecture and the nontrivial one-dimensional Clark–Laugesen range, with equality conditions.

## Audit finding

The November Riesz-ratios manuscript proves a two-point bound for the reverse ratio and a simplex result when the smaller positive exponent is at least two. The record concerns interval maximization and the nontrivial one-dimensional range. The Omega circle result treats three points on a circle in Conjecture 5.9, rather than the interval Conjecture 4.1.
