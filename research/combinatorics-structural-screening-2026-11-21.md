# Structural target screening

Research checkpoint, 21 November 2026. No result is counted in this note.

## Excluded: Makhija's conjecture

The four-pattern characterization of Schubert polynomials that are products of elementary symmetric polynomials in initial alphabets is explicitly proved by Xuanying Han, Shunli Ma, and Sophie C. C. Sun, *Factorizations by column blocks and Schubert polynomials as products of key polynomials*, arXiv:2610.36993v1, 27 October 2026, Theorem 1.2.

Primary full text was opened and read: <https://arxiv.org/html/2610.36993v1>. It states exactly the avoidance of 1423, 1432, 3142, and 4132, allows repeated alphabets, and attributes the original conjecture to Makhija. Excluded before mathematical work.

## Excluded: Lee's conjecture

Conjecture 29 of Jon Lee's arXiv:2610.07413v1 proposed a diameter gap of at most one for order and chain polytopes of a connected poset. Versions two and three withdraw it. Dave Goel's *Diameters of the order and chain polytopes of a connected poset*, Hexagon 2611.00523v1, 16 November 2026, gives connected counterexample families in both directions; its first version is dated 28 October and an expanded version is archived at Zenodo 23351398.

Primary author release opened: <https://hexagonmath.org/2611.00523v1>. It gives an unbounded gap in one direction and a 2-versus-4 family in the other. This is a full prior disproof, so the target is excluded. The latest Lee PDF was downloaded but no new proof work was undertaken.

## Paused: Xie–Xu's conjecture

The full additive-2-matroid conjecture remains a possible target, but bounded MILP discovery found no counterexample. Exact hypothesis separation, exclusions, and finite-search limits are in `xie-xu-checkpoint.md` and issue #206, comment 7059548142. The repository's Li coefficient-sign result concerns chromatic symmetric coefficients and connected partitions, and is distinct from this rank-count conjecture.

## Proposed full proof: Tamimi's conjecture

Conjecture 4.4.2 of the 2022 thesis has a complete proposed integral-presentation proof in `tamimi/PROOF.md`. Issue #208 reserves the full statement. The proof gives all invariant factors, not just their product, and treats the n=3 case separately. Root is independently checking the original graph definition and prior-art boundary. The mathematics is undergoing independent review. It remains research until those checks pass.
