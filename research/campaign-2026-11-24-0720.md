- **Lángi–Wang convex tiling question**: [issue #361](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/361) and [proof](langi-wang-convex-tiling/PROOF.md), answering final-paper Question 5.1 (arXiv Question 5.6) about both disk averages. ### Further exclusions for prior resolution

- **Downing–Hartung–Lucido–Williams Conjecture 1** (regular words): [Kuba–Panholzer (2012)](https://www.dmg.tuwien.ac.at/kuba/preprintStirpattern.pdf), Theorem 5 gives the bijection and Theorem 9 gives the exact binomial formula for `123` and `132` on `212`-avoiding words; Theorem 1 gives the overview. Complement then reversal maps `(123,121)` to `(123,212)`. Segovia’s 2026 enumeration gives the same formula for a distinct Tamari model. The competing independent derivation is not counted.
- **Steinerberger §41 Fourier-support question:** [Logan (1983)](https://doi.org/10.1137/0514024) bounds the positive zero count of the normalized even characteristic function by `floor(2T/pi)`. Symmetrizing the original probability density and rescaling shows its ordered frequencies satisfy `a_j >= j/(4r)`, which by summation implies the exact asked lower bound. Not counted.

### Third full named resolution

**Coulangeon–Lazzarini’s two-shell conjecture:** [published proof](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/tree/main/results/coulangeon-lazzarini-two-shells), [PR #369](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/pull/369), commit `37ea6cbb`, closed [issue #365](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/365).
