# Named conjecture research checkpoint

Started 2026-11-24 at 07:20 UTC. This checkpoint distinguishes complete proofs from narrower results and prior-art exclusions.

- [Tom–Vailaya’s weighted trace conjecture, journal Conjecture 74](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/tree/main/results/tom-vailaya-weighted-trace), [issue #338](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/338). ## Results after the first publication checkpoint

- **Alexandersson’s parking-function excedance conjecture: full counterexample**. Length 17 violates the Laguerre inequality at the rational input −1/5. Two independently derived integer counting recurrences agree. [Published proof and audit](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/tree/main/results/alexandersson-parking-excedance) are on main via [PR #360](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/pull/360), commit `af75f5c5`; [issue #106](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/106) is closed. No Lean or external review is claimed. This is the second full named resolution in the campaign. [Website deployment tracking](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/352).
- **Lángi–Wang convex tiling question**: [issue #361](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/361) and [proof](langi-wang-convex-tiling/PROOF.md), answering final-paper Question 5.1 (arXiv Question 5.6) about both disk averages. The convex Steinhaus minimax corollary is not the general nonconvex conjecture, and is not counted as a full named resolution.
- **Ellzey coefficientwise partial**: [source-ideal injection](ellzey-circular-arc/two-part-residue.md) proves positivity for two-part residual partitions with smaller part one or two across arbitrary independence number. No full coefficientwise or unimodality claim.

### Further exclusions for prior resolution

- **Downing–Hartung–Lucido–Williams Conjecture 1** (regular words): [Kuba–Panholzer (2012)](https://www.dmg.tuwien.ac.at/kuba/preprintStirpattern.pdf), Theorems 1 and 5–7, give the exact binomial formula for `132` and `321` on `212`-avoiding words. Value complement maps the other conjectured class `(123,121)` to `(321,212)`. The competing independent derivation is not counted.
- **Steinerberger §41 Fourier-support question:** [Logan (1983)](https://doi.org/10.1137/0514024) bounds the positive zero count of the normalized even characteristic function by `floor(2T/pi)`. Symmetrizing the original probability density and rescaling shows its ordered frequencies satisfy `a_j >= j/(4r)`, which by summation implies the exact asked lower bound. Not counted.
