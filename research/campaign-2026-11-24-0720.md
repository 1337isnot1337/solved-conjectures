# Named conjecture research checkpoint

Started 2026-11-24 at 07:20 UTC. This checkpoint distinguishes complete proofs from narrower results and prior-art exclusions.

## Reviewed full result

- [Tom–Vailaya’s weighted trace conjecture, journal Conjecture 74](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/tree/main/results/tom-vailaya-weighted-trace), [issue #338](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/338). Full directed multigraph trace identity for all natural unit interval inputs on at least two vertices. The written proof was independently reviewed internally. The separate enumeration is a check, not a dependency; no Lean or referee verification.

## Published open questions answered in narrower programs

- [Shift-graph questions](gyarfas-sumner/finite-shift-unions.md), [#320](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/320): Adenwalla et al. Problems 1.13 and 1.14, with a stronger finite-union theorem. The full Gyárfás–Sumner conjecture and unrestricted noninjective shift-colorable hosts remain open.
- [Strict cube covering gap](rado-sokolin/README.md), [#341](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/341): Dall’Ara–Dumitrescu Problem 7.2(6), all higher dimensions. Ajtai disproved the original universal equality in 1973; not counted as a new disproof of that named conjecture.

## Other research, not claimed solutions

- [Alspach–Liversidge](alspach-liversidge/README.md): zero-pattern compression, component gluing, and deletion constraints on minimum weak obstructions.
- [Lehmer](lehmer-totient/local-obstruction.md): simultaneous real and prime-adic approximations that fail true divisibility. [Denominator restriction](lehmer-totient/denominator-obstruction.md) and [prior-claim audit](lehmer-totient/prior-claim-audit.md).
- [Erdős pinned distances](erdos-982-pinned-distances/README.md): exact target for convex conic samples; [near-injective pins](erdos-982-pinned-distances/CURVATURE.md) for nonlinear polynomial parametrizations in any ambient dimension.
- [Gabow](gabow/README.md): complete exchange chains for generalized spikes in the stated size range.
- [Manickam–Miklós–Singhi](manickam-miklos-singhi/README.md): all cardinalities in the stated regime for one-positive cores with opposite pairs.
- [Ellzey](ellzey-circular-arc/positive-real-q.md): pointwise positivity for each real q; separate coefficientwise results in [restricted](ellzey-circular-arc/independence-two.md) [families](ellzey-circular-arc/cyclic-almost-clique-chains.md). They do not establish general coefficientwise positivity or unimodality.
- [Ryser](ryser/README.md): partwise degree range criterion. [Payne](payne-buckling/README.md): spectral comparison with cyclic symmetry.
- [Grimm](grimm/README.md): semiprime Hall matching and short-interval obstructions. [Erdős–Nešetřil](erdos-nesetril/README.md): fractional upper bound and a barrier to a local Reed method.
- [Erdős–Sierpiński](erdos-sierpinski/README.md): prime-factor complexity restricted to the stated odd-member families.
- [Bressoud–Goulden](bressoud-goulden/README.md): reciprocity assertion fails, but the unimodality conjecture is not resolved.
- [Erdős–Mollin–Walsh](erdos-mollin-walsh/README.md): necessary super-Wieferich orders for power centers; conditional fixed-base finiteness.
- [Clique-isodiametric](clique-isodiametric/PROOF.md): excluded because the disproof is an immediate consequence of a 2007 theorem. Separate measurable-set bounds and [quantitative planar strictness](clique-isodiametric/QUANTITATIVE.md) remain research.

- [De Loera–Haws–Köppe](de-loera-haws-koppe/README.md): strict h* log-concavity for rank-three sparse paving and its dual, using all-size polynomial certificates.
- [Erdős–Graham parallelograms](erdos-graham-parallelograms/README.md): all areas for periodic colorings and the stated polynomial-modular colorings. The unrestricted problem remains open.

None of the research items above is counted toward the full-conjecture total for this checkpoint. Priorities are qualified in the linked notes.
