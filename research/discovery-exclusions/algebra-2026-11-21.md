# Algebra discovery exclusions

These are prior results found during target selection, not new solutions from this campaign. Primary sources were read on 21 November 2026.

## Alexandersson–Shapiro's conjecture

[MathDB 374944](https://mathdb.com/p/374944/alexandersson-shapiro-square-graph-cone-conjecture-for-deriv) repeats the open square-graph cone statement from [Shapiro's arXiv v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.25743v1), dated 25 May 2026.

The current primary [v3 by Riener and Shapiro](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.25743v3), dated 21 October 2026, has a different title, *Discriminants of derivatives: square graphs and sums of squares*. Theorem 1.4 gives an exact separating functional for the terminal quartic discriminant whenever $`n\ge23`$. In particular, $`(n,k)=(23,19)`$ already refutes the universal square-graph conjecture. The source separately proves that these discriminants are sums of squares; that weaker property does not restore square-graph membership.

Decision: exclude the entire square-graph conjecture. Its status cannot be inferred from v1 or the catalogue's open label. The distinct general sum-of-squares and Hermite-factorization questions are not settled by the square-graph counterexample and were not investigated here.

## Makhija's conjecture

[MathDB 414178](https://mathdb.com/p/414178/makhija-s-conjecture-on-products-of-elementary-symmetric-pol) reports an unresolved four-pattern classification for Schubert polynomials that factor as elementary symmetric polynomials in initial alphabets.

[Han, Ma, and Sun, arXiv:2610.36993v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.36993v1), dated 27 October 2026, proves the exact classification in Theorem 1.2: the permitted permutations avoid $`1423,1432,3142,4132`$. The statement allows repeated initial alphabets and gives the unique factors from the nonempty Rothe columns. Thus it covers the full conjecture, including the repeated-alphabet convention.

Decision: exclude; no computation was started.

## Vontobel's averaged coefficientwise conjecture

[MathDB 321930](https://mathdb.com/p/321930/coefficientwise-bethe-permanent-lifting-conjecture) presents the averaged coefficientwise Bethe-permanent inequality as open and mentions a stronger inequality for each individual cover.

[Huang, Kashyap, and Vontobel, arXiv:2306.02280v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.02280v3), Lemma 2 and Theorem 3, prove the averaged coefficient inequality. Their discussion explicitly identifies this as the first, weaker assertion in Vontobel's Conjecture 52. The same discussion states that the Smarandache–Haenggi proof of the stronger assertion is incomplete.

Decision: exclude the averaged target as already proved. This note does not infer the status of the individual-cover assertion from the averaged theorem or from the incomplete earlier proof.
