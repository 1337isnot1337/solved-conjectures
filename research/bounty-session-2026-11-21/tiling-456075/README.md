# Square tiling by 45–60–75 triangles: excluded bounty lead

Checked 2026-11-21. New solutions: 0. Awards accepted: 0. Payments received: 0.

## Decision

Exclude the proposed sub-100 construction target. Exact constructions below that threshold were published years ago. No new construction search was started. The existing Gaussian research directory was not modified.

## Offer and exact scope

The [MathPuzzle archive](https://mathpuzzle.com/27Nov2011.html) offers a dollar reward of `100 − N` for a proper new tiling of a square by N triangles with angles 45°, 60°, and 75°. It asks for the smallest possible number. The item is under the archive's **20 April 2011** section; the URL's 27 November date is not the item's date.

The triangles must be similar; their sizes may differ. Congruence is not required. This reading is confirmed by the sponsor's subsequent discussion and accepted construction. The specified target is an exact square dissection, not an approximate diagram.

## Remaining question and limits

The exact minimum is a different question from producing any construction below 100. This screening did not prove 32 is minimal or establish that a smaller construction is impossible. It found no verified current offer specifically paying for an optimality proof. A new record search would also conflict with the user's original exclusion of numerical hillclimbs.

No independent coordinate replay was necessary to exclude this target: the sponsor's own exact verification and acknowledgement already predate this session. No sponsor contact, email, submission, or payment action was taken. The older reward formula cannot be treated as an unclaimed entitlement today.
