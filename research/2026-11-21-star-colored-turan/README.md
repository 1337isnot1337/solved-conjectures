# Chen–Liu–Qi–Yang's conjecture

Unresolved screening checkpoint, 21 November 2026. No complete result is claimed and this target contributes zero to the campaign tally.

## Target and structural route

The target is Conjecture 7.1 in [arXiv:2511.17000](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.17000), on the total edge count of a colored multigraph avoiding the specified star-colored clique. The complete source definition, including the requirement on every color class inside the forbidden clique, was read. The full conjecture ranges over all permitted orders and numbers of colors.

The source's bound with an additive term linear in the number of vertices suggests a blow-up argument. Blowing up every vertex preserves the forbidden-pattern condition and scales the edge total quadratically. This removes the linear error, but yields the fractional Turán bound. It does not recover the bounded rounding correction in the exact Turán number at every order. No argument closing that gap was found.

A small mixed-integer diagnostic produced no usable counterexample; a larger trial timed out. Neither establishes a positive case of the conjecture, and neither is counted as progress toward a general proof. No parameter-by-parameter program is being promoted as a resolution.

## Status

The structural route is incomplete. No full prior-resolution audit was completed after the route stalled, so the conjecture's current novelty status is not certified here. This note preserves the attempted route and its specific obstruction for later work.
