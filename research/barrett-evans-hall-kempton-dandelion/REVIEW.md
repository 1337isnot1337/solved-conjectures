# Independent review of the all-orders dandelion theorem

27 November 2026 UTC. The graph and its complement are simple on the same vertex set and have diameter exactly three. For every order admitting such a pair, the minimum sum of their algebraic connectivities is attained by the dandelion. All minimizers up to complementation arise by adding arbitrary edges within its ordinary pendant class. This is the entire domain of Barrett–Evans–Hall–Kempton’s Conjecture 2, arXiv:2201.04225v1, §4.1, p. 15. The smallest admissible order is four, with the four-vertex path. [Primary statement and Lemma 4.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2201.04225#page=15) · [Journal version](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.laa.2022.04.021)

## The anchored search

A distance-three pair in the graph is nonadjacent and has disjoint open neighborhoods. A distance-three pair in its complement forms a dominating edge in the graph. The two pairs cannot share a vertex. If an endpoint of the nonedge lay on that edge, domination would force the other endpoint of the nonedge to see the other end of the edge, producing a common neighbor. Consequently the two nonedge endpoints must meet the dominating edge in a perfect matching. In path order these four vertices induce precisely a path. Every other vertex can be adjacent to at most one endpoint and must see one or both interior vertices. These are the nine masks used in the search. Conversely every attachment assignment and choice of exterior edges gives a connected graph dominated by its middle edge and a connected complement dominated by the original endpoints. Both diameters are at most three, and their designated remote pairs certify equality. This equivalence does not rely on the optional Fiedler permutation-graph reduction. [Earlier structural proof](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/blob/e41d1279f655efd121e144777bd51b7047f03c4d/research/barrett-evans-hall-kempton-dandelion/ANCHORED-PERMUTATIONS.md)

Ordering exterior vertices by their masks loses only relabelings. Path reversal and complementation followed by path relabeling are actual symmetries of the objective. The complement path relabeling is 2,0,3,1 in the original zero-based indices, and its square is reversal. Thus selecting a minimum count vector retains an assignment from each orbit. At any partial board, transposing two exterior twins simultaneously preserves both sets of already assigned edges and all untouched edge positions. For a fixed outside vertex the listed twin swaps generate every permutation of its selected class, so only the number of new edges of the first color to that class matters. Recursing over all those counts covers every completion. The counts do not implicitly require a permutation pattern among the unassigned vertices.

At every node both assigned colors are already connected through the anchors. If a vertex has all but one of its possible edges assigned to one color, its remaining edge has the opposite color in each complete graph. This forces a leaf there. If both colors acquire leaves, use the analytical all-orders comparison. Otherwise a successful exact Schur certificate gives strict spectral sum above the dandelion already at the two partial Laplacians. Completing a graph only adds positive-semidefinite edge Laplacians to its assigned color, so the strict inequality holds throughout that subtree. An unresolved leaf or search limit returns failure. The arithmetic implementation has a separate independent review. The earlier graph6 enumeration, sampling rules and graph count are not needed by the new 11–20 verifier.

I independently reran all ten orders using the fixed integer-based branch ordering. The runs covered 510,034 symmetry-reduced roots from 2,037,970 root masks by multiplicity and visited 2,658,963 partial boards, leaving zero unclosed nodes. Order 20 alone covered 183,999 of 735,471 root multiplicity vectors after reduction and visited 1,391,125 boards. The branch order differs from the eigenvector-guided search and never enters the proof of an accepted spectral bound.

## Leaves, equality and the remaining orders

Let l be a leaf in the graph and h a leaf in the complement. They are distinct at every admissible order, since their degrees in the graph are 1 and n−2. If l and h are nonadjacent in the graph, then h misses only the leaf l. If adjacent in the graph, then in the complement l misses only h, which is a leaf there. Applying the [missing-leaf comparison](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/blob/1cfca7af629cca5c90e2caed4e0c63769a19dbc6/research/barrett-evans-hall-kempton-dandelion/MISSING-LEAF.md) in the indicated orientation gives the lower bound and equality classification. Every stated equality graph has both diameters three. For example an ordinary pendant vertex and the stem-tip leaf retain distance three in the graph; the almost-universal vertex and the intermediate stem vertex have distance three in the complement. The relevant vertex classes form an equitable Laplacian partition. On vectors supported and summing to zero on the ordinary class, the Laplacian is the identity plus that class’s internal Laplacian. Its eigenvalues therefore lie between one and n−2. The outer quotient roots of the original dandelion cubic lie strictly below one and above n−1, so its two extreme positive eigenvalues and the complementary objective are unchanged. The original authors already observed the pendant-edge invariance; the exclusion of all other families is the additional conclusion. [Source, immediately above Conjecture 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2201.04225#page=15)

The [lower endpoint](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/blob/8e7a8227ed084b5e48ff1ada1a07baa417d3cda8/research/barrett-evans-hall-kempton-dandelion/SMALL-REMAINDER.md) covers 4–10 for connected complementary pairs, including every diameter-three pair. The [finite interval certificate](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/blob/1ee0932d56279d63c70c3ed70fbffbea52067694/research/barrett-evans-hall-kempton-dandelion/FINITE-INTERVAL.md) covers every order 21–64 for connected complementary pairs. I reran this exact verifier and obtained 1,638 terminal boxes, 3,276 successful degree branches and 1,084 uses of the signed-extrema matching. The [uniform certificate](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/blob/305d415766af4f9aae0d77574a3b69a33fccd5f0/research/barrett-evans-hall-kempton-dandelion/INTERVAL-LEAF-THRESHOLD.md) treats n≥65 by conservatively substituting the threshold order in the estimates, with a single collection of 600 branch closures. Its rerun returned all 600. I also reran the missing-leaf arithmetic through 49. The written inequalities treat all larger orders in the missing-leaf case. These disjoint ranges combine with the ten new anchored finite searches.

## Prior results and the stronger problem

The 2021 Laplacian spread theorem of Einollahzadeh and Karkhaneei and Li–Ji’s 2026 reproof give the universal sum bound one. The 2022 authors give the dandelion polynomial and a bound on its objective. Their nearby weighted conjecture has a different aim. Searches on the author names and titles, equivalent spectral-spread maximization, complementary diameters, dandelions, and citing results did not locate an earlier proof of the exact diameter-three minimum. [2021 resolution](https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.02047) · [Li–Ji](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.03711) · [Current discovery claim](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/962)

The new exact search covers only pairs admitting the two anchors. A connected complementary pair without those anchors is outside its certificate. The original Conjecture 2 is a complete computer-assisted resolution. The later [connected-complement argument](CONNECTED-EXTENSION.md) supplies the additional finite certificate; its [independent review](CONNECTED-REVIEW.md) covers that stronger domain. No Lean verification or external refereeing is claimed.
