# Prior resolutions excluded from the campaign

Audit checkpoint: 21 November 2026. None of the targets below is counted as a new resolution. An independently rediscovered proof does not establish a new result.

## Sun's rising-binomial q-log-convexity conjecture

The target is Conjecture 1.1(i) in the current version of [arXiv:2505.02767](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.02767). [MathDB 368293](https://mathdb.com/p/368293/sun-s-q-log-convexity-conjecture-for-binomial-sums-and-the-p) records a counterexample attributed to Qiping Zhou on 24 July 2026: parameter five and index seven give a coefficient of minus 499140. The public treatment also supplies failures for every parameter at least four. This target was already disproved before the current search.

## Pandey's parity conjecture

The generalized-Petersen independence-polynomial parity statement has a public refutation in the [Demonstrandum artifact index](https://github.com/demonstrandum-research/artifacts/blob/main/RESULTS.md) and the [published refutation note](https://www.trureturing.org/research/pandey-parity-conjecture-refutation/). Small exact counterexamples and an isomorphism obstruction settle the unrestricted statement. The author's revised material acknowledges the refutation. Our independent observation of the same isomorphism obstruction is not counted.

## Ramane–Kitturmath's regular-graph energy conjecture

Conjecture 3.11 in the 2023 source [DOI 10.1016/j.exco.2023.100115](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.exco.2023.100115) rules out regular-graph energies equal to a positive even integer times the square root of two. A Galois-conjugation proof was independently found during this run. A subsequent search located the same argument in the [public resolution package](https://the-omega-institute.github.io/trureturing-mdbook/Problems/ramane-kitturmath-2023-regular-graph-energy-conjecture-3-11.html). The conjecture was therefore excluded.

## Zhang's Eulerian-pencil conjecture

Philip B. Zhang's Conjecture 3.2 in [arXiv:1408.4235](https://arxiv.org/abs/1408.4235) is settled by Shi-Mei Ma's *The Eulerian Combinations*, uploaded 22 August 2026. The [author-uploaded source](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/413485066_THE_EULERIAN_COMBINATIONS) explicitly identifies Zhang's conjecture. A different 2026 Motzkin result was initially considered during discovery; it is not the reason for this exclusion.

## Karp–Vishnyakova–Zhang's Hankel-product conjecture

This assertion was mistakenly reintroduced as new during the present campaign. [Repository issue94](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/94) already documented the prior implication from Thomas Simon, [arXiv:1207.6464](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1207.6464), printed page4 main theorem and page16 Remark(b). With parameters b=d=1/2, the product of two multiplicative totally positive kernels is not sign regular of order three. Logarithmic coordinates and a common shift of finite witnessing grids yield the exact positive-quadrant Hankel counterexample required by KVZ. The main definition has the negative exponential; the remark has a missing-minus typo. Root and analysis independently checked the theorem and substitution. The result package was removed and the campaign tally reduced from six to five.

A deeper search located a prior complete strict-log-concavity proof of the exact INTEGERS26A55 Conjecture1 in [xzwwwwww/math](https://github.com/xzwwwwww/math), under the Motzkin conjecture directory. The current run stopped this target immediately. [Issue179](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/179) records the exclusion. The initial English-language title searches did not reveal the Chinese-language package.
