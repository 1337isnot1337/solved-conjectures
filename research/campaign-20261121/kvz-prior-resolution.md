# Prior-resolution correction for two KVZ targets

Correction date: 21 November 2026. The two targets below had already been excluded on November 20. Their reintroduction in issues [164](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/164) and [169](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/169) was a priority error. This note supersedes the new-resolution claims for those targets and reduces the campaign checkpoint by two.

## Reciprocal Jackson q-gamma kernel

[Knowledge Sense, *Strict sign regularity of the reciprocal Jackson q-gamma kernel*](https://github.com/KnowledgeSense/math/blob/b95a52a54597e8b01c1a28486f2a9b29a3a54c58/preprints/Strict-Sign-Regularity-of-the-Reciprocal-Jackson-q-Gamma-Kernel-October-13-2026/build/source/strict-sign-regularity-of-the-reciprocal-jackson-q-gamma-kernel.tex), dated 13 October 2026, proves the full assertion for every real base strictly between zero and one, every determinant order and every pair of increasing positive real grids. The complete source is pinned at revision `b95a52a54597e8b01c1a28486f2a9b29a3a54c58`, whose recorded commit date is 14 October. The proof was reread and its full scope checked. Its positive coefficient expansion, Cauchy–Binet sums and explicit nonvanishing lower bound establish the exact strict signature.

The [earlier repository audit](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/blob/a354c349b80c70bb575ffc0230d17c3ced5ec7f7/results/kvz-reciprocal-qgamma/NOVELTY.md) already records this prior resolution. A different independent proof does not reopen the target.

## Hankel-product sign regularity

Thomas Simon, *Produit Beta-Gamma et régularité du signe*, [arXiv:1207.6464](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1207.6464), submitted 27 July 2012 and published in 2014, gives two totally positive multiplicative kernels whose pointwise product fails sign regularity. The theorem and the relevant remark on printed pages 4 and 16 were checked. The [pinned November 20 correction](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/blob/a354c349b80c70bb575ffc0230d17c3ced5ec7f7/research/kvz-hankel-product/PRIOR-SIMON-CORRECTION.md) gives the complete transfer to the exact positive-quadrant Hankel conjecture.

In that transfer, logarithmic coordinates change a ratio into an additive argument and reverse one grid. Both factors acquire the same all-order signature. A single translation moves both finite witnesses of opposing determinant signs into the positive quadrant while preserving their values. Thus the earlier theorem already negates the conjecture on its stated domain; the strictness of the factors can also be established. This is not an inaccessible or merely suggestive prior lead.
