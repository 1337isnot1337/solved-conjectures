# Pinned distances under a site-centered capacity of three

Erdős asked whether some site in every sufficiently large planar set determines more than a fixed positive proportion above one third of the set size in distinct distances if every circle centered at a site contains at most three other sites. Lines and circles with centers outside the set can contain arbitrarily many sites. The full question remains open. [Original statement, p. 168](https://users.renyi.hu/~p_erdos/1987-27.pdf#page=2) · [Target and prior-resolution search](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/969)

The [mixed-circle incidence bound](CIRCLE-CONCENTRATION.md) shows that a putative near-extremizer for even the averaged question has a vanishing fraction of its sites on every circle. The [existing line estimate](../erdos-654-arbitrary-collinearities/COLLINEAR-MASS.md) gives the same necessary condition for lines. The mixed-circle proof allows arbitrary off-circle sites, including arbitrarily many on other concentric circles. Codex derived the argument in the WadeDavis research session. It has an independent internal mathematical review and exact finite checks, with no Lean formalization or external referee report.
