# Cuong–Kien–Matsuoka–Truong’s determinantal numerical-semigroup conjecture

**Partial only.** No resolution or counterexample is asserted. The following reduces a family search to explicit factorization properties and gives an arithmetic-progression preserving semigroup construction.

## Exact source and where the open implication stands

The original statement appears in [*Defining ideals of some numerical semigroup rings with arithmetic pseudo-Frobenius numbers*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.14025), Conjecture 1.1. See also [Murai–Takahashi](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.18136v1#S1.Thmtheorem1) and [Kien](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.39218v1#S1.Thmconjecture1) (both September 2026, restating the conjecture). Let H be a numerical semigroup minimally generated by n≥3 elements. The open implication predicts that if its set of pseudo-Frobenius numbers is an arithmetic progression of length n−1, then the toric ideal I_H of its semigroup ring is generated by the minors of a cyclic matrix of monomial powers, of the form with top row X_i^{c_i} and bottom row X_{i+1}^{d_{i+1}}, cyclically indexed. The implication from such a determinantal presentation to the AP condition was known. The recent papers prove partial converses under uniqueness assumptions and give numerical criteria. No prior full proof or counterexample surfaced in the author/conjecture/title searches or in the repo’s issues searches for “pseudo-Frobenius”, “Matsuoka”, and “Kien”.

## A necessary as well as sufficient uniqueness condition

Suppose PF(H)={h+α,…,h+(n−1)α}, α>0. Kien's Theorem 1.2 proves that if h has only one factorization in the minimal generators, then the conjectured determinantal presentation exists.

Conversely, if the cyclic minors presentation exists, the displayed formula in Murai–Takahashi §1 gives, from its positive difference orientation, h=Σ_i(c_i−1)a_i. Such an orientation is always available: if the common bottom-minus-top degree difference is negative, interchange rows and reverse cyclic order; the difference is nonzero since PF(H) has at least two members. The monomial M=∏ X_i^{c_i−1} is divisible by neither term of any of the generating minors, because every term of every minor contains a top-row power X_i^{c_i}. Thus no polynomial in their ideal has a nonzero coefficient at M. A second factorization of h would give a different monomial M' with M−M'∈I_H, a contradiction. So a counterexample is exactly a semigroup of embedding dimension n with an AP PF set of length n−1 and a multiply factored lower anchor h. This observation does not assert that such a counterexample exists.

## Arithmetic-progression lift

Suppose H≠ℕ has positive generators and

    PF(H) = {h+jα : 1≤j≤t},

where t≥2, h≥0, and α>0. In fact h∈H and α∉H by the boundary argument in Kien Lemma 3.2. Define E={x∈H : x+α∈H}, a cofinite H-ideal not containing 0, and write μ(E) for the number of its minimal generators as an ideal over H. For any q≥2 coprime to α, put

    T = qH ∪ ⋃_{1≤j<q} (qE+jα).

Then

    PF(T) = {qh+jα : 1≤j≤q(t+1)−1},
    embdim(T) = embdim(H) + (q−1)μ(E).

Moreover, the factorizations of qh with respect to the minimal generators of T are exactly those of h in H, scaled by q.

**Closure and minimal generators.** Represent integers in the distinct residue classes as qx+jα with 0≤j<q, possibly with x negative. In a sum with no carry in j, closure follows from E+H⊆E and E⊆H. In a sum with a carry, both summands lie in shifted E classes. For x,y∈E, x+y+α∈E since x+y+α∈H and x+y+2α∈H. This handles carry even when the target residue is zero. Each residue class is cofinite because H and E are. Nonzero E elements and α are positive.

Copies q a_i of minimal generators of H stay minimal: a decomposition using shifted terms must carry, i.e. its residue indices sum to qL. If there are R shifted terms, 0≤L<R because their indices are <q. Replace L underlying E-coordinates x by x+α, leaving every coordinate a positive member of H. This would decompose a_i in H whenever shifted terms occur, since then R≥2. If only unshifted terms occur, use minimality in H. Similarly the other minimal generators are exactly q e+jα for e minimal in E and 1≤j<q. If a decomposition uses shifted terms, at least one unshifted E-coordinate survives after absorbing carries because L<R. In a genuine decomposition the remaining positive H-coordinate sum would contradict minimality of e in the H-ideal. Conversely every q e+jα with e nonminimal has a decomposition by subtracting an unshifted positive element of qH.

**Pseudo-Frobenius calculation.** In residue zero a missing qx is pseudo-Frobenius in T exactly when x is pseudo-Frobenius in H. Necessity tests q(H\{0}); sufficiency also uses x+E⊆E, since both e and e+α are positive H-elements.

For a missing qx+jα with 1≤j<q, testing positive qH requires x,x+α∈H∪PF(H). Since x∉E, at least one is in PF(H), so x=h+kα, 0≤k≤t. (For any integer gap, being addable to all positive H-elements is precisely the definition of PF, including when considering x potentially negative.) Conversely all these k satisfy the requirement: the endpoints h and h+(t+1)α belong to H, the latter lying beyond the Frobenius number. To add a shifted E-element, the target H-coordinate is x+e or x+e+α; if the residue is not zero we also need the next α-shift. This follows since x,x+α are in H∪PF(H), and e,e+α are positive H-elements. Combining the residue classes proves the claimed full PF progression.

**Anchor factorization.** If a factorization of qh used at least one shifted term, the same carry allocation leaves at least one E-coordinate e unshifted and gives h=e+s for s∈H. This would imply h+α∈H because e+α∈H, a contradiction. So all factorizations are unshifted and correspond to those of h.

In particular, an arithmetic-PF seed with a nonunique h would yield a counterexample to CKMT after the lift whenever

    embdim(H)+(q−1)μ(E)=q(t+1).

The formula is only a conditional construction, not evidence that a seed with this equality exists. An exhaustive generator search for embedding dimension four through max generator 120, and for embedding dimension five through max generator 70, found no direct CKMT counterexamples. These searches were exploratory, are not a certified negative classification, and are not the content of the transfer.

## Relation to duplication and gluing

The q=2 set shape is similar to [D’Anna–Strazzanti's numerical duplication](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00233-012-9451-x), with a special ideal and a shift α outside H rather than in H. The special closure property E+E+α⊆H supplies what is needed. Searches for arithmetic PF progressions plus duplication found related constructions for PF sets *containing* an AP, such as [Moscariello–Strazzanti](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.08260v1#S3), not this exact full-PF transfer. No priority claim.

Ordinary gluings of two nonsymmetric numerical semigroups cannot substitute for this AP lift. In a gluing d₁H₁+d₂H₂, where d₂∈H₁ and d₁∈H₂ are positive and coprime, the standard PF formula is the scaled sumset d₁PF(H₁)+d₂PF(H₂)+d₁d₂. If both component types exceed one, its least point and next-least point differ by α divisible by one scale, say d₁; a sum changing both coordinates could not be second-least. If the entire sumset were an AP of difference α, all sums would be congruent modulo d₁. Holding the first component fixed would force all members of PF(H₂) congruent modulo d₁, impossible since the positive difference of two such members cannot be a multiple of d₁∈H₂.
