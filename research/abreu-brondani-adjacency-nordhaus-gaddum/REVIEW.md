# Independent review of Conjecture 17

I reviewed the proof from the 2017 printed statement and the two cited structural sources. I had no role in developing the graph argument or its checker. The bound and the complete adjacency equality classification hold for every stated order. This is an independent internal written review.

The published Conjecture 17 on p. 133 asks for the specified bound on the two ordinary second adjacency eigenvalues, with equality exactly for the indicated equal four-block graph when four divides the order. Proposition 16 already verifies the attaining family. Xiong and Hou supply the threshold Seidel gap, and Haemers and Topcu's Theorem 3.2 gives switching equivalence to the clique-plus-isolates family or its complement from the stated two-outlier hypothesis. The source's connected convention for threshold graphs extends by complementation because the signed interval is symmetric. [Original conjecture](https://doiserbia.nb.rs/img/doi/1452-8630/2017/1452-86301701123A.pdf#page=11) · [Xiong and Hou](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.amc.2022.127177) · [Haemers and Topcu, Theorem 3.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2109.02522#page=4) · [Independent corroboration by Li, Wang and Brunetti](https://iris.unina.it/retrieve/d9e78694-73ef-40e6-a302-89d080b1eadc/Seidel_Dilworth_Brunetti_LAA.pdf#page=3)

I recomputed the coefficient signs with a zero first coordinate, two zero first coordinates, a zero second coordinate and equal ratios. With the specified ordering the latter coordinate controls every nonzero coefficient. The two independent Rayleigh inequalities give the spread reduction. At a sharp completion the same two unit tests attain both extremes. For balanced blocks their projector difference has no zero entries on any block, so the original signed matrix admits no alternate completion on a zero coefficient. The squared trace forces every middle modulus to be one and the two simple extremes to be opposite in this equality case. The quotient has determinants at plus and minus one equal to minus twice the opposite block size times one less than the other block size. Thus both quotient roots are indeed extreme. Switching, permutation and negation preserve the projector argument. A block of size zero or one reduces to the constant switching classes, which fall strictly below the proposed even-form bound for orders greater than two and below the parity-corrected Seidel maximum for orders greater than three.

Both doubled adjacency matrices are positive rank-one updates of the indicated signed matrices. At equality their inherited largest eigenvalues are simple. The adjugate at a simple root is a nonzero scalar times the eigenvector projector, so retaining that root forces the all-ones update vector to be orthogonal to its eigenvector. Applying this to both signs forces each block's switching signs to sum to zero separately. Reading the resulting signs gives clique endpoints, independent inner parts and the three complete joins of the stated path. Complementation produces an isomorphic equal-size construction. Conversely the preserved eigenvalue and the all-ones Rayleigh test occur on orthogonal invariant subspaces. The lower bound on the second test exceeds the preserved eigenvalue for block half-size at least two, since the squared comparison has positive difference twice the half-size times one less than the half-size. This proves sufficiency as well as necessity. The explicit order-two exception is strict.

I also checked the all-order Seidel-spread refinement. With three nonunit eigenvalues, some middle modulus is at least the square root of two, bounding the squared spread by twice the square of one less than the order. The near-balanced quotient squared spread is larger by one for odd orders, by two for even orders. For unequal block sizes the projector difference on block indicators is the quotient doubled and then shifted by the negative of its trace, all divided by the spread. Its two diagonal entries before division are the order minus two and its negative, and the off-diagonal entries are twice the geometric mean of the block sizes. This keeps all entries nonzero in vertex coordinates and transfers the extremal block classification back to the original matrix. Orders two and three are immediate constant switching classes up to overall sign.

The exact verifier on the original proof reproduced 1,098 labeled graphs through order five with twelve adjacency equalities, 1,022 threshold words through order ten, and all 256 eight-site switchings with 36 balanced equalities. I examined its real-root counts, rational enclosures, exact radical test and rank-one shifts. It counts signs after translating a real-rooted characteristic polynomial, where Descartes is exact with the roots at zero counted separately. These checks corroborate the written argument. Bounded source searches by author, conjecture, sharp bound, extremal family and signed matrix spread located the cited components and results for other matrices. I found no independent published resolution of the exact adjacency target in those searches.

It detects 46 threshold words attaining the parity-sensitive sharp spread. Jana, Mahato and Sivasubramanian independently recover Xiong and Hou's Theorem 3.3 as Theorem 3, subsequent to the 2023 corrigendum. The accessible corrigendum record does not give its detailed correction, so I do not attribute any particular proof change to it.
