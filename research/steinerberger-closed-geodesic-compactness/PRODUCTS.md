# Compact symmetric spaces, Besse factors, and products

The qualitative attainment theorem extends to every positive-dimensional finite Riemannian product of factors drawn from the following classes: compact connected globally symmetric spaces; compact connected Besse manifolds (all unit-speed geodesics periodic); and compact connected flat manifolds. It also extends to finite Riemannian quotients of such products. A bi-invariant compact connected Lie group is one of the symmetric cases.

The common sufficient property is a compact family of continuous maps Φ:T→M from a fixed flat torus T, in the uniform topology, such that:

1. Each Φ maps a nonconstant parametrized straight line to a nonconstant constant-speed geodesic. The speed can depend on the given line and does not need to be one.
2. Each closed geodesic is the image of a rational straight circle under some Φ, up to constant-speed parametrization or integer repetition.

On a compact connected symmetric M one fixes an isometric maximal flat torus and takes its translates by the isometry group. The isometry group is compact, and every geodesic is contained in a translate: at a base point any tangent vector lies in a maximal abelian subspace of the symmetric tangent summand, and the isotropy conjugates maximal such subspaces. The resulting maximal flat is an embedded flat torus. See [Gorodski's notes, §3.3](https://www.ime.usp.br/~gorodski/ps/symmetric-spaces.pdf) and the [closed-geodesic treatment for compact symmetric spaces](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/proceedings-of-the-royal-society-of-edinburgh-section-a-mathematics/article/on-the-string-topology-of-symmetric-spaces-of-higher-rank/9CC612193E5CA6D21691780195D9B23F). On a compact Besse M, [Wadsley's theorem](https://wrap.warwick.ac.uk/id/eprint/74139/) gives a common period L of the unit geodesic flow. Use maps from ℝ/ℤ given by Φ_u(t)=γ_u(Lt), with u ranging over the compact unit tangent bundle. Flat tori trivially have the property. Replace flat factors by their finite torus covers for now.

The properties survive products. Combine the factor tori and take coordinatewise product maps. The image of any straight line is a product geodesic with constant component speeds and at least one nonconstant component. For property 2, a period of a closed product geodesic is a common multiple of the positive components' primitive periods. A Besse parametrization by the common period may itself wrap around the component's primitive geodesic an integer number of times. Repeat the product geodesic by a common multiple of those finitely many wrapping numbers. It is then the image of a circle with integer factor directions, with zero coordinates for stationary components. In the stationary factors choose a map through the desired point, which can be done for all the stated families.

For the resulting torus family choose maps Φ_n and circle directions v_n whose maximal averages approach the global supremum. By compactness Φ_n→Φ uniformly along a subsequence. Hence f∘Φ_n→f∘Φ uniformly. Applying the Fourier lemma in [ATTAINMENT.md](ATTAINMENT.md) to the directions gives a fixed circle direction w whose best translate in the limit torus attains at least the limiting absolute average. Its image under Φ is a closed geodesic of M, and the normalization is unchanged by constant-speed parametrization. Finally descend from any finite Riemannian cover: after repeating finitely many times every downstairs periodic geodesic lifts periodically, and all upstairs periodic geodesics project periodically. This gives the flat factors and finite quotients as claimed.
