# Prior-resolution audit

Date: 21 November 2026. Reviewer: root agent.

**Decision: do not admit this package as a new resolution.** A reproduced abstract of a 2011 thesis states exactly the full group formula. The original thesis text has not been retrieved, so this note distinguishes the matched prior claim from direct inspection of its proof. The strong prior claim is sufficient to withhold novelty admission.

## Exact match under another graph name

The graph in Tamimi's Conjecture 4.4.2 is the edge corona of a cycle with a complete graph: attach a private complete graph on n−2 vertices to the two endpoints of each cycle edge. The edge itself is retained once. Indexed primary-source text of Tamimi's Definition 1.3.1 confirms that one of the two copies of the identified edge is deleted.

A Chinese-language search found Xianghua Tan (谭湘花), *几类冠图的临界群* (Critical groups of several corona graphs), a 2011 master's thesis at Hunan Normal University, supervised by Yaoping Hou. The public [reproduced thesis abstract and contents](https://www.xueweilunwen.com/doc/461874) state in item (5b):

```math
K(C_s\diamond K_t)\cong
(\mathbb Z/(t+2)\mathbb Z)^{st-2}
\oplus\mathbb Z/(2s(t+2))\mathbb Z.
```

The contents locate the edge-corona case in §3.2, pages 54–61. Setting s=r and t=n−2 gives the entire Tamimi formula, for all original parameters. This is not merely a matching spanning-tree count or one small clique size.

The author, period and corona-graph research are independently corroborated by the publisher's records for [Tan–Zeng's 2011 tree/star edge-corona paper](https://zkxb.jsu.edu.cn/CN/Y2011/V32/I6/11) and [Tan and coauthors' 2013 vertex-corona paper](https://www.scientific.net/AMM.357-360.2802). Those two journal articles concern different graph constructions and are not themselves being presented as proofs of Tamimi's whole assertion. The exact earlier formula above comes from the reproduced thesis abstract. Direct primary-text authentication of that thesis remains an explicit limit.

## Additional partial overlap

Alfaro, Medrano, Serrano, Téllez Téllez and Villagrán, [arXiv:2607.23506](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23506), July 2026, compute the sandpile group of a cycle with a private triangle attached to each edge. The algebra reviewer downloaded and read the primary PDF: Lemma 8 and planar duality give exactly the clique-size-three case for every cycle length. Their general block-graph theorem does not apply to the complete two-parameter family, which is not a block graph. This earlier special case must also be credited in any reuse of our independent proof.
