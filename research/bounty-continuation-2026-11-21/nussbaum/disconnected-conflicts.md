# Disconnected incompatibility graphs in Nussbaum’s period problem

**Theorem.** Let $`\mathcal F`$ be a family of distinct faces of the $`n`$-dimensional Boolean cube. Join two faces when their Boolean vertex sets are disjoint. If this graph is disconnected and its components all have the same size, then $`|\mathcal F|\le 2^n`$. In particular this proves the bound for every ternary formula orbit with a disconnected incompatibility graph, at any period. Unlike a bound on the union of the completion faces, this is a bound using the ambient dimension. [Sponsor’s statement](https://sites.math.rutgers.edu/~zeilberg/math640_10.html)

**Proof.** A coordinate is called conflicting if one face fixes it to zero and another fixes it to one. All faces fixing a given conflicting coordinate belong to the same graph component: those fixing it to zero and those fixing it to one give the two nonempty sides of a complete bipartite subgraph. Every face outside that component is free in the coordinate. Each nonconflicting coordinate allows at most two symbols across the whole family (a star and at most one definite value).

If all components are singletons, there is no conflicting coordinate and there are at most $`2^n`$ distinct words. Otherwise let $`t\ge2`$ be the number of components, each of size $`m\ge2`$. Let $`u`$ be the number of nonconflicting coordinates, and $`d_i`$ the number of conflicting coordinates belonging to component $`i`$. Then $`\sum_i d_i=n-u`$. Every face in component $`i`$ has a disjoint neighbor, so it fixes at least one of those $`d_i`$ coordinates. It is free in all conflicting coordinates belonging to other components. Consequently

```math
m\le (3^{d_i}-1)\,2^u .
```

Take $`d=\min_i d_i\ge1`$. For integer $`d\ge1`$, the inequality $`2(3^d-1)\le4^d`$ holds. For $`d=1,2`$ it is equality, and for $`d\ge3`$ already $`2\cdot3^d<4^d`$. Since $`2^{td}/t`$ is nondecreasing for integer $`t\ge2`$, it follows that $`t(3^d-1)\le2^{td}`$. Thus

```math
|\mathcal F|=tm\le 2^{u+td}\le 2^n .
```

On a ternary formula orbit the cyclic permutation preserves compatibility and acts transitively on the faces, so its incompatibility graph indeed has equicardinal components. Equality occurs already for the four-cycle of the map $`(x,y)\mapsto(y,1-x)`$ through $`(0,*)`$. Its incompatibility graph is a pair of disjoint edges.

For the remaining search one can check graph connectivity arithmetically. Label orbit positions by $`\mathbb Z/p\mathbb Z`$. If the nonempty set of differences from position zero to disjoint faces is $`D`$, the connected component of zero is the subgroup generated by $`D`$. Hence a counterexample would need $`\gcd(p,\{d:d\in D\})=1`$. It would also have to fail the [least-stabilizer condition](prime-power-periods.md) and the [two-prime reduction](semiprime-periods.md). These are necessary conditions only.
