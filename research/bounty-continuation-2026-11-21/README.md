In the sparse edge-critical reduction, all full cliques are now proved pairwise disjoint, and there are at least three. The source audit credits the earlier independence-three exclusion, pentagon obstruction, component restriction, boundary matching lemma, and general DP-coloring theorem. No priority or full-solution claim is made.

It assumes a valid total outcome function and does not establish that the actual game is determined. The all-starts prize remains open.

The coordinator reviewed the proof and replayed the exact checker. This blocks a proposed recurrence argument; it neither proves absorption nor refutes the prize conjecture.
