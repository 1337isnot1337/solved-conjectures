## Current paid targets

## Subsequent mathematical attempts

- [Erdős 389](erdos389/README.md), coordinated in [issue #232](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/232): two independent derivations reduce the remaining prime obstructions to exact cofactor carry inequalities. - [Erdős 617](erdos617/README.md) continues in the existing [issue #45](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/45). In the sparse edge-critical reduction, all full cliques are now proved pairwise disjoint, and there are at least three. If the reduced graph is connected, their number q satisfies q(q−1)<r; a minimum-degree vertex has no neighbors in their union. Connected and disconnected configurations still survive. An explicit recoloring permits subsequent cross-color arguments. The source audit credits the earlier independence-three exclusion, pentagon obstruction, component restriction, boundary matching lemma, and general DP-coloring theorem. No priority or full-solution claim is made.
- [The Althöfer game obstruction](althofer-periodicity/README.md), coordinated in [issue #224](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/224), proves that a complete winning/losing classification cannot eventually depend only on a residue class modulo any fixed positive integer. The standalone Lean proof was compiled and its axiom list checked, then replayed by the coordinator. It assumes a valid total outcome function and does not establish that the actual game is determined. The all-starts prize remains open.
- [The stochastic Althöfer phase calculation](althofer-stochastic-phase/README.md), coordinated in [issue #220](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/220), proves an exact phase-duration law and arithmetic-progression bound. An explicit infinite family shows that expected successor height, and time to the next good residue class, cannot be bounded by the current phase height alone. The coordinator reviewed the proof and replayed the exact checker. This blocks a proposed recurrence argument; it neither proves absorption nor refutes the prize conjecture.
- [The Althöfer incoming-weight calculation](althofer-incoming-measure/README.md), in the same issue, proves that no power weight multiplied by an arbitrary positive function of a fixed residue class and current two-adic phase height can satisfy the proposed incoming-flow inequality, even outside a finite set. A different construction is finite almost everywhere for three-adic Haar measure, but finiteness and summability on the required positive integers remain unproved. The independent review checks both the obstruction and that limitation.

The later [Althöfer integer-transfer note](althofer-incoming-measure/three-adic-integer-transfer.md) proves an exact martingale inequality: a sufficient condition for all-start absorption is subsequential decay of a stated weighted integer mass. It controls the part above the growing residue modulus and one-entry endpoint phases. The subsequent [recurrent-phase note](althofer-incoming-measure/recurrent-binary-phases.md) proves weighted Green summability for arbitrary repetitions of the two binary inverse branches, including initial entrance and holding time. Larger division exponents remain uncontrolled. Dense three-adic poles and a family of integer spikes rule out specific regularity shortcuts without disproving absorption. The route stops at the explicitly unproved mass estimate.

The later E617 checkpoint adds [near-clique blocks](erdos617/erdos617-near-clique-obstructions.md), an [exact weighted inequality](erdos617/weighted-two-level-akp.md), and a [separate extremal normalization](erdos617/erdos617-extremal-core-normalization.md).
