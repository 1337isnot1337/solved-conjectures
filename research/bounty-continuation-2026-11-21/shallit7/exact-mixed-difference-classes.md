# The exact mixed-difference classes of primitive words

Shallit’s £200 problem asks whether the language of primitive binary words is context-free. Kászonyi’s 2010 paper proposes an intermediate conjecture about exact classes of run vectors. Under Definition 10 as printed, the conjecture on page 328 is false. The same obstruction contradicts the lower-class assertion of its Theorem 6 when there are at least two distinct prime divisors. We prove this directly below. The whole language of primitive words, and the conjecture that its fixed-run slices are context-free, remain open here. [Sponsor’s problem 7](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~shallit/Talks/bc4.pdf) · [Kászonyi, pp. 316–318 and 325–328](https://publi.math.unideb.hu/paper/1506/download/10_5486_PMD_2010_4680.pdf)

## A counterexample on 105 runs

Encode a vector $`x\in\mathbb N_0^{105}`$ by the word $`ab^{x_0}\cdots ab^{x_{104}}`$. Indices are taken modulo 105. For $`p=3,5,7`$ write $`D_p=I-T_{105/p}`$, where $`(T_sx)_i=x_{i+s}`$. Definition 10, with $`\alpha=2`$, asks for nonzero differences in pairs of prime directions and zero difference in all three directions. Definition 4 also allows any nonzero multiple of each step modulo its prime order. Lemma 2 of the paper proves that the property of a difference being identically zero is independent of this choice.

Put $`f_p(i)=1[p\mid i]`$ and
```math
e=f_3f_5+f_3f_7+f_5f_7,
```
where multiplication is coordinatewise. The start of each pair difference at $`i=0`$ has value 1. Indeed the two difference operators annihilate every summand except the corresponding product, where both factors change from 1 to 0. The triple difference vanishes on every summand. Each single difference is nonzero, since a nonzero pair difference factors through it. A run word is a proper power precisely when some $`D_p`$ annihilates its vector: any repeated root contains a whole number of records, and the repetition count has a prime divisor of 105. Thus every $`e+c\mathbf1`$, $`c\ge0`$, belongs to the exact primitive class in question.

Let $`H=\ker(D_3D_5D_7)`$. No nonzero vector supported on one or two coordinates lies in $`H`$. In Chinese remainder coordinates the image of a standard basis vector has support a translate of a two-point subset in each of $`\mathbb Z/3,\mathbb Z/5,\mathbb Z/7`$. Each two-point subset has trivial translational stabilizer in a group of odd prime order. The 105 images therefore have distinct nonempty supports. Two such images cannot cancel in a nontrivial two-term linear combination.

Every period of a linear set contained in a linear space belongs to that space: subtract the base from the base plus that period. In a stratified linear set every period has support of size at most two. Consequently every stratified linear set contained in $`H`$ is a singleton. The exact class contains infinitely many distinct translates of $`e`$ and is contained in $`H`$, so it is not stratified semilinear. The coordinatewise letter-bounded encoding of any context-free run language is a finite-state transduction and must have a stratified semilinear exponent set by the Ginsburg–Spanier theorem. This disproves the printed intermediate-class conjecture already at $`\alpha=2`$. The [integer checker](check_mixed_strata.py) verifies all 105 columns and the differences for the explicit vector.

## All numbers of prime directions

Let $`n>1`$, let $`P`$ be its distinct prime divisors, and let $`k=|P|`$. Set $`D=\prod_{p\in P}(I-T_{n/p})`$. On each coset of the subgroup generated by the steps, these steps give independent cyclic coordinates of orders $`p`$. The support of a column of $`D`$ is a translate of a product of two-point sets in those coordinates. Columns from different cosets have disjoint support.

If $`n`$ is odd, all columns have distinct supports as before. Every context-free language of $`n`$-run words whose vectors lie in $`\ker D`$ is finite. If $`n`$ is even, the only nontrivial stabilizer of a column's support is the shift $`n/2`$. This shift negates the column, because the factor for 2 subtracts its two antipodal coordinates. Hence a nonzero vector of support at most two in the kernel is a scalar multiple of $`\mathbf e_i+\mathbf e_{i+n/2}`$. Chords for two different antipodal pairs cross. Stratification therefore allows periods in at most one such direction in any single linear set. A context-free run language in the kernel has its exponents in a finite union of affine lines, each varying only an antipodal pair.

For $`k\ge2`$ and any $`1\le\alpha<k`$ this excludes stratified semilinearity of the entire exact lower class. On a fixed coset use a prime coordinate $`u_p`$ increasing by one under $`T_{n/p}`$ and put $`g_p=1[u_p=0]`$. Define
```math
e=\sum_{\substack{S\subseteq P\\ |S|=\alpha}}\ \prod_{p\in S}g_p
```
on that coset and set it to zero elsewhere. At the coordinate origin every difference of order $`\alpha`$ has value 1, all differences of higher order vanish, and every prime direction is active. As above, $`e+c\mathbf1`$ is primitive and stays in the same exact class for every $`c`$. In the even case $`n>2`$ and this affine line is parallel to none of the allowed antipodal-pair lines. A finite union of those lines can contain only finitely many of its points. Taking $`\alpha=1`$ gives the contradiction to the exact assertion about $`E_1\cap E(Q_n)`$ in Theorem 6. This argument does not affect its assertion about the top class $`E_k`$.

## Consequence for the prize problem

A proof that every vector in a class has a context-free certificate of *primitivity* can admit vectors of higher mixed-difference order. Such a covering need not describe the exact class. Page 327 of the paper exhibits conditions under which a vector lies in a stratified set of primitive vectors. The lower-class counterexample concerns the stronger equality in the printed statement. The constructions of certificates can still be used when studying the union of classes.

There is also a direct obstruction to extracting this counterexample from the full primitive language by intersecting with a regular language. Suppose a regular language contains infinitely many $`n`$-run words from $`\ker D`$. Some accepted vector has one coordinate long enough that a positive power of the unary transition can be pumped inside that run. This gives accepted vectors $`x+jh\mathbf e_i`$ for every $`j\ge1`$ and some $`h>0`$. Their full differences are nonzero since $`D\mathbf e_i\ne0`$. All these words are primitive, because any prime repetition would kill its own difference factor. Thus any such regular filter also admits infinitely many primitive words outside the kernel. Ordinary context-freeness of all primitive words is not contradicted.
