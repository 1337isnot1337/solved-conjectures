# Gatzweiler–Krattenthaler conjecture

## Scope of this continuation

The [earlier CoPEER counterexample](https://github.com/qqliu/CoPEER/blob/105523fdaf018b62eaf2883347724dfe79935611/mathematics/discoveries/entries/number-theory/fake-gaussian-positivity-counterexample.md) refutes the arbitrary-exponent Conjecture 4. This continuation concerns the separate **symmetric** Conjecture 3 in [the original paper](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11139-025-01285-2): for any positive integers $m,n$ and symmetric nonnegative integer exponents $a_i=a_{n+1-i}$, polynomiality of
```math
\prod_{i=1}^n \left(\frac{1-q^{m+i}}{1-q^i}\right)^{a_i}
```

is conjectured to imply nonnegative coefficients. This problem remains unresolved here. It is also distinct from Stanton's stronger all-shifts polynomiality hypothesis.

## Why the first construction does not directly symmetrize

Let $j$ be the smallest index with a nonzero exponent. A particularly simple negative-coefficient construction would make $t=m+j$ absent from the additive semigroup generated by the support, while imposing $3j>t$. That last condition reduces semigroup nonmembership at $t$ to excluding single generators and pairs.

For a symmetric support, $2j\le n+1$. If also $m\ge n$, the condition $3j>m+j$ gives
```math
n\le m<2j\le n+1.
```

Therefore $m=n=2j-1$, and symmetry leaves only the single support index $j$. The quotient is then a positive power of $(1-q^{3j-1})/(1-q^j)$. Polynomiality requires $j$ to divide $3j-1$, hence $j=1$. At $j=1$, the target exponent is in the support semigroup. Thus the exact short-gap mechanism used to find Conjecture 4's counterexample cannot produce a symmetric counterexample with $m\ge n$.

This is a restriction on one construction method, not a positivity theorem.

## Restricted small-shift probe

[symmetric_probe.py](symmetric_probe.py) imposes symmetry, exact cyclotomic nonnegativity constraints, a smallest support index $j$, exponent bounds of 64, and the exclusion of generators/pairs summing to $m+j$. Every candidate returned by the mixed-integer solver would be rechecked using integer cyclotomic valuations and an exact semigroup computation.

On 20 November 2026, the command

```bash
python3 research/gatzweiler-krattenthaler/symmetric_probe.py 50
```

made 11,024 solver calls with $3\le n\le50$, $1\le m<n$, and the applicable $j$ range. It returned no witness. The per-call time limit is 0.1 seconds. Infeasibility is not accompanied by independently checkable certificates, and unsuccessful/time-limited calls are not distinguished in this exploratory driver. This outcome proves nothing about Conjecture 3, even in the searched finite range. It is recorded to prevent repeating the same restricted search.

Dependencies: NumPy and SciPy. Local elapsed time was approximately 11.5 seconds. No Lean work.
