# Lean verification of the rank-five Jack witness

This note covers the Lean formalization of the concrete counterexample to Chen–Khare–Sahi Conjecture 4, equation (4.5), tracked in [#965](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/965). It covers the written witness with five variables, source parameter $`\sigma=1`$, target parameter $`\tau=3/2`$ and the partitions $`(3,2)\succeq(3,1,1)`$. [Written proof](PROOF.md)

**Scope.** Lean proves the five-variable witness. The written extension to every rank $`n\ge5`$ and every $`1\lt\tau\lt2`$ is **not** formalized.

## Main theorem

**Lean excerpt**

```lean
theorem conjecture4_counterexample :
    Dominates ![3, 2, 0, 0, 0] ![3, 1, 1, 0, 0] ∧
      (∃! F : Index → Index → ℝ, IsJackFamily F) ∧
      ∀ F : Index → Index → ℝ, IsJackFamily F → ¬ SchurSemiring (jackTarget F)
```

[Source](../../../lean/Conjectures/ChenKhareSahiJackTheorem.lean)

The objects in the statement are defined as follows.

- `SchurSemiring` is the full source semiring at $`\sigma=1`$, with Schur polynomials as generators (see *Remaining scope*). It is generated by the normalized Schur polynomials $`s_\lambda(x)/s_\lambda(1^5)`$ for every partition with at most five parts, of any size. It also contains every normalized difference $`S_\lambda-S_\mu`$ with $`\lambda\succeq\mu`$, and it is closed under sums, products and nonnegative real scalars. Dominance (`Dominates`) compares all prefix sums of partitions of equal size. Schur polynomials are five-variable Jacobi–Trudi determinants $`\det(h_{\lambda_i-i+j})`$, with $`h_k`$ the literal complete homogeneous symmetric polynomial.
- `IsJackFamily` is Stanley's characterization of the monic Jack polynomials $`P_\lambda(x;\alpha)`$ in degree five at $`\alpha=1/\tau=2/3`$. Each $`P_\lambda`$ is monic and dominance-triangular in the monomial basis, and the family is pairwise orthogonal for $`\langle p_\lambda,p_\mu\rangle=\delta_{\lambda\mu}z_\lambda\alpha^{\ell(\lambda)}`$. Monomial symmetric polynomials are literal orbit sums over distinct exponent rearrangements. The power sums are literal products. Lean checks that the coordinates used in the scalar product are the power-sum coefficients of the same five-variable polynomial functions (`jackPoly_power`).
- `jackTarget F` is $`P_{(3,2)}/P_{(3,2)}(1^5)-P_{(3,1,1)}/P_{(3,1,1)}(1^5)`$ (`jparts_witness` checks the indices).

The theorem includes existence and uniqueness of the Jack family (`family_exists_unique`), so the negative statement is not vacuous.

## Proof structure

1. **Homogeneous extraction.** Every semiring element is a finite sum of homogeneous terms (`SchurSemiring.expand`). The target is homogeneous of degree five. Comparing coefficients of the polynomial in $`t`$ along each ray $`t\cdot x`$ (`eq_degreePart`) shows that the target equals the degree-five part. That part is a homogeneous element of degree five (`degreePart_hom`).
2. **Finite value cones.** Record each function by its values at $`(1,1,1,0,0)`$, $`(2,1,0,0,0)`$ and $`(1,1,1,1,0)`$. For each $`d\le5`$ the list `tab d` contains the value vectors of all degree-$`d`$ generator products. Lean checks the following:
   - Every partition with at most five parts and size at most five is among the 19 tabulated shapes (`partition_mem`).
   - Every normalized Schur polynomial and every dominance difference of size $`d`$ has its vector in `tab d` (`schurRaw_gen`, `schurRaw_diff`).
   - The lists are closed under multiplication (`tab_mul_closed`).

   Hence every homogeneous degree-five semiring element has its value vector in the nonnegative cone of `tab 5` (`HomSchur.cone`). Exact Schur values come from Jacobi–Trudi determinants. These are computed by a checked Laplace expansion (`lap_eq_det`) from tabulated values $`h_0,\dots,h_9`$ at the four points.
3. **Separation.** The functional $`-45f(1,1,1,0,0)+10f(2,1,0,0,0)+18f(1,1,1,1,0)`$ is nonnegative on `tab 5` (`tab5_sep`). It equals $`-3/4522`$ on the normalized Jack difference (`jackTarget_sep`).

This certificate replaces the written proof's leading-monomial and column-coefficient arguments. It does not depend on them.

## Source fidelity

The Jack side follows the source definition literally. In [§2.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.19649v2#S2.SS3), Chen–Khare–Sahi set $`\alpha=1/\tau`$. Equation (2.8) uses the inner product $`\langle p_\lambda,p_\mu\rangle_\tau=\delta_{\lambda\mu}z_\lambda\tau^{-\ell(\lambda)}`$. Equations (2.9)–(2.10) define $`P_\lambda(\tau)`$ as the unique pairwise orthogonal family with $`P_\lambda=\sum_{\lambda\succeq\mu}K_{\lambda\mu}m_\mu`$ and $`K_{\lambda\lambda}=1`$. At $`\tau=3/2`$ this is `IsJackFamily` with weight $`z_\lambda(2/3)^{\ell(\lambda)}`$. The source semiring in §4.2 consists of $`\mathbb R_{\ge0}`$-linear combinations of products of normalized $`P_\lambda(x;\sigma)`$ and normalized differences with $`\lambda\succeq\mu`$. `SchurSemiring` is this semiring with the generators described below.

## Remaining scope

- **Source generators.** Lean uses the five-variable Jacobi–Trudi Schur polynomials as generators. The source's generators are the Jack polynomials at $`\sigma=1`$. These are the Schur polynomials ([§1.2.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.19649v2#S1.SS2.SSS1)), and Jacobi–Trudi expresses them as determinants. Both identifications are classical (Macdonald, *Symmetric Functions and Hall Polynomials*, I.(3.4) and VI.10), but neither is formalized here.
- **Parameter family.** The written extension to every $`n\ge5`$ and every $`1\lt\tau\lt2`$ remains unformalized.
