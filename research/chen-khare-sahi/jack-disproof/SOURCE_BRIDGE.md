# The rank-five Jack counterexample in the source semiring

The counterexample has five variables, source parameter 1, target parameter 3/2, and partitions (3,2) and (3,1,1). It disproves Conjecture 4 in the source's equation (4.5). The extension to every rank at least five and every target parameter strictly between one and two has a written proof. ## From the defining equations to the separating functional

In each degree through five, all partitions fit in five variables. The formal source family has monomial and power-sum coefficient matrices for the same actual polynomial functions. The monomial expansion is monic and dominance-triangular. The power-sum coordinates are mutually orthogonal for the diagonal weights specified in equation (2.8) at parameter 1. The functions are defined by distinct permutations of the monomial exponents and literal products of power sums. [Source definition, equations (2.8)–(2.10)](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.19649v2#S2.SS3) · [Lean definitions](../../../lean/Conjectures/ChenKhareSahiJackSourceFamily.lean).

In degrees zero through five there are respectively 1, 1, 2, 3, 5 and 7 partitions. The associated exponent vectors give interpolation nodes. Lean checks rational right inverses to the power-sum evaluation matrices at these nodes. The equal polynomial evaluations identify the change of basis. An exact factorization of the Hall Gram matrix changes the unknown monomial coefficient matrix into a unit lower triangular matrix with orthogonal rows. Such a matrix is the identity by induction on its row index. This determines every monomial coefficient. Lean also checks that each resulting power-sum expansion equals its monomial expansion as a polynomial function at every real five-tuple and constructs an explicit completion of the low-degree family. [Matrix argument](../../../lean/Conjectures/ChenKhareSahiJackSource.lean) · [Rational certificates](../../../lean/Conjectures/ChenKhareSahiJackSourceData.lean) · [Polynomial identities](../../../lean/Conjectures/ChenKhareSahiJackSourceBasis.lean) · [Existence](../../../lean/Conjectures/ChenKhareSahiJackSourceWitness.lean).

At each of the three separating points and at the all-ones normalization point, these source evaluations reproduce the exact Jacobi–Trudi table already checked in the contributor’s formalization. Homogeneous extraction along rays keeps every term of total degree five, including arbitrary products and normalized dominance differences. All generators of degree above five may be replaced by arbitrary homogeneous functions in their respective degrees. Their coefficients at degree five vanish. The three-point functional is nonnegative for every remaining source term and takes the value $`-3/4522`$ on the target.
