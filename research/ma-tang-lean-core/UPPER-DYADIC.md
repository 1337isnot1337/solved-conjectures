# Ma–Tang’s random subset-sum threshold conjecture: upper-bound bookkeeping

**Checked partial formalization.** [The integer recurrence](../../lean/Conjectures/MaTangCoreUpperDyadic.lean) turns the initial missing-mass estimate and the exact shift-square identity into the per-prefix failure bounds. It sums those bounds to an iid failure fraction at most 5/16 and verifies that the last good prefix leaves zero missing elements. The proposed sample size also meets the collision budget for every alphabet of size at least 65536.

[The actual ordered-distinct transfer](../../lean/Conjectures/MaTangCoreUpperDistinct.lean) counts collisions on any duplicate-free finite alphabet and proves the seven-eighths retained fraction. Given an iid failure count at most 5/16, it proves distinct failure at most 5/14 and strictly more than half successful ordered distinct words.

This formalizes parts of the finite upper-bound route for [Ma–Tang Problem 4.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.05768v2#S4), using the smoothing argument of [Erdős–Rényi, Theorem 2](https://www.renyi.hu/~p_erdos/1965-15.pdf). The literal group second moment and shift-square identity must still supply the inputs. The unordered general-group endpoint, threshold bridge and real-log limsup are not claimed. There is no new conjecture, status increase or change of attribution.

[The word-prefix bridge](../../lean/Conjectures/MaTangCoreUpperPrefixes.lean) now proves the actual sample decomposition by separating inherited and new failures. Its endpoints `iid_missing_le` and `distinct_missing_success` are for the existing explicit ordered-word enumerator. They require only the initial missing-mass estimate and successor shift-sum identity for the chosen missing statistic. No assumption about counts of events over abstract levels is needed.
