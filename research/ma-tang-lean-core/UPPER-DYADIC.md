# Ma–Tang’s random subset-sum threshold conjecture: upper-bound bookkeeping

**Checked partial formalization.** [The integer recurrence](../../lean/Conjectures/MaTangCoreUpperDyadic.lean) turns the initial missing-mass estimate and the exact shift-square identity into the per-prefix failure bounds. It sums those bounds to an iid failure fraction at most 5/16 and verifies that the last good prefix leaves zero missing elements. The proposed sample size also meets the collision budget for every alphabet of size at least 65536.

[The actual ordered-distinct transfer](../../lean/Conjectures/MaTangCoreUpperDistinct.lean) counts collisions on any duplicate-free finite alphabet and proves the seven-eighths retained fraction. Given an iid failure count at most 5/16, it proves distinct failure at most 5/14 and strictly more than half successful ordered distinct words.

This formalizes parts of the finite upper-bound route for [Ma–Tang Problem 4.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.05768v2#S4), using the smoothing argument of [Erdős–Rényi, Theorem 2](https://www.renyi.hu/~p_erdos/1965-15.pdf). The literal group initial missing-mass estimate remains an input. The unordered general-group endpoint, threshold bridge and real-log limsup are not claimed. There is no new conjecture, status increase or change of attribution.

[The word-prefix bridge](../../lean/Conjectures/MaTangCoreUpperPrefixes.lean) now proves the actual sample decomposition by separating inherited and new failures. Its endpoints `iid_missing_le` and `distinct_missing_success` are for the existing explicit ordered-word enumerator. For an abstract missing statistic they require the initial missing-mass estimate and successor shift-sum identity. No assumption about counts of events over abstract levels is needed.

[The subset-sum translation bridge](../../lean/Conjectures/MaTangCoreUpperShift.lean) discharges the shift identity for the literal subset-value list with commutative addition and translation inverses. It counts ordered pairs of missing points, proves that zero missing points is equivalent to full coverage, and leaves only the initial missing-mass bound as an explicit input to `conditioned_cover_from_initial`.
