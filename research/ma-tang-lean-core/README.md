# Ma–Tang’s random subset-sum threshold conjecture

**Incomplete formalization.** Checked deterministic and finite counting components only. This is a work-in-progress checkpoint on research; no full result is registered or counted.

The existing [main result](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/tree/main/results/ma-tang-random-subset-sums), [recorded proof](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/blob/main/results/ma-tang-random-subset-sums/PROOF.md), and [discovery issue #490](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/490) resolve Ma and Tang's Problem 4.1 of [arXiv:2602.05768v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.05768v2#S4). This formalization follows equations (4)–(5) of that proof, credits Jie Ma and Quanyu Tang as proposers and the written proof's existing authors, and claims no new conjecture resolution or exclusive priority.

## Verified scope

- [MaTangCoreQuotient](../../lean/Conjectures/MaTangCoreQuotient.lean) proves every binary subspace is the kernel of an explicitly constructed surjective matrix with the correct row sizes. For each injective encoded generator with (k) columns and (r) independent coordinates, `quotient_of_good_generator` constructs a surjective ((k-r))-row matrix whose kernel is exactly that generator's image. The construction proceeds by induction on the ambient dimension. It establishes existence on both sides of the kernel coupling, not yet the uniform multiplicity law. [Nine-module replay](verify_quotient.py) and [log](verification/quotient-reproduction.log) passed strict compilation, transitive axiom checks of 234 source-named declarations and stock kernel replay.

- [MaTangCoreGenerator](../../lean/Conjectures/MaTangCoreGenerator.lean) proves the exact equivalence of the nonzero-null-vector event and noninjectivity and its uniform independent-column count bound: failure probability at most (2^{-d}) for (k=d+r), (r>0), in exact integer-count form. [MaTangCoreDecay](../../lean/Conjectures/MaTangCoreDecay.lean) proves the explicit sequence (m=2^r, q=4r+16, k=2mq+1, d=k-r) has singleton-occupancy fraction at most (1/(16m)), by integer powers and a block estimate. For (r=2^j), the checked dimension squeeze is (2^r\le d\le 2^{r+j+6}), and (C(j+6)\le 2^j) for (j\ge C+6). This is an exact discrete form of the negligible logarithmic loss. The choice of sequence differs from the source's floor formula but retains the sharp second-order coefficient; it does not yet prove the limiting real-log statement. [Eight-module replay](verify_decay.py) and [log](verification/decay-reproduction.log) passed strict Lean 4.35.0 compilation, transitive axiom checks of all 217 source-named declarations and stock kernel replay.

- [MaTangCoreSubspace](../../lean/Conjectures/MaTangCoreSubspace.lean) and [MaTangCoreRank](../../lean/Conjectures/MaTangCoreRank.lean): construct a bijective encoded-column parametrization of every binary subspace by induction on its first coordinate. For a surjective binary matrix with k input coordinates and d rows, count its duplicate-free fibers to prove exactly r=k−d; prove that every covering mixed binary/ternary matrix sample has an actual encoded kernel of that dimension and a singleton occupied nonzero type. Endpoints: `exists_encoding`, `encoding_rank_nullity`, and `mixed_coverage_actual_kernel`. These results no longer assume the existence or dimension of a kernel basis. The distribution of sampled kernels, conditioning, asymptotic bounds and universal upper threshold remain outside this checked scope. [Six-module replay](verify_rank.py) and [log](verification/rank-reproduction.log) record strict Lean 4.35.0 builds, transitive axiom checks of all 164 source-named declarations (standard axioms only) and stock kernel replay.

- [MaTangCoreOccupancy](../../lean/Conjectures/MaTangCoreOccupancy.lean): enumerates all length-k words over m labels and three ternary representatives without duplicates; proves total count `(3*m)^k`, the exact singleton count `k*2*(3*m-2)^(k-1)` and union bound `(m-1)*k*2*(3*m-2)^(k-1)`. The endpoint `successful_word_count_le` bounds the number of successful words under the explicit per-word matrix kernel-spanning hypothesis. It proves a finite independent-column-model counting result, not the distribution of the actual random kernel or the limiting conjecture.

- [MaTangCoreBalance](../../lean/Conjectures/MaTangCoreBalance.lean): ternary sign balancing for arbitrary grouped integer coefficients, including zeros.
- [MaTangCoreIndexed](../../lean/Conjectures/MaTangCoreIndexed.lean): ordered labeled coefficient positions, exact projection to the original list, and a binary selection with constant residue under every type-dependent flip fixing type zero. Conditional deterministic noncoverage.
- [MaTangCoreMatrix](../../lean/Conjectures/MaTangCoreMatrix.lean): literal binary matrix multiplication over F₂ and XOR linearity; an actual missing pair, with first coordinate in the image of the matrix and ternary residue in {0,1,2}, under an explicit spanning representation of the kernel by the supplied encoded columns. The endpoint is `MaTangCoreMatrix.exists_missing_matrix_pair`.

[Reproduction script](verify_matrix.py) and [exact log](verification/matrix-reproduction.log): strict compilation against Lean 4.35.0, transitive axiom checking for all 74 source-named declarations (only the three standard axioms), and stock kernel replay for all three modules. The audit is scoped to source-named declarations, not the full environment-wide repository audit. No Mathlib import is needed for these modules. The full aggregate check and hosted CI have not passed.
