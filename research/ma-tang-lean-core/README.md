# Ma–Tang’s random subset-sum threshold conjecture

**Incomplete formalization.** Deterministic fiber and exact finite occupancy components have passed local Lean verification; the other three Mathlib draft modules remain unverified. No catalogue completion status change.

Existing result: [main catalogue entry](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/tree/main/results/ma-tang-random-subset-sums), [recorded proof](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/blob/main/results/ma-tang-random-subset-sums/PROOF.md), [discovery issue #490](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/490), and [publication PR #492](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/pull/492). The primary source is Jie Ma and Quanyu Tang, *An Erdős problem on random subset sums in finite abelian groups*, arXiv:2602.05768v2, [Problem 4.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.05768v2#S4). Credit the original problem to Ma and Tang and the written resolution to the authors credited in the repository's existing proof package. The earlier Erdős question had already been resolved; no new discovery or exclusive priority is claimed.

## Scope

- [MaTangCoreGenerator](../../lean/Conjectures/MaTangCoreGenerator.lean) proves the exact equivalence of the nonzero-null-vector event and noninjectivity and its uniform independent-column count bound: failure probability at most (2^{-d}) for (k=d+r), (r>0), in exact integer-count form. [MaTangCoreDecay](../../lean/Conjectures/MaTangCoreDecay.lean) proves the explicit sequence (m=2^r, q=4r+16, k=2mq+1, d=k-r) has singleton-occupancy fraction at most (1/(16m)), by integer powers and a block estimate. For (r=2^j), the checked dimension squeeze is (2^r\le d\le 2^{r+j+6}), and (C(j+6)\le 2^j) for (j\ge C+6). This is an exact discrete form of the negligible logarithmic loss. The choice of sequence differs from the source's floor formula but retains the sharp second-order coefficient; it does not yet prove the limiting real-log statement. [Eight-module replay](verify_decay.py) and [log](verification/decay-reproduction.log) passed strict Lean 4.35.0 compilation, transitive axiom checks of all 217 source-named declarations and stock kernel replay.

- [MaTangCoreSubspace](../../lean/Conjectures/MaTangCoreSubspace.lean) and [MaTangCoreRank](../../lean/Conjectures/MaTangCoreRank.lean): construct a bijective encoded-column parametrization of every binary subspace by induction on its first coordinate. For a surjective binary matrix with k input coordinates and d rows, count its duplicate-free fibers to prove exactly r=k−d; prove that every covering mixed binary/ternary matrix sample has an actual encoded kernel of that dimension and a singleton occupied nonzero type. Endpoints: `exists_encoding`, `encoding_rank_nullity`, and `mixed_coverage_actual_kernel`. These results no longer assume the existence or dimension of a kernel basis. The distribution of sampled kernels, conditioning, asymptotic bounds and universal upper threshold remain outside this checked scope. [Six-module replay](verify_rank.py) and [log](verification/rank-reproduction.log) record strict Lean 4.35.0 builds, transitive axiom checks of all 164 source-named declarations (standard axioms only) and stock kernel replay.

The fourth [checked core module](../../lean/Conjectures/MaTangCoreOccupancy.lean) enumerates the actual length-k words over the m-label, three-residue alphabet without duplication, proves the sample-space size (3m)^k, exact singleton count k·2·(3m−2)^(k−1) for every specified label in range, and the union count upper bound (m−1)k·2·(3m−2)^(k−1). The endpoint `MaTangCoreOccupancy.successful_word_count_le` combines the occupancy event with the checked matrix obstruction, conditionally on the explicit kernel spanning representation for each supplied matrix. It is an exact finite counting bound, not yet the law of the kernel of a uniformly sampled matrix or an asymptotic theorem. [Reproduction script](verify_occupancy.py) and [log](verification/occupancy-reproduction.log) pass strict compilation, a transitive axiom audit of all 119 source-named declarations, and stock replay of the four core modules. Occupancy source SHA256 `bc3c930220c3b98c19115a52d1d5801f64b456b5ef53cdb30999de3bce2a8279`.

The third [checked core module](../../lean/Conjectures/MaTangCoreMatrix.lean) specializes the ordered-position theorem to a literal binary matrix, proves its XOR linearity, and shows that a kernel vector with the specified encoded column representation induces a type-constant flip fixing zero. The endpoint `MaTangCoreMatrix.exists_missing_matrix_pair` gives a missing ternary residue for an actual image element of the first-coordinate matrix, and `not_covers_of_matrix_kernel` excludes coverage of the correctly dimensioned binary/ternary codomain. The hypothesis that every kernel vector is represented by the supplied encoded columns remains explicit. It does not assert the kernel dimension, existence of a basis of that dimension, uniformity, probability estimates, or the asymptotic threshold. [Strict scoped replay](verify_matrix.py) and [log](verification/matrix-reproduction.log) pass for all three core modules, with transitive axiom checks of 74 source-named declarations.

The second [checked core module](../../lean/Conjectures/MaTangCoreIndexed.lean) eliminates the assumption that the input is already grouped. It handles an arbitrary ordered list of column labels and integer coefficients (including zeros), constructs an actual binary selection list of the same length, and proves invariance of the ordinary subset sum modulo three under every type-constant flip fixing the zero type. The endpoint `MaTangCoreIndexed.not_covers_of_type_fibers` then proves that an arbitrary first-coordinate map cannot cover every pair of first coordinate and ternary residue, under the explicit hypothesis that its fiber differences have the specified type-flip representation. This is the deterministic noncoverage reduction from equations (4)–(5); the actual linear-map kernel representation and random occupancy/distribution/asymptotic arguments remain to be verified. Both core modules passed strict compilation, a transitive audit of all 57 source-named declarations (only the three standard axioms), and stock kernel replay. [Reproduce](verify_indexed.py) and see the [exact log](verification/indexed-reproduction.log). Indexed module SHA256 `983c021c9e0bb930d34571517065c28c1ba923e400f91fc75e253d5b28e88388`.

The [checked Init-only module](../../lean/Conjectures/MaTangCoreBalance.lean) proves sign balancing for arbitrary integer representatives modulo three, including zero coefficients, and constructs an exact list of annotated grouped coefficients for which every type-constant binary flip preserving the zero type leaves the ternary sum unchanged. This is the deterministic grouped-list component of equations (4)–(5), not the full subset-sum conjecture. Its endpoint is `MaTangCoreBalance.exists_grouped_constant_fiber`; it does not yet connect the lists to a random linear map or prove the limiting threshold. The pinned Lean 4.35.0 strict compilation passed; all 25 source-named declarations were checked transitively and only `propext` and `Quot.sound` occurred; stock `leanchecker` replay passed. Reproduce with [verify_core.py](verify_core.py); [log and source hash](verification/core-reproduction.log). The scoped source hash is `ddb959fd9a40f664dead70a468aee434a492ec14375941ea567885f3d42a3b80`. This source-named check is narrower than the repository's environment-wide all-owned audit.

The draft [Lean module](../../lean/Conjectures/MaTangRandomSubsetSumsCore.lean) follows equations (4) and (5) of the recorded proof. It replaces the pairing/tripling sign construction by a subset of size (2n mod 3), where n is the number of occupied indices in a nonzero type. It groups the finite signed sum by binary kernel-column types and shows that the second-coordinate subset sum is constant on the chosen first-coordinate fiber.

The endpoint `MaTangRandomSubsetSums.not_surjective_of_kernel_columns` is an attempted formalization of the deterministic noncoverage implication, under an explicit fiber representation and no-singleton hypothesis. It is not an asymptotic or probabilistic theorem.

The second draft [kernel module](../../lean/Conjectures/MaTangRandomSubsetSumsKernel.lean) attempts to discharge the representation hypothesis for an actual binary linear map using a basis of its kernel. The endpoints are `kernel_basis_represents_fiber` and `not_surjective_of_kernel_basis`. A canonical finite-dimensional kernel basis is supplied as a definition; no claim is made about its distribution.

A separate standard-library [finite check](check_core.py) passed 6,361 admissible pairs of column labels and coefficients with k ≤ 4 and r ≤ 2, checking every type-constant flip that preserves the zero type. This validates the tested mathematical construction only. It does not establish any Lean theorem.

The third draft [occupancy module](../../lean/Conjectures/MaTangRandomSubsetSumsOccupancy.lean) counts words with exactly one occupied letter of a given type by a hit/miss equivalence, then bounds a union using an injection into the disjoint union of hit-position classes. Its specialization `singleton_occupancy_card_le` bounds the number of bad words by (2^r-1) k 2 (3·2^r-2)^(k-1). The total number of words is (3·2^r)^k; their uniform normalization would produce the source's union bound. The finite checker also confirmed the exact single-type count and union bound for all 15 pairs 0 ≤ r ≤ 2, 0 ≤ k ≤ 4. This module is still an uncompiled draft, not a verified lemma.
