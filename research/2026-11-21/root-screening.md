# Named conjecture screening, 21 November 2026

This continuation searches for complete new resolutions of named conjectures. No Lean work, finite-order extension, existing theorem, or numerical evidence is counted as a resolution. The three parallel research lanes are special functions, permutation statistics, and graph theory. This root file records source checks and negative results so later workers do not repeat them.

## Excluded: Yuster's entropy inequality

Tanay Wakhare, *Iterated Entropy Derivatives and Binary Entropy Inequalities*, [arXiv:2312.14743v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.14743v2), Conjecture 1, asks for the sharp binary-entropy inequality for all real exponents greater than one. It is already proved by Boon Suan Ho, *A generalization of Boppana's entropy inequality*, [arXiv:2601.19327v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.19327v1), Theorem 1 (27 January 2026). The full four-page primary proof was inspected. Its key monotonicity follows from strict concavity of the logarithmic mean. An independent derivation of that same argument during this session does not create a new result.

The related polynomial-root conjecture must be screened separately. [This public record](https://mathdb.com/p/357503/the-root-count-conjecture-for-p-k) already links a claimed exact four-root counterexample by Myeongjin Kang at k=48. That claim has not been independently audited in this session. It is a priority warning, not a result of this repository.

## Excluded: Holevo–Utkin's norm conjecture

The original paper is [arXiv:2603.24017](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.24017). Haonan Zhang's [arXiv:2605.05243v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05243), *Proof of the Holevo–Utkin conjecture on sharp l_p norms for zero-sum vectors*, explicitly settles the remaining dimensions for the exact minimum and maximum formulas. The primary abstract and revision record were inspected (first posted 4 May 2026, revised 21 May 2026). Excluded from new work.

## Pereira–Plosker's trumping conjecture

The existing full candidate and independent reviews in [the research directory](../pereira-plosker-trumping/README.md) were read. The original [arXiv:1405.2845](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1405.2845), Definition 2 and Conjecture 1, was reopened directly. The two existing gates remain: the intended interpretation of a simple zero at the boundary of absolute convergence, and the precise scope of Diamond–Essén (1978), DOI [10.1016/0022-247X(78)90091-4](https://doi.org/10.1016/0022-247X(78)90091-4). Searches through both search engines and a direct publisher attempt did not retrieve the latter theorem; the publisher returned an access error. No full-conjecture resolution is counted.

Coordination is recorded in [issue 96](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/96#issuecomment-7058557833). Current external lanes on Currie words, wild numbers, and circular edge colouring were identified in issues 146–155 and left to their current workers.
