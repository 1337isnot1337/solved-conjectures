## Acceptance and novelty gates

- **Mathematical scope:** all ten are whole author-stated universal propositions. A single exact counterexample negates a universal statement; no finite search is counted as proving an infinite conjecture.
- **Dependencies:** none of these ten requires accepting the repository's RH or Dirichlet-GRH claims. The separately tracked Robin, Nicolas, Grosswald, Brent, Pomerance, Soundararajan, and other GRH consequences import existing conditional theorems and are **not** included as new independent proofs here.
- **Proof completeness:** all ten have symbolic/all-quantifier arguments in the linked files. Source-review and internal mathematical proof checks are recorded in their corresponding issues. This is a collection of *unrefereed research outcomes*, not ten externally established mathematical theorems.
- **Historical priority:** targeted web/paper searches found no earlier exact resolution of these targets that would invalidate a novelty claim, but such a negative search cannot prove absence of an unindexed, unpublished, or inaccessible earlier argument. The Baricz and Wagner audit files note specific inaccessible follow-up manuscripts.
- **Terminology:** Rowland–Sistos Barrón use “pseudo-polynomial” without defining it. Richard Stanley's *Enumerative Combinatorics*, vol. 1, 2nd ed., §4.4, p. 474, calls pseudopolynomial a synonym of quasipolynomial, supporting the interpretation used by the counterexample. Confirmation from the conjecture's authors would resolve remaining terminological uncertainty.
- **Publication status:** none of the arguments is claimed to have undergone external journal peer review. Attribution should retain the authors of each conjecture, earlier partial/conditional results, and all collaborating researchers in the corresponding GitHub histories.
