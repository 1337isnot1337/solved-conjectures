# Source and review

The target is [Conjecture 6.2 in Bóna–Pantone, *Permutations avoiding sets of patterns with long monotone subsequences*](https://arxiv.org/html/2103.06918v2#S6.SS2), for $A_{5,3}$, not the nearby questions for $A_{5,4}$ or $A_{5,5}$. The earlier growth-rate theorem supplies the exponential base, not the asymptotic equivalence. A targeted Nov. 24, 2026 search by source and conjecture numbers, author names, the exact forbidden set and proposed scale found the original problem and related work, but no earlier resolution. This is not an exclusive priority claim.

The argument uses the independently reviewed marker estimate from the [second-largest-terminal proof](../pantone-bona-second-largest-terminal/PROOF.md). Independent checks of the new steps:

1. **Shape location.** First truncate the recording tableau at the end of the interval; deleting its earlier recording labels corresponds to deleting the same prefix of the input and performing jeu de taquin. Conditional on the full shape, every recording tableau is equally likely. Fixing the before/after interval shapes, the number of skew recording segments rectifying to a fixed straight tableau is the Littlewood–Richardson coefficient. The product $p_1^a s_\nu p_1^{m-a-g}$ has the same coefficient of $s_\lambda$ for each location by commutativity; this validates the exact equality of location laws. Passing to the inverse turns an interval of values into an interval of positions, preserving the relevant longest increasing subsequence.
2. **Band compatibility.** A length-$d$ increasing witness before the first high entry forces every later value between its last two values. If both halves of a value prefix have witnesses, the maximum lower endpoint exceeds the minimum upper endpoint. A large admissible band cannot itself contain a length-$d$ witness, since that would contradict minimality of the upper endpoint. Two independent estimates are not assumed: the proof interpolates two upper bounds on the same event, then sums.
3. **Tail and limit.** Prepending the first $d-1$ witness values forbids `123` and `132` in the remaining tail. The condition $d\ge3$ allows a generating-function parameter strictly between $2/d^2$ and the radius supplied by the band estimate. This makes all tails of size at least two lower order. The one-entry tail has $m+1$ potential cuts minus the location of the first marker, whose expectation is bounded. Therefore its contribution is asymptotic to $n f_{n-1}$, with the stated constant.

[verify.py](verify.py) enumerates literal avoiders in small sizes, checks the exact one-entry-tail count and necessary conditions on other tails, and compares all contiguous interval shape laws at fixed final shape through length seven. These finite checks are supplementary. Internally reviewed; not Lean-formalized or externally refereed.
