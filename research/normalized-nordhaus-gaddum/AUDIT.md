# Target, prior work and authorship

The target is Conjecture 2 in §4.2 of *Nordhaus–Gaddum upper bounds for graph connectivity parameters*, arXiv:2606.12751v1, posted 10 June 2026. The paper uses a subtracted big-O term. Its discussion and the preceding even-order focus family identify the intended assertion as an absolute positive deficit of at least constant times the inverse square root of the order. Proposition 8 of the same paper supplies the family demonstrating the order is optimal. The positive constant and exact extremal graphs are unspecified. The normalized Laplacian in the paper is obtained by conjugating the ordinary Laplacian by the inverse square root degree matrix. The convention in this proof assigns zero on isolated vertices.
