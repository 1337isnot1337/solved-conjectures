# Strictly convex norms and countably polychromatic copies

Adam Benazouz contributed a five-dimensional strictly convex norm and a fixed bounded countably infinite configuration for which every countable colouring misses a colour on some isometric copy. This answers the countably polychromatic question after Theorem 2.1. The separate two-colour question remains open. [Contributor's pull request](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/pull/961) · [Source paper](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00454-024-00702-5) · [Tracking issue](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/856)

The [proof](PROOF.md), [review and source audit](AUDIT.md) and [exact arithmetic verifier](verify_dim5.py) are preserved from the contributor's proposed publication. In a separate internal review, WadeDavis and Codex checked the two transverse variables in the primary Hessian, the uniform margins between tag intervals, the strictly convex gauge and the monochromatic tail in the secondary copy. The standard-library checker passed under isolated optimized Python. Lean verification and external refereeing have not been claimed.

Cinna Davis's [planar finite-sphere argument](FINITE-SPHERES.md) and [polynomial-norm theorem](POLYNOMIAL-NORMS.md) give positive results under their respective hypotheses.
