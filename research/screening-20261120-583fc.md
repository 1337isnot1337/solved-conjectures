## Exclusions and source ambiguities

### Li–Morgan–Siegrist's concavity-transition conjecture

The original publisher text of Conjecture 6.1 asks for a unique sign-changing zero of the second derivative under Theorem 5.2. A public counterexample record already exists. Exclude the target.

- Original paper, 13 October 2026: https://doi.org/10.1007/s41478-026-01192-9
- Prior refutation and source dossier: https://www.trureturing.org/research/li-morgan-siegrist-2026-inflection-points-conjecture-6-1/
- Prior proof description: https://the-omega-institute.github.io/trureturing-mdbook/Blueprint/D5/S0/Certificates/LiMorganSiegristInflectionRefutation.html

### Xu–Yang's Mills-remainder conjecture

A prior all-index proof for the intended range n >= 2 is public. That record also identifies the failure at n = 1 in an unqualified reading. Neither the proof nor the omitted-index observation can be counted here.

- Original paper: https://doi.org/10.1186/s13660-026-03505-9
- Prior complete proof and index discussion: https://the-omega-institute.github.io/trureturing-mdbook/Problems/xu-yang-2026-mills-remainder-cm.html

### Zhang's symmetric-modulus conjecture

The current MathDB page for a quasisymmetric-modulus statement has no posted solution. The primary problem dossier distinguishes the arithmetic and quadratic moduli and warns of a misidentification in a later source. The arithmetic-modulus conjecture already has a September counterexample. An empty solution list is not evidence of novelty.

- Original Zhang source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.01046
- Source reconciliation and prior resolution: https://github.com/ajt60gaibb/OpenProblemsInNLA/blob/main/matrix-inequalities-and-norms/MI-06/README.md
