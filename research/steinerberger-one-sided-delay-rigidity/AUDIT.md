# Scope and source check

**Exact question.** The unconditional periodic theorem and the later conjecture differ by their quantifiers. Steinerberger's [journal article, §1.4 and Theorem 3](https://www.impan.pl/shop/en/publication/transaction/download/product/91307#page=3) assumes the shift equation for every x and then states periodic rigidity. [§3.1, printed p.275](https://www.impan.pl/shop/en/publication/transaction/download/product/91307#page=13) explicitly asks for rigidity when the equation is required only at points where f(x)<0. The latter is also printed in [the author's March 31, 2026 open-problem list, §37](https://faculty.washington.edu/steinerb/openproblems.pdf#page=38). The latest [arXiv record (v3)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1410.0588) corresponds to the published-era text. The counterexample is periodic, C∞, sign-changing, and of mean zero; adding those hypotheses from the surrounding discussion does not repair the statement.

**What is not claimed.** No claim is made about the separate conjecture that finitely many *maximizing* averaging radii force a sinusoid. Differentiating a stationarity condition does not preserve its sufficiency, much less characterize global maximizers. This is also distinct from the analytically trivial vacuity of taking f≥0 everywhere; our negative set is a nonempty interval in each period and its endpoints are simple zeros.

**Prior-art screen (2026-11-27).** Checked the journal proof and conjecture, author-maintained open-problem list, latest arXiv record, journal DOI listings, zbMATH/MathReviews-linked metadata through MaRDI, backwards and forward related work, the author's 2015 Oxford talk summary, and indexed arXiv, Hexagon, MathOverflow, GitHub, and general-web results by exact article title, DOI, arXiv ID, one-sided/negative-region equation, periodic bump, and mixed-frequency queries. The searches surfaced the original conjecture and the periodic global theorem, not a previous explicit periodic sign-changing counterexample to §3.1 or a periodicity correction to that conjecture. The source itself explains why the **ungated nonperiodic** equation has many solutions, and its Fourier multiplier gives mixed-frequency examples immediately; these are not claimed as new. This is a bounded priority search, not a claim that unpublished counterexamples do not exist.

**Self-check.** The support and shifted-support intervals are strictly disjoint; equality of endpoint signs is not needed. Both shifted function values are 1 and both derivatives 0 on the entire negative set. The integral over a period is exactly zero. The distinct strict minima force the stated least period. At resonant periods the equation reduces to f'=0 on each negative component and continuity excludes a finite endpoint. No numerical approximation or formal verification is used.
