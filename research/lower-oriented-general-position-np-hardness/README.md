# The lower oriented general position NP-hardness conjecture

**Status:** proved by a written argument; not formalized in Lean.

The decision problem in Conjecture 5.3 of [*The general position number of digraphs*](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.15909v1#S5.Thmtheorem3) is NP-complete, even for planar bipartite graphs with two leaves and every other vertex of degree three. In any graph with at least two leaves, an orientation of general position number two exists exactly when the graph has a Hamilton path.
