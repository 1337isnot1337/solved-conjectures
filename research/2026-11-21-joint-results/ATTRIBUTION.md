## 4. Source-scope checks

The substantive restrictions in the reviewed packages are preserved in the final ledger:

- **Chen–Kitaev–Zhang:** only original Conjectures 1 and 3 supply the new package. Conjecture 2 is credited prior work, with no additional count.
- **Novelli–Thibon:** the intended corrected statement and its hook consequence are additional results outside the strict tally. The printing error is not being counted as a newly discovered disproof. The separate Schur-positivity consequence is excluded as prior work.
- **McCrea:** the comparisons are restricted to fixed densities in the original interval, with the crossing removed. There is no claim at the crossing, outside that interval, or with a threshold uniform in density. The exact domain is

```math
k\ge3,\qquad
x\in\left(2^{-k-1},\frac1{4k}\right)\setminus\{c_k\}.
```

- **Fang–Zhang–Zhao, excluded:** the counterexamples do refute the full universal infinite-log-convexity assertion, but the same disproof was already public. It contributes zero, independently of parameter counting. The Baxter-number case remains separate.
- **López Pouso–Segura:** the Bessel package covers its three full sequence assertions, while the quadrature package refutes the two stated upper bounds. They count once together. The ledger does not claim to settle every conjecture in the source paper.
- **Vishnyakova:** the original and weighted all-degree formulations count together. Earlier equal-parameter and bounded-degree results are credited, rather than being counted independently.
- **Gröchenig, excluded:** the [checked September manuscript](../bounty-continuation-2026-11-21/grochenig-prior-resolution/) proves a stronger counterexample to the exact upper-density assertion. The repository construction has the correct source scope but is a rediscovery and contributes zero.
