# Additional primary-source checks

Checked 21 November 2026. These exclusions and leads are not mathematical
solutions by this campaign.

## Rump's matrix prizes: both already solved

The [sponsor's current PDF](https://www.tuhh.de/ti3/rump/100EuroProblem.pdf)
and [homepage](https://www.tuhh.de/ti3/rump/) explicitly mark both prizes as
solved. Teng Zhang supplied the first solution on 8 March 2026. The second
problem, involving Euclidean row norms at least `sqrt(n-1)`, was solved on
6 August 2026; the sponsor says the second 100-euro award was paid.

Zhang's [April paper revision](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.07423v2) does not
contain that August update. Reading only its discussion of the stronger
conjecture would therefore produce a stale research target. Neither prize is
selected for this campaign.

## Chebyshev primality test: limited payment terms

The proposer's [MathOverflow question](https://mathoverflow.net/questions/286304/chebyshev-polynomials-of-the-first-kind-and-primality-testing)
offers 100 euros for a proof published in one of three named journals.
The question also asks for counterexamples, but the payment paragraph does not
offer payment for a counterexample. The live discussion includes reasons to
expect very large pseudoprimes; it contains no completed converse proof in
the material inspected. This is not selected as a paid counterexample target.
