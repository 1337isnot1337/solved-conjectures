# Uniform abundance of every triangle pair in finite counterexamples

**Corollary.** There are constants $`\varepsilon>0`$ and $`n_0`$ such that in every exactly balanced six-coloring of $`K_{6t+1}`$ with $`6t+1\ge n_0`$ and no rainbow six-cycle, each of the fifteen pairs of colors is contained in at least $`\varepsilon(6t+1)^3`$ rainbow triangles. The constants are not effective in the argument below. The conclusion is a necessary condition, not an assertion that counterexamples exist.

In fact a stronger compactness formulation holds in colored graphon space. For every fixed edit-error threshold $`\eta>0`$, there is $`\delta>0`$ such that a balanced six-color graphon with rainbow six-cycle density and some pair's rainbow-triangle density both at most $`\delta`$ has a measurable parity cut with total misplaced edge mass at most $`\eta`$.

For the graphon assertion, suppose a sequence violates it as $`\delta\to0`$. Fix the specified pair after a subsequence. By compactness in the simultaneous colored cut metric and continuity of colored homomorphism densities, the limit is balanced and has both densities zero. By [the missing-pair parity theorem](MISSING-PAIR-PARITY.md) it has a parity cut. The integrals of the prescribed color-palette kernels on the appropriate rectangles of that cut converge to zero. This contradicts the fixed error exclusion on every cut in the sequence.

For the finite assertion, if it fails, choose unbounded exactly balanced, rainbow-six-cycle-free orders with one pair's triangle count $`o(n^3)`$, fixing the pair by a further subsequence. Use the colored cut limit as in [GRAPHON-CRITERION.md](GRAPHON-CRITERION.md); normalized vertex degrees tend to $`1/6`$, diagonal cells vanish, and repeated-vertex maps do not affect the two zero limits. The missing-pair theorem gives exact parity of the limit. Discrete cuts with $`o(n^2)`$ misplaced edges follow by independent rounding of the limiting cut inside the vertex cells. The [global parity-cut repair](PARITY-CUT.md) then yields a rainbow six-cycle for every sufficiently large order of the sequence, a contradiction.

This strengthens the [finite fixed-pair triangle-packing result](PAIR-TRIANGLE-TRANSVERSALS.md) in the special case of hypothetical six-cycle-free colorings. It gives no better universal fixed-pair bound when rainbow six-cycles are allowed. Coordination: [#870](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/870).
