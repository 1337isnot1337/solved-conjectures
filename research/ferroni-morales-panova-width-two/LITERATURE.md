# Ferroni–Morales–Panova conjecture: literature screening

**Checked 20 November 2026.** Bounded searches in two web engines, current primary published text, arXiv records, author-indexed and specialist pages. **No earlier full proof or counterexample to the width-two statement was found in checked materials; this does not guarantee novelty or exclusive priority.**

- **Primary:** Luis Ferroni, Alejandro H. Morales, Greta Panova, *Skew shapes, Ehrhart positivity, and beyond*, *Proceedings of the London Mathematical Society* **133** (2026), e70204. Published 20 August 2026; funding-statement correction dated 25 August. [Full text](https://londmathsoc.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1112/plms.70204), [arXiv:2503.16403](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.16403), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1112/plms.70204). **Conjecture 7.1 / Conjecture 1.5(i)** quantifies over every width-two poset. Theorem 3.4 reduces it to linear-coefficient nonnegativity. Theorem 1.1 proves the different case of skew-shape *cell posets*; Remark 7.2 records the overlap. Remark 7.3's complement-zigzag tests up to 200 vertices do not prove the full conjecture.
- [Conference version: FPSAC 2026, SLC 95B.24](https://www.mat.univie.ac.at/~slc/wpapers/FPSAC2026/24.html), final version 1 April 2026. It is not an independent later complete width-two proof.
- Yakob Kahane, *Combinatorial interpretation of the coefficients of the order polynomial of fence posets*, [arXiv:2607.11225](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11225), with version 2 posted **2 November 2026**, concerns fences and proposes extensions. It does not claim the full width-two statement.
- Pyuyi Chufeng Huang, *Greedy Records and Bernstein Transfers for Fence and Circular-Fence Order Polynomials*, [arXiv:2607.22767](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22767), concerns fence and circular-fence families. Those do not exhaust all width-two posets.
- Braun–Jal, *Order polytopes of generalized snake posets are h*-real-rooted* ([topic bibliography](https://arxiv.symmetricfunctions.com/keyword/order%20polytope)), concerns a restricted family and the Ehrhart h*-polynomial, not the unrestricted coefficient-positivity assertion.
- Jochemko–Menon, *Ehrhart positivity for marked order polytopes*, [arXiv:2604.08394](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08394), had already proved **different** skew Gelfand–Tsetlin and generalized Pitman–Stanley conjectures. These must not be counted as still-open targets or conflated with Conjecture 7.1.
- [MathDB listing](https://mathdb.com/p/367613/nonnegative-order-polynomial-coefficients-for-width-two-pose), accessed as a secondary specialist index, lists no posted solution. That label by itself is not evidence of global openness.

Search terms included exact author/title/conjecture number, width-two and width-2 variants, order-polynomial linear coefficient and positivity, proof/counterexample language, and 2026 date restrictions. We compared publication and revision dates and read the full primary article's statement and Theorem 3.4. We did not inspect every unpublished manuscript, and any unindexed or inaccessible later solution could alter status.

The explicit six-vertex non-real-zero example in [PROGRESS.md](PROGRESS.md) refutes only a **stronger real-rootedness proposal**; it leaves all coefficients nonnegative. It is not presented as a novel literary first, as a conjecture counterexample, or as a separate named solved problem.
