# Source and novelty audit

- [Cameron and Clow, On Gyárfás’ Path-Colour Problem, §7](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.19100v1#S7)
  poses the independence-four question explicitly as Problem 7.2.
  Lemma 2.2 treats only independence number at most three. Our
  statement covers every finite graph in the exact class, and also
  identifies its sharp boundary.
- [Jedličková and Kratochvíl, Theorem 3.7](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.03668v1#S3.SS3)
  ([journal version](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcss.2025.103754))
  gives the relevant complete traceability obstructions for
  graphs of independence number at most four. The structural
  theorem is earlier work, not an original ingredient here.
  The deduction observes that the two-cut obstruction is
  impossible in a vertex-critical graph. The traceability paper
  does not state a conclusion about path chromatic span.
- The standard two-cut statement for critical graphs goes back
  to Dirac; see [Bondy and Murty, §8.5](https://cs.bme.hu/fcs/GTWA.pdf)
  and [the Dirac–Gallai decomposition, Theorem 5](https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/article/download/v25i1p50/pdf/).
  We reproduce the short coloring argument using vertex
  criticality only, without assuming edge criticality.
- The [Erdős Problem 640 working report](https://erdosproblemaday.com/report/640#explicit-13-vertex-path-witness)
  already gives the 13-vertex graph used for sharpness. Its
  existence, construction and path chromatic span are not new
  claims. We use its independence number, established directly,
  to show the threshold is sharp. No reliance is placed on the
  report's finite-order minimality or uniqueness counts.
- [Long–Milans–Munaro, longest path transversals](https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/article/download/v30i1p32/pdf)
  addresses a different longest-path property at independence
  number four; intersection of longest paths does not imply
  that a longest path spans a chromatic-critical subgraph.

Searches on 27 November 2026 included the exact problem number,
the source title and identifier, the conjecture authors'
[publication lists](https://www.smcs.upei.ca/~brcameron/research.html)
and [Clow’s list](https://www.alexanderclow.com/publications),
and combinations of “path-perfect,” “independence number four,”
“vertex-critical,” “traceable,” “two-cut,” and the structural
theorem authors. No earlier explicit solution of Problem 7.2
was found. The contribution is the deduction of the exact
boundary from credited ingredients, not a claim that their
component theorems or the witness were unknown.
