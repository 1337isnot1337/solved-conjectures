# Literature and scope audit

Checked on 20 November 2026. No resolution of the real-rootedness and interlacing assertion was located in the sources below. Search absence does not establish priority. This file records target selection; the investigation has not yet produced a solution.

## Exact primary target

Xavier Coulter and Norman Do, *From Weingarten calculus for real Grassmannians to deformations of monotone Hurwitz numbers and Jucys–Murphy elements*, [arXiv:2506.04002v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.04002v1), [full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.04002v1), submitted 4 June 2025. The arXiv record checked lists one version.

Conjecture C in the introduction consists of Conjectures 4.21 and 4.22 in Section 4.3. For every nonnegative half-integer genus, positive number of profile parts, positive integer profile, and real deformation parameter $`b\notin\{0,-1\}`$, the connected $`bt`$-monotone Hurwitz polynomial should be real-rooted in $`t`$. It should interlace each polynomial obtained by increasing one profile part by one. Corollary 4.16 supplies the recursion; Tables 2–4 give small symbolic examples. The reported root checks use nine integer values of $`b`$, not a continuous-parameter proof.

The zero polynomial at the degenerate degree-one profile and positive genus is not being used as a convention-based counterexample. A disproof must exhibit a nonzero polynomial with nonreal roots, or a genuine failure of interlacing.

## Distinct results that do not settle the target

1. Coulter, Do and Ellena Moskovsky, *Integration on complex Grassmannians, deformed monotone Hurwitz numbers, and interlacing phenomena*, [arXiv:2308.04015](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.04015), [published article](https://doi.org/10.4153/S0008414X25101168). The one-parameter predecessor states the $`b=0`$ root and interlacing conjectures. It is not a proof of the two-parameter assertion.

2. Raphaël Fesler, Marvin Anas Hahn, Maksim Karev and Hannah Markwig, *A refined twist on Hurwitz numbers*, [arXiv:2508.06188v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.06188v1). Its abstract mentions partial progress on a Coulter–Do conjecture. Section 1.4 identifies that target as the operator conjecture numbered 5.4 in Coulter–Do, involving commuting operators and a simultaneous eigenbasis. It is not Conjecture C. The full text contains no real-rootedness theorem.

3. The [trureturing record for Conjecture 5.4(a)](https://www.trureturing.org/research/coulter-do-2025-deformed-jucys-murphy-noncommutation/) reports a refutation of the commutation assertion. Its [separate Conjecture 5.8 record](https://www.trureturing.org/research/coulter-do-2025-conjecture-5-8/) concerns an operator identity. These records exclude the operator assertions as fresh targets; neither is a root statement about the connected Hurwitz polynomial.
