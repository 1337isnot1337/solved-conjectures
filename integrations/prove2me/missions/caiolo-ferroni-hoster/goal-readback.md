For every natural number $q\ge 17$ with $q=p^n$ for some prime $p$ and positive integer $n$, there exist types $F$ and $K$ in the base type universe, each equipped with a field structure and a finite enumeration, and a unital $F$-algebra structure on $K$, such that $|F|=q$ and $\dim_F K=4$. On the disjoint union of the one-dimensional $F$-subspaces of $K\times K$ and the underlying set of $K$, there exists a simple matroid $M$ with that entire union as its ground set and with extended rank equal to the finite value $5$. Simplicity means every finite subset of this union with at most two elements is independent in $M$. For each ordered sublist $s=(r_1,\ldots,r_m)$ of $(4,3,2,1)$, including the empty sublist, let $N_s$ be the real-valued count of tuples of flats $E(M)\supseteq B_1\supseteq\cdots\supseteq B_m$ of respective extended ranks $r_1,\ldots,r_m$, with $N_{\varnothing}=1$. Let $d_0,\ldots,d_m$ be the successive differences along $(5,r_1,\ldots,r_m,0)$, so the empty sublist has sole difference $d_0=5$. Put $a_d(T)=-(-1)^d\sum_{0\le j\le d}T^j$ and $c_d(T)=-(-1)^d\sum_{0\le j<d}T^j$. The real polynomials $P(T)=\sum_s N_s c_{d_0}(T)\prod_{i=1}^{m}a_{d_i}(T)$ and $Q(T)=\sum_s N_s\prod_{i=0}^{m}a_{d_i}(T)$, with empty products equal to $1$, have natural degrees $4$ and $5$, respectively. For every real $x$, $P(x)>0$. For every real $x$, $Q(x)=0$ if and only if $x=-1$. Also $Q'(-1)\ne 0$, and neither polynomial splits over $\mathbb R$.
