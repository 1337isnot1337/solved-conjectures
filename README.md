For Steinke–Zakynthinou’s agnostic VC conjecture, Lean verifies empirical accuracy on every sample and the information bound on every fixed paired pool. For iid data, measurability of a globally tie-broken empirical minimizer under independent fair labels suffices. A VC-one counterexample rules out the unrestricted claim if every proper learner must be measurable on the completed iid law. A separate standard Borel VC-one class admits a Borel empirical minimizer, yet the constructed learner fails measurability for every tie-breaking order at every sample size of at least two. Whether another learner works throughout the intended scope remains unresolved. [Manuscript](results/steinke-zakynthinou-agnostic-vc/paper.pdf) · [Scope audit](results/steinke-zakynthinou-agnostic-vc/SCOPE.md)

For the 🟢 Lonely Runner results, the written proofs reduce the classification to finitely many cases. Exact computations exhaust those cases, with some results drawing on archived searches from cited papers. Completing the Lean proofs requires formalizing the remaining reductions and cited bounds and checking the remaining search certificates in Lean. The [verification guide](docs/verification.md#computational-checks) gives the replay commands, and the [Lean status report](docs/lean-status.md) gives the remaining steps for each result.

For Pomerance–Sárközy’s fluctuation conjecture, the Lean scope covers the quarter-root Gaussian law and the square-root convergence in probability of the original counting function. The [formalization scope](results/pomerance-sarkozy-fluctuations/FORMALIZATION.md) records the two declarations and the remaining written results.

The rank-five matroids in the Caiolo–Ferroni–Hoster result also settle Badalov’s first-index question for the ordinary inverse Kazhdan–Lusztig polynomial. The first Turán ratio tends to zero. The supplement has a reviewed written proof and exact arithmetic checks. [First-index counterexample](results/caiolo-ferroni-hoster/FIRST-INDEX.md)

## Major theorem consequences (not separately solved conjectures)

The dandelion theorem answers the 2018 question of Afshari, Akbari, Moghaddamzadeh and Mohar. The larger algebraic connectivity of a graph and its complement is always at least $`2-\sqrt2`$, with equality only for the four-vertex path. [Sharp bound and proof](results/barrett-evans-hall-kempton-dandelion/SHARP-MAXIMUM.md)

This repository takes Anthropic's VP ≠ VNP theorem and its stated lower bounds as valid research premises under the [standing assumption in `AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md#standing-research-assumption-anthropics-vp-versus-vnp-proof). The applications below use that imported result; they do not independently establish the source theorem, claim outside verification, or add a solved named conjecture.

- [Quantitative decision lower bound for the permanent graph](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/tree/research/research/bss-permanent-graph/). Deciding $`y=\mathop{\mathrm{per}}_m(X)`$ over $`\mathbb C`$ requires $`2^{\Omega((\log m)^{4/3})}`$ operations in the stated algebraic computation-tree model, with defined division and arbitrary constants. The written deduction combines the pinned Anthropic border lower bound with Bürgisser's corrected factor theorem and gives the uniform BSS simulation. It is not a new named-conjecture resolution or a proof of $`\mathsf P_{\mathbb C}\ne\mathsf{NP}_{\mathbb C}`$. No Lean formalization of this deduction is claimed. [Proof](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/blob/research/research/bss-permanent-graph/PROOF.md) · [Review](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/blob/research/research/bss-permanent-graph/REVIEW.md) · [Issue #247](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/247)

- [Bounded-occurrence 3-CNF weighted counting](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/blob/research/research/vp-vnp-bounded-occurrence-3cnf/README.md). Explicit all-characteristic Hamiltonian-cycle projection to a weighted 3-CNF polynomial with at most three occurrences per variable. With Anthropic's November 23, 2026 VP ≠ VNP theorem adopted as valid, its all-field border-circuit lower bound transfers to this family. This is a VNP-completeness application of the imported theorem, not a newly solved conjecture; it remains excluded from result totals. [Finite verifier](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/blob/research/research/vp-vnp-bounded-occurrence-3cnf/verify_reduction.py).

## Partial results on open problems

This section is ordered by the significance of the partial contributions.

- **Blitvić–Kammoun–Steingrímsson packing cutoff question.** No fixed number of occurrences determines all subsequent packing counts. [Proof and remaining length-dependent question](results/blitvic-kammoun-steingrimsson-packing-cutoff/).
- **Erdős’s four-cycle threshold conjecture.** Regular candidates have large leave components and must differ from an affine polarity graph by a superlinear number of edge deletions in the plane order. [Proofs and exact scope](results/erdos-85-four-cycle-threshold/).
- [Wild number of complete graphs](results/wild-number-complete-graphs/). The conjectured value holds for all $n\ge155$. The range $13\le n\le154$ remains open.
- [Seven upper-half circular chromatic index certificates](results/circular-index-upper-half-remaining-deltas/). Tomdif’s examples at degrees $9,11,13,15,17,19,23$, together with the earlier notes, cover every maximum degree $\Delta\ge8$. [Lean proof for every degree](results/circular-index-upper-half-remaining-deltas/FORMALIZATION.md).
- [Upper-half circular chromatic index for degrees congruent to 1 or 2 modulo 4](results/circular-index-upper-half-delta-2-mod-4/). Constructions for degrees $\Delta=4g+1$ and $4g+2$ with $g\ge6$, plus finite certificates for degrees $10,14,18,22$. [Lean proof for every parameter](results/circular-index-upper-half-delta-2-mod-4/FORMALIZATION.md). [Lean certificates for the 17 stored nonregular graphs](results/circular-index-upper-half-circulants/FORMALIZATION.md).
- [Upper-half circular chromatic index for degrees congruent to 3 modulo 4](results/circular-index-upper-half-delta-3-mod-4/). Examples for every $\Delta\equiv3\pmod4$ with $\Delta\ge27$. Together with [tomdif’s seven certificates](results/circular-index-upper-half-remaining-deltas/), the combined results cover every maximum degree $\Delta\ge8$. [Complete Lean verification](results/circular-index-upper-half-delta-3-mod-4/FORMALIZATION.md).
- [Shifted Lonely Runner](results/shifted-lonely-runner-counterexamples/). Lean verifies the exact maximum for each counterexample with $18\le n\le57$. The range $58\le n\le94$ remains open.
- [Steinke–Zakynthinou’s agnostic VC learning conjecture](results/steinke-zakynthinou-agnostic-vc/). Lean verifies the rates under the stated measurable-selection hypothesis.
- [Frankl’s union-closed sets conjecture](results/frankl-generator-families/). A four-position generator theorem and a frequency bound for regular, high-girth incidence graphs. Both all-point frequency bounds are fully Lean-verified, including the high-girth theorem for every uniform generator size at least three. The cyclic span-four formalization remains incomplete. [Checked scope](docs/lean-status.md#complete-frankl-high-girth-frequency-theorem).
- [Lonely Runner Conjecture](results/lonely-runner-two-insertions/). Two arbitrary speeds can be adjoined to every Goddyn–Wong simultaneous acceleration. The full prescribed-interval theorem, acceleration bridge, unbounded and finite cases, common-dilation value bound, and three-insertion obstruction are Lean-verified. [Checked scope](docs/lean-status.md#complete-unrestricted-two-speed-insertion-theorem).
- [Erdős–Purdy conjecture](results/erdos-block-size-multiplicity/). In a nontrivial pairwise balanced design on $`v`$ points with a point-transitive abelian automorphism group, at least $`v`$ blocks have one size. A power improvement over the square-root lower bound also holds for finite noncollinear point sets in a projective plane over any field.
- [Erdős–Hajnal–Szemerédi independence-ratio problem](results/erdos-hajnal-szemeredi-independence/). A graph of cardinality at most the continuum is countably colorable if its finite subgraphs have bounded vector chromatic number. Finite graphs can have bounded Hall ratio and arbitrarily large vector chromatic number.
- [Erdős–Hajnal set-mapping conjecture](results/erdos-hajnal-set-mapping/). Matching missing colors to available subsets yields $`H(n)\le \log_2 n+\log_2\log_2 n+O(1)`$. A separate circuit obstruction restricts constructions from binary nullspaces.
- [Erdős–Hammer convex-subset problem](results/erdos-hammer-838/). The binary Erdős–Szekeres order-type limit has convex-subset probability $`2^{-k^2/4+O(k\log k)}`$. Balanced substitutions give a quadratic exponent of at least one half for the minimum limiting density.
- [Komjáth’s property B problem](results/komjath-property-b/). There is an almost-disjoint family of infinite sets on a countable ground set, with no singleton intersections, that admits a proper two-coloring yet defeats every attempt to put two points of each color in every edge.
- [Erdős’s bounded blocking-set problem](results/erdos-bounded-blocking-set/). Translation planes of bounded dimension over a kernel field of bounded characteristic have blocking sets meeting each line in a uniformly bounded number of points.
- [Gyárfás’s nearly bipartite graph conjecture](results/gyarfas-nearly-bipartite/). If Gyárfás’s conjecture has a counterexample, one exists on at most 330 vertices. The conjecture holds for arbitrary subdivisions of rectangular Möbius grids.
- [Multicolor Steiner triple system sail problem](results/multicolor-steiner-sail/). For every fixed number of colors, almost every Steiner triple system has a positive density of monochromatic sails in every vertex subset of fixed positive density, simultaneously for all block colorings.
- [Blázsik–Lemons one-intersection conjecture](results/blazsik-lemons-one-intersection/). A finite hypergraph whose edges have size at least two and whose distinct edges never meet in exactly one vertex admits a proper weak coloring that is rainbow on every part of any prescribed partition into equal-sized blocks of size at least two.
- [Erdős–Sauer conjecture](docs/erdos-sauer-research.md). The hypergraph decomposition bound holds asymptotically for each fixed uniformity.
- [Bollobás–Erdős–Tuza conjecture](results/erdos-611-clique-transversals/). For every fixed hollowness fraction the required transversal can have size $`n^{1-o(1)}`$. Excluding a fixed induced biclique gives a sublinear upper bound.
- [Erdős–Granville–Pomerance–Spiro conjecture](results/erdos-granville-pomerance-spiro/). For every natural density-zero target set, the preimage conclusion holds on a fixed family of inputs of natural density approximately 53.46%. The full conjecture remains open.
- **Bourin’s crossed-Heinz conjecture.** The inequality holds in every dimension and every unitarily invariant norm for all exponents when the spectra of both inputs lie in one positive interval with endpoint ratio at most √2. [Theorem and scope](results/bourin-crossed-heinz/).
- [Erdős essential-component conjecture](docs/erdos-essential-component-research.md). An equivalent expansion problem modulo powers of two, with quantitative density gains on all sufficiently long prefixes.
- [Erdős–Gyárfás power-of-two cycles](results/erdos-gyarfas-abelian-covers/). A sharp cycle-length bound for abelian covers of a singly subdivided tetrahedron. The universal voltage-cover theorem, arbitrary-voltage normalization, and sharp connected 95-vertex cyclic example are Lean-verified. [Checked scope](docs/lean-status.md#complete-sharp-95-vertex-example-and-arbitrary-voltage-normalization).
- [Erdős–Oler conjecture](docs/erdos-oler-research.md). The conjectured lower bound holds for packings on any unit-separated lattice, and triangular lattice packings with one site removed are locally rigid.
- [Hadamard existence, order 2396](results/hadamard-2396/). The automorphism obstruction, fixed-border symmetry bounds, complete conditional fixed-border construction with all fourteen patterns, and length-598 order-eleven obstruction are Lean-verified. The actual two-row Hadamard construction from a Legendre pair and its invariant quotient transport are also checked. The order-nine subgroup and orbit statements are also checked. The length-1197 multiplier order is proved to divide 27; the sharper bound of nine and finite quotient/no-lift certificates remain pending. Existence stays open. [Checked scope](docs/lean-status.md#complete-conditional-fixed-border-construction-and-fourteen-patterns).

## Leech lattice cold spots

As the Gaussian parameter tends to infinity, the cold spots approach deep holes of type $`E_8^3`$. This corrects the $`A_{24}`$ limiting type stated by Bachoc, Moustrou, Vallentin and Zimmermann. [Proof and exact comparison](docs/research-results/leech-cold-spots-asymptotics/README.md)

A supplement characterizes which deep holes of any lattice remain global minimizers for all sufficiently large parameters. It also computes the exponentially small displacement near a Leech hole of type $`A_{24}`$. [Mixed-hole criterion](docs/research-results/leech-cold-spots-asymptotics/MIXED-HOLES.md)

## Universal Gaussian cold spots of dual type A

For every Gaussian parameter, the minima on $`A_n^*`$ occur exactly at the deep holes. The proof settles the intermediate parameters in every rank at least four and gives the minimum as an explicit product. [Theorem and review](docs/research-results/dual-a-universal-cold-spots/README.md)

## Site-centered pinned distances

A separate relaxation of Erdős’s one-third question restricts only circles centered at sites. Mixed-circle incidence bounds, an active-bisector criterion, uniform-disk limits and a four-grid obstruction describe this case. A positive gap holds under the stated bounds on isosceles trapezoids and under a weaker bound on reflected-pair multiplicities that permits sparse exceptions. Under the global quadratic bound alone, every rescaled finite limiting measure at the one-third barrier is purely atomic or wholly nonatomic. If the limit contains several atoms, at least one atomic component must persist when their shrinking clouds are rescaled at a fixed high mass cutoff. In any growing independent sample from such a sequence, the expected fraction of convex-hull vertices tends to zero. [Site-centered results and exact scope](results/erdos-general-position-pinned-distances/README.md#circles-centered-at-sites)

## Gaussian cold spots of high-rank type-A root lattices

Above a rank threshold, the deep holes of $`A_n`$ are exactly its minimizers for every positive Gaussian scale. Uniform small- and large-scale intervals also work in every rank. In every rank and at every scale the deep classes minimize when all but one coordinate are congruent. The remaining finite ranks at intermediate scales require a comparison with arbitrary translates. [All central levels](docs/research-results/root-a-high-rank-cold-spots/ALL-CENTRAL.md) · [One-outlier theorem](docs/research-results/root-a-high-rank-cold-spots/ONE-OUTLIER.md) · [Partial theorem and review](docs/research-results/root-a-high-rank-cold-spots/README.md)

## Recognizing subdivided trees

Unlabelled distances between leaves determine the total length of a generic tree and characterize every possible ambiguity. A binary tree’s shape is recovered, and all metric alternatives come from permuting the sums at pairs of sibling leaves. [Leaf-distance classification](docs/research-results/stanley-tree-subdivisions/CHERRY-CLASSIFICATION.md)

For every fixed tree topology with at least 28 leaves, a generic subdivision is identified by its ordinary distance histogram against all trees with at most two more leaves. The marked two-extra-leaf theorem holds at every order. [Unmarked and marked results](docs/research-results/stanley-tree-subdivisions/TWO-SURPLUS-TAIL.md)

For any fixed tree topology, the ordinary distance histogram of a generic subdivision identifies it among all trees with at most one additional leaf. There is no degree bound. [One-extra-leaf theorem](docs/research-results/stanley-tree-subdivisions/ONE-EXTRA-LEAF.md)

For any fixed allowance of extra rival leaves, one finite test decides generic distance-histogram identification across every tree topology, with degrees unrestricted. A lone high-degree hub forces a surplus growing at least linearly in its degree. [Finite reduction and single-hub bounds](docs/research-results/stanley-tree-subdivisions/LEAF-SURPLUS-KERNEL.md)

For every fixed tree topology, almost every subdivision is the unique minimum-leaf tree with its ordinary vertex-distance histogram. The histogram identifies it among all trees when every direction at each high-degree vertex contains sufficiently many leaves. [Exact condition and proof](docs/research-results/stanley-tree-subdivisions/ROBUST-HUB-DISTANCES.md)

Almost every independent subdivision of each tree with one four-valent vertex and otherwise trivalent hubs is determined among all trees by its ordinary distance histogram alone. [Proof for the first positive degree excess](docs/research-results/stanley-tree-subdivisions/QUARTIC-DISTANCE-RIGIDITY.md)

For any fixed degree excess, one finite test decides whether almost every independent subdivision of every reduced tree of that excess is determined by its unlabelled distance histogram. Any failure already occurs on a core with at most twelve times the excess minus two vertices. [Finite reduction and scope](docs/research-results/stanley-tree-subdivisions/FIXED-EXCESS-REDUCTION.md)

Degree counts and the unlabelled histogram of vertex-pair distances determine almost every independent integer subdivision of any fixed reduced tree. The paths alone suffice when the target has only trivalent branch vertices, even against arbitrary competitors. [Observable certificate and quantitative bounds](docs/research-results/stanley-tree-subdivisions/PATH-DISTANCE-CERTIFICATE.md)

For any fixed internal tree with arbitrary rooted caps at the tips, a proportion tending to one of the independent exterior length choices in the stated intervals give a tree identifiable among all trees. The proof recovers the internal branching pattern and path lengths. [Arbitrary-core theorem](docs/research-results/stanley-tree-subdivisions/ARBITRARY-CORE.md)

The ordinary chromatic symmetric function determines a tree after independent, sufficiently long subdivisions of every edge incident to any chosen branch vertex. It also distinguishes every sufficiently long subdivision of a single edge. Two hubs are recognizable for arbitrarily independent lengths under a condition on terminal leaf counts, or for arbitrary rooted ends in specified exterior-order regimes, including a dominant arm. A balanced-edge argument gives a bound in terms of both end orders. Other theorems recognize trees with enough leaves at their centroid or with specified repeated branches. Stanley’s question for all trees remains open. [Theorems and reviews](docs/research-results/stanley-tree-subdivisions/README.md)
