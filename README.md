For Steinke–Zakynthinou’s agnostic VC conjecture, Lean verifies empirical accuracy on every sample and the information bound on every fixed paired pool. For iid data, measurability of a globally tie-broken empirical minimizer under independent fair labels suffices. A VC-one counterexample rules out the unrestricted claim if every proper learner must be measurable on the completed iid law. A separate standard Borel VC-one class admits a Borel empirical minimizer, yet the constructed learner fails measurability for every tie-breaking order at every sample size of at least two. Whether another learner works throughout the intended scope remains unresolved. [Manuscript](results/steinke-zakynthinou-agnostic-vc/paper.pdf) · [Scope audit](results/steinke-zakynthinou-agnostic-vc/SCOPE.md)

For the 🟢 Lonely Runner results, the written proofs reduce the classification to finitely many cases. Exact computations exhaust those cases, with some results drawing on archived searches from cited papers. Completing the Lean proofs requires formalizing the remaining reductions and cited bounds and checking the remaining search certificates in Lean. The [verification guide](docs/verification.md#computational-checks) gives the replay commands, and the [Lean status report](docs/lean-status.md) gives the remaining steps for each result.

## Major theorem consequences (not separately solved conjectures)

This repository takes Anthropic's VP ≠ VNP theorem and its stated lower bounds as valid research premises under the [standing assumption in `AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md#standing-research-assumption-anthropics-vp-versus-vnp-proof). The applications below use that imported result; they do not independently establish the source theorem, claim outside verification, or add a solved named conjecture.

- [Quantitative decision lower bound for the permanent graph](research/bss-permanent-graph/). Deciding $`y=\mathop{\mathrm{per}}_m(X)`$ over $`\mathbb C`$ requires $`2^{\Omega((\log m)^{4/3})}`$ operations in the stated algebraic computation-tree model, with defined division and arbitrary constants. The written deduction combines the pinned Anthropic border lower bound with Bürgisser's corrected factor theorem and gives the uniform BSS simulation. It is not a new named-conjecture resolution or a proof of $`\mathsf P_{\mathbb C}\ne\mathsf{NP}_{\mathbb C}`$. No Lean formalization of this deduction is claimed. [Proof](research/bss-permanent-graph/PROOF.md) · [Review](research/bss-permanent-graph/REVIEW.md) · [Issue #247](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/247)

- [Bounded-occurrence 3-CNF weighted counting](research/vp-vnp-bounded-occurrence-3cnf/README.md). Explicit all-characteristic Hamiltonian-cycle projection to a weighted 3-CNF polynomial with at most three occurrences per variable. With Anthropic's November 23, 2026 VP ≠ VNP theorem adopted as valid, its all-field border-circuit lower bound transfers to this family. This is a VNP-completeness application of the imported theorem, not a newly solved conjecture; it remains excluded from result totals. [Finite verifier](research/vp-vnp-bounded-occurrence-3cnf/verify_reduction.py).

## Partial results on open problems

This section is ordered by the significance of the partial contributions.

- [Wild number of complete graphs](results/wild-number-complete-graphs/). The conjectured value holds for all $n\ge155$. The range $13\le n\le154$ remains open.
- [Upper-half circular chromatic index for degrees congruent to 1 or 2 modulo 4](results/circular-index-upper-half-delta-2-mod-4/). Constructions for degrees $\Delta=4g+1$ and $4g+2$ with $g\ge6$, plus finite certificates for degrees $10,14,18,22$.
- [Upper-half circular chromatic index for degrees congruent to 3 modulo 4](results/circular-index-upper-half-delta-3-mod-4/). Examples for every $\Delta\equiv3\pmod4$ with $\Delta\ge27$. The all-degree question remains open at degrees $9,11,13,15,17,19,23$.
- [Shifted Lonely Runner](results/shifted-lonely-runner-counterexamples/). Exact counterexamples for every $18\le n\le54$. The remaining range is $55\le n\le94$.
- [Steinke–Zakynthinou’s agnostic VC learning conjecture](results/steinke-zakynthinou-agnostic-vc/). Lean verifies the rates under the stated measurable-selection hypothesis.
- [Frankl’s union-closed sets conjecture](results/frankl-generator-families/). A four-position generator theorem and a frequency bound for regular, high-girth incidence graphs. Both all-point frequency bounds are fully Lean-verified, including the high-girth theorem for every uniform generator size at least three. The cyclic span-four formalization remains incomplete. [Checked scope](docs/lean-status.md#complete-frankl-high-girth-frequency-theorem).
- [Lonely Runner Conjecture](results/lonely-runner-two-insertions/). Two arbitrary speeds can be adjoined to every Goddyn–Wong simultaneous acceleration. The full prescribed-interval theorem, acceleration bridge, unbounded and finite cases, common-dilation value bound, and three-insertion obstruction are Lean-verified. [Checked scope](docs/lean-status.md#complete-unrestricted-two-speed-insertion-theorem).
- [Erdős–Sauer conjecture](docs/erdos-sauer-research.md). The hypergraph decomposition bound holds asymptotically for each fixed uniformity.
- [Bollobás–Erdős–Tuza conjecture](docs/bollobas-erdos-tuza-research.md). A lower bound of order $\sqrt{n\log n}$ for maximal independent set transversals, and a constant upper bound at bounded modular width.
- [Erdős–Granville–Pomerance–Spiro conjecture](results/erdos-granville-pomerance-spiro/). For every natural density-zero target set, the preimage conclusion holds on a fixed family of inputs of natural density approximately 53.46%. The full conjecture remains open.
- [Erdős essential-component conjecture](docs/erdos-essential-component-research.md). An equivalent expansion problem modulo powers of two, with quantitative density gains on all sufficiently long prefixes.
- [Erdős–Gyárfás power-of-two cycles](results/erdos-gyarfas-abelian-covers/). A sharp cycle-length bound for abelian covers of a singly subdivided tetrahedron. The universal voltage-cover theorem, arbitrary-voltage normalization, and sharp connected 95-vertex cyclic example are Lean-verified. [Checked scope](docs/lean-status.md#complete-sharp-95-vertex-example-and-arbitrary-voltage-normalization).
- [Erdős–Oler conjecture](docs/erdos-oler-research.md). The conjectured lower bound holds for packings on any unit-separated lattice, and triangular lattice packings with one site removed are locally rigid.
- [Hadamard existence, order 2396](results/hadamard-2396/). The automorphism obstruction, fixed-border symmetry bounds, complete conditional fixed-border construction with all fourteen patterns, and length-598 order-eleven obstruction are Lean-verified. The actual two-row Hadamard construction from a Legendre pair and its invariant quotient transport are also checked. The order-nine subgroup and orbit statements are also checked. The length-1197 multiplier order is proved to divide 27; the sharper bound of nine and finite quotient/no-lift certificates remain pending. Existence stays open. [Checked scope](docs/lean-status.md#complete-conditional-fixed-border-construction-and-fourteen-patterns).

## Open research investigations

- [P_C versus NP_C over C](research/complex-bss-p-vs-np/README.md) remains open in this campaign. The notes fix the uniform model and HN encoding, give a sound weighted-sum formula with growing alternation, prove exact failures of proposed compressions, and isolate the missing incidence and arbitrary-multiple implications. They build on the graph lower bound above. [Issue #248](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/248) · [Review and checks](research/complex-bss-p-vs-np/REVIEW.md).

- [Boolean P versus NP via directed Hamiltonicity](research/boolean-hamiltonicity/). Research remains open. Proved coefficient identities, a containment-preserving restriction, a monotone logarithmic-space predicate matching the first logarithmic band of layers, and counterexamples to proposed circuit-rank bounds. No unrestricted superpolynomial circuit or uniform time lower bound is claimed. [Attack record](research/boolean-hamiltonicity/ATTACKS.md) · [Review and checks](research/boolean-hamiltonicity/REVIEW.md) · [Issue #251](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/251).
