# Permutation digraph general position conjecture

**Disproved throughout its stated range.** For every $k\geq 3$ and $d\geq 2k$, a three-marker general position set is larger than the conjectured value. Optimizing the marker count gives an unbounded ratio to that value for fixed $k$ as $d$ grows. The proof also determines the order of magnitude uniformly in both parameters: a fraction of order $1/k$ of the vertices can be in general position.

This refutes the sharpness conjecture after [Theorem 3.6 of Chandran et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.15909#S3.SS3). It does not refute their lower bound or settle the separate exact-value question.
