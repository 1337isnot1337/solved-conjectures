# Scope and independent audit

The exact target is published Conjecture 4.10 of Higashitani–Kummer–Michałek, *Interlacing Ehrhart polynomials of reflexive polytopes*, also Kölbl's restatement (which refers to earlier numbering). We test the precise operation of decreasing a largest part by one, not deleting an entire part. Two independent counterexamples refute both clauses of one conjecture.

Besides reviewing the transport gauge and missing-part counts, an independent implementation counted each part by its positive and negative coordinate masses, multiplied the bivariate count arrays, and recovered all three exact Ehrhart numerators. Exact Sturm counts give three positive squared imaginary roots for the first witness, but degree five; for the proposed interlacing pair they give six positive roots each and counts one versus three below squared height 441/100. The individual polynomials therefore lie on the line while the pair does not interlace. A bounded current priority search located no earlier resolution of the same clauses.

No Lean verification, external review or absolute priority claim is made.
