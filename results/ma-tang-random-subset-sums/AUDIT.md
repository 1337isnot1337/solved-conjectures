# Scope and independent audit

The exact target is [Ma–Tang arXiv:2602.05768v2, Problem 4.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.05768v2#S4). They conjecture equality at the universal limsup upper coefficient. This is not the earlier Erdős error-term question #543, already answered by Ma and Tang, nor a claim for prime-order groups. The 1965 Erdős–Rényi upper bound and 1978 Erdős–Hall cyclic and elementary-abelian results are explicitly credited. Priority screening included source-ID and mathematical scope checks in the collection, newer citing sources, and indexed MathDB, Hexagon and public result catalogues. No earlier exact mixed-group result was located in that search. We could not inspect the complete separately linked Sothanaphan exchange; the primary source describes its scope.

Independent mathematical review checked conditional random-kernel uniformity, independence from the odd factor, occupancy bounds, sign assignment in characteristic three, the fiber identity and the without-replacement transfer. The elementary argument resolves the full conjecture without Yu's preprint. The supplement has an elementary stronger lower bound and then obtains the bounded remainder using [Yu, Theorem 3](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.01300v2), with the base-packing and finite-field transfer independently checked.

No Lean verification, external peer review or absolute priority claim is made.
