# Prior-art audit

This note concerns $h_3(n,0)$ in the sense of [Erdős–Galvin–Hajnal (1975)](https://users.renyi.hu/~p_erdos/1975-24.pdf), with a single witness for the full finite induced-density profile. It is not the finite asymptotic question sometimes denoted by $h_3$ in the Erdős problem catalogues, nor the function $g_3(n,0)$ of the same source, which only requires uncountable chromatic number.

- **Original text.** Definition 3.4 gives the no-equally-large-independent-set convention; Corollary 3.6 gives $\lfloor(n-1)^2/4\rfloor$ as the universal lower profile. Theorem 10.5 constructs one $H$ that attains it assuming CH, and Corollary 10.7 states the equality. This manuscript asks whether the CH conclusion itself is a theorem of ZFC; it does not attribute a separately numbered problem about that profile to the authors.
- **Related negative and positive directions.** [Abraham–Cummings–Smyth, Theorem 2 and Remark 4](https://www.math.cmu.edu/users/jcumming/papers/polyx_revised_dec2006.pdf) gives the exact positive pair-rainbow result required here, attributing ZFC-relative consistency to [Todorčević (1983)](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9947-1983-0716846-0). [Soukup](https://www.renyi.hu/~soukup/setop/otka61600/rainbow.pdf) reviews Galvin's negative result under CH and gives further models with negative bounded pair-rainbow relations. The existence of these contrasting rainbow models is known; the claim here is the exact ZFC equivalence with the unrestricted 3-graph density profile. The 1983 full scan was not accessible; its application here is through the later authors' precise attribution.
- **Search scope.** Searched using $h_3$, the source title and author names, the six-point/seven-edge threshold, terminology for the lack of large free sets, and links between local triple density and bounded or polychromatic pairs. The closest indexed results in finite extremal hypergraph theory studied other functions; those in infinite hypergraphs often considered chromatic number rather than independent sets of full cardinality. No previous proof of the exact unrestricted equivalence was located in this pass. This is not a certification of priority.

The local obstruction consists of seven distinct triples on six distinct vertices: both fixed pairs share one vertex, their other endpoints are distinct, and the remaining three vertices form a triple in their common co-neighborhood. Changing that threshold to six *or fewer* is essential to this argument. For the quantitative consequence no infinite list of colorings is refined at once; each fixed parameter uses only finitely many rainbow refinements.
