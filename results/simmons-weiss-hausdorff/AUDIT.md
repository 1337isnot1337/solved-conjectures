# Scope and independent audit

The precise open scope is the natural Hausdorff measure left out in [Simmons–Weiss, Example 9.2, printed p. 32](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/145241/1/BQ_and_BA.pdf#page=33). Their Bernoulli theorem and its implications for irrational sumsets are prior. The new argument is an almost-sure cusp result for uniformly conditionally positive laws, not an equidistribution theorem.

The audit verified the inverse-block semigroup, adapted trial probability bound, compact orbit accumulation and the nilpotent stable direction, explicit Möbius transfer, and the Hausdorff conditional-mass comparison. It found no earlier result for the stated natural measure and every irrational translate.

No Lean verification, external review or absolute priority claim is made.
