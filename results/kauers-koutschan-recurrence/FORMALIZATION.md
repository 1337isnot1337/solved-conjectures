# Lean scope for the Kauers–Koutschan recurrence

Lean proves [Conjecture 10 of Kauers and Koutschan](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.07966) for the actual permutation count in A189281. The theorem [`KauersKoutschan.conjecture10`](../../lean/Conjectures/KauersKoutschanConjecture.lean) applies the nine coefficient rows of the source order-eight operator at every natural index. The Lean operator table matches all 98 integer coefficients in [the source table](operator.txt).

- [Divided differences](../../lean/Conjectures/KauersKoutschanDividedSum.lean). Lean proves the equality of the paired and centered full sums in §4, both first-order state recurrences, and equation (5) for every natural index. It also proves the omitted-pair identity in equation (6) for every admissible shifted cutoff, including an empty omitted range.
- [Polynomial certificate](../../lean/Conjectures/KauersKoutschanCertificate.lean). The original coefficient certificate from [PR #281](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/pull/281) checks the published order-eight and order-thirteen operators for both parities and all six independent state components. It also checks the four initial residuals on the finite-sum model. The decisions are exact kernel reductions over rational coefficients.

- [Exact finite-sum recurrence](../../lean/Conjectures/KauersKoutschanFiniteRecurrence.lean). Lean evaluates the rational coefficient operations and reconstructs the literal shifted derangement states from all six basis states. It proves that the exact sixteen-mask sum in equation (4) satisfies the original order-eight operator at every natural index, including the four initial indices.
- [Actual permutation inclusion–exclusion](../../lean/Conjectures/KauersKoutschanPermutationCount.lean). Lean defines the original forbidden-rise predicate and proves its signed count in terms of intersections of the chosen value-adjacency events.

- [Maximal blocks and positions](../../lean/Conjectures/KauersKoutschanBlockLengths.lean). Lean identifies every value with a unique head and finite offset. The block lengths sum to the permutation length. Every realizing permutation places the blocks without gaps or overlaps in the two position chains, and the positions of the block heads determine that permutation uniquely.

- [Signed factorial expansion](../../lean/Conjectures/KauersKoutschanSignedBlocks.lean). Lean proves that realizing permutations correspond bijectively to legal block assignments and a separate order of the blocks in each position chain. The two factorials and the product of maximally selected block signs give the exact signed expansion of the literal permutation count.

- [Composition sum](../../lean/Conjectures/KauersKoutschanCompositionCount.lean). Lean reindexes the selected edges by the two value-chain compositions. Each composition part is identified with a literal block and its exact length, and the signed factorial weights transfer to the two-composition sum.
- [Formal column series](../../lean/Conjectures/KauersKoutschanColumnSeries.lean). Lean establishes the signed block series, clears its two geometric denominators, and identifies each coefficient from the quadratic trace–determinant recurrence and column numerator.
- [Schur moment](../../lean/Conjectures/KauersKoutschanSchurMoment.lean). The independent factorial functional on the actual bivariate trace–determinant polynomial gives the character times the rational derangement divided difference. The proof checks the radial and uniform moment decomposition, including coincident scalings.
- [Trace product](../../lean/Conjectures/KauersKoutschanTraceProducts.lean). The product of the two complete homogeneous trace polynomials splits over all common twist indices through the smaller degree.

- [Exact coefficient extraction](../../lean/Conjectures/KauersKoutschanCompositionCoefficients.lean). The coefficient of the target row lengths in the two marked columns equals the signed factorial count of the actual permutations. The polynomial Schur moment and the diagonal character then identify [every source mask](../../lean/Conjectures/KauersKoutschanMaskCoefficients.lean) with its exact divided-difference sum, including infeasible masks and empty sums.
- [Complete endpoint](../../lean/Conjectures/KauersKoutschanConjecture.lean). The actual permutation count equals the finite sum in equation (4) for every length. Applying the verified finite-sum recurrence proves the original conjecture, including the empty permutation and the initial indices.
