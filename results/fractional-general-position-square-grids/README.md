# Fractional general position conjecture for square grids

**Proved.** The exact fractional general position number of every square grid is given by the conjectured lower bound: corner truncation for even side length and checkerboard truncation for odd side length. The proof gives a minimum-weight fractional geodesic cover. The two orientations are coupled through the rank graph of the grid, using a linear potential whose boundary values satisfy a closed recurrence.

This resolves both assertions of [Conjecture 5.38 of Ullas Chandran, Klavžar and Tuite](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.19385v5#S5.SS4). The lower constructions and the two-flow formulation are from [Fitzpatrick, Janssen and Nowakowski (2006)](https://ajc.maths.uq.edu.au/?page=get_volumes&volume=34).
