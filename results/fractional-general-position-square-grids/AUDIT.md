# Proof audit and prior-resolution search

## Scope and source

Ullas Chandran S.V., Sandi Klavžar and James Tuite, [*The General Position Problem: A Survey*, arXiv:2501.19385v5, §5.4, Definition 5.30 and Conjecture 5.38](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.19385v5#S5.SS4), specifies the two assertions in [the proof](PROOF.md). The earlier author-hosted v2 numbered it Conjecture 5.37; v3, v4 and current v5 use 5.38. The latest text credits the original exact values through seven and the separately checked orders through ten, not an all-orders proof.

Shannon L. Fitzpatrick, Jeannette Janssen and Richard J. Nowakowski, [*Fractional isometric path number*, Australas. J. Combin. 34 (2006), 281–298](https://ajc.maths.uq.edu.au/?page=get_volumes&volume=34), supplies the prior lower labelings, the two basic path orientations and earlier upper bounds. The [author-hosted report](https://cdn.dal.ca/content/dam/dalhousie/pdf/faculty/computerscience/technical-reports/CS-2004-02.pdf) gives the details in Lemmas 2.1–2.2 and §§2.2–2.4. The fractional general position parameter in the survey is twice the fractional covering parameter in the original paper. The new contribution is the exact upper cover by a linear rank potential, boundary recurrence and interlacing, in both parities.

## Review gates

- **Geodesics:** a shortest path in a grid has no backtracking in either coordinate. Extending both ends to the relevant opposite corners preserves geodesicity. The two rotations account for all such paths and every used cover path belongs to the exact same graph.
- **Normalization:** each orientation has total weight W, so the dual cover weight is 2W and the upper bound on the label sum is 4W, not 2W. Diagonal positions in the odd rank graph count once as positions; their Laplacian terms cancel. The rank graphs are connected after omitting loops.
- **Even cutoffs:** comparison with either neighboring cutoff gives t ≤ W ≤ t+1. The two central parity baselines differ, so ordinary convexity is not assumed. The recurrence uses the same-parity deletion of both extreme neighbors; the endpoints determine its two constants. Reflection gives the upper nonnegativity bound without making an extra assumption about the Laplacian solution.
- **Odd cutoffs:** both checkerboard masses have the same row total W. The closed boundary formula has a component factor; the ratio bound compensates for that factor at every adjacent increment. The lower construction attained by at least one of the two parities makes the upper bound exact even if the subcritical parity overcovers its active cells.
- **Transport:** row sums alone are insufficient. The proof checks both signed interlacing inequalities using single sign changes, verifies the active-to-outer seam and the final singleton row, and then uses one common quantile across the ranks. An independent implementation of the couplings allocates consecutive nonzero masses greedily in increasing position, rather than assuming those signs.
- **Exact run:** `python3 verify.py --limit 110 --also 199 200` checked 109 orders, 529,322 cell-cover inequalities and 12,778 adjacent-row couplings, plus both primal corner orientations. It computes rational potentials from the recurrence and checks every row sum; the primal maximum path weight is recomputed by dynamic programming. Neither the written proof nor this check relies on floating-point optimization. Small order three is covered directly by the written argument.

## Priority screen

Searched the exact conjecture title and both version numbers; the fractional general position and fractional isometric path names; the 2006 title, author combinations, its later citations and journal record; the exact leading constant; and exact grid formulas in arXiv, general scholarly search, MathOverflow, MathDB and formal-library indexes. The clearly identified nearby integral-cover result is [Fisher and Fitzpatrick, *The Isometric Path Number of a Graph*](https://combinatorialpress.com/article/jcmcc/Volume%20038/vol-038-paper%209.pdf), which concerns an integer cover instead. Public indexes for [the exact two-parity conjecture](https://www.emergentmind.com/open-problems/optimal-fractional-grid-bounds) and [the larger grid question](https://www.emergentmind.com/open-problems/fractional-general-position-grids) still list them as open; these indexes are leads and not mathematical evidence. The proof and open wording were rechecked against v5. Before the claim and again for publication, neither branch's catalogue nor the issue/PR searches showed a duplicate. The similarly named ordinary, oriented, and lower general position results make different claims.

This is a bounded priority search and a written mathematical/algorithmic audit, not a claim of external verification, peer review, or Lean formalization. [Research history and publication issue](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/909).
