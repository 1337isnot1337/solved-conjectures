# Scope and independent audit

The exact target is Ulas's **Conjecture 4**, printed p. 333 of [*On products of disjoint blocks of consecutive integers* (2005)](https://www.e-periodica.ch/view/ens-001/2005/51/417), with [publisher OCR for the full volume](https://www.e-periodica.ch/download/ens-001:2005:51/ocr-fulltext). The common length is fixed first, then every sufficiently large fixed number of blocks is allowed. Solutions are distinct collections, each with disjoint intervals; the original statement does not require all solutions to be mutually disjoint across collections.

Earlier variable-cardinality results and the specific-length work of Ulas, Bauer–Bennett, Bennett–van Luijk and [Yıldız–Gürel](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00574-019-00158-9) are credited. In particular, this is not the separate conjecture that fixes a number of blocks uniformly before their length grows. The separate supplement proves the bounded mixed-length case for multisets, not a prescribed spatial ordering. It does not add a second named conjecture count.

Independent review checked the affine-root and leading-coefficient parity, the uniform [Granville–Soundararajan mean estimate](https://annals.math.princeton.edu/2001/153-2/p04) and disjoint random sampling, odd-prime Walsh inversion, and residue stabilization above arbitrary endpoints. It also verified the exact printed quantifiers and found no earlier general all-count proof. The mixed-length audit checked the independent uniform short-interval estimate, disjoint squareclass realization, and the finite dominant-length case split.

No Lean verification, external review or absolute priority claim is made.
