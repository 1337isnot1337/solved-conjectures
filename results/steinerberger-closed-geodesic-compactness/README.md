# Steinerberger’s closed-geodesic compactness problem

[Steinerberger (2019), Question 1.3](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0004972718001636) asks whether maximal absolute closed-geodesic averages are attained, with a length principle like that on the flat two-torus, in more general settings.

- [Hyperbolic surfaces](HYPERBOLIC.md): no. Every closed orientable hyperbolic surface admits a mean-zero smooth base function whose supremum is not attained, even when only simple closed geodesics are allowed.
- [Compact flat manifolds and compact connected Lie groups](ATTAINMENT.md): exact attainment for continuous functions, with a bi-invariant metric in the Lie-group case.
- [Length on flat tori](LENGTH.md): exact optimizing length is not locally bounded in the smooth topology in dimensions at least three. For band-limited data the optimal length scale is sharp, as is an approximate length bound for Hölder data.
- [Prior-art audit](AUDIT.md): literature checks and limits of the claims.

This settles the hyperbolic extension in the negative and gives a positive qualitative group result, not a classification of all Riemannian manifolds. No Lean verification is included.
