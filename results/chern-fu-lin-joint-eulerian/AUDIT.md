# Scope and independent audit

The exact statement is [Chern–Fu–Lin Conjecture 6.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2209.12137#S6). This is separate from the two unweighted equalities in Burstein’s conjecture and from Chern–Fu–Lin Conjecture 6.1. We use the separately documented [maximum-insertion tree](../burstein-a218225-enumeration/PROOF.md).

An independent internal audit reconstructed both systems and checked both universal formal polynomial identities by an alternative exact GMP calculation. It also compared brute permutation statistics through length 7 and weighted tree counts through length 13. The supplied standalone certificate passed both lanes with exact zero pseudo-remainders; this is not a finite truncation. A search for the exact class and joint statistics did not locate an earlier proof. No priority, external review or Lean verification claim is made.
