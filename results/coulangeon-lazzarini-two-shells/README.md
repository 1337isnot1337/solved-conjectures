# Coulangeon–Lazzarini’s two-shell conjecture

**Refuted.** An integral lattice in dimension ten has first two shells that are spherical 2-designs and a third that is not. The first two are cubic shells, of squared norms 4 and 8. The third lies in a coordinate hyperplane and has squared norm 9.

The proof uses a cubic lattice and one extra residue class. A second construction gives orthogonally indecomposable lattices with an arbitrarily long initial run of 2-design shells before a failing shell. Hence no fixed number of initial shells suffices across dimensions.
