# Scope and independent audit

The exact [Conjecture 6.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2209.12137#S6) specifies the set of positions of left-to-right minima as well as both counts. The authors’ earlier single-statistic marginal, the unweighted Burstein result and Conjecture 6.2 are distinct.

Independent review checked the min decomposition and all crossing cases, the genuine statistic weights, the maximum-insertion transitions, nonzero kernel denominators and simple formal root. The symbolic certificate passed independently, and comparison of 92,466 literal class memberships at lengths up to 8 passed. The supplied separate length-13 comparison was also checked by its author; counts are auxiliary to the all-order proof.

No Lean, independent outside review, or absolute priority claim is made.
