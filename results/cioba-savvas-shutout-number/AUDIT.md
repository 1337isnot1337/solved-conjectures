# Audit and priority record

- **Exact target:** Cioba–Savvas, [*Shutout Games on Graphs*, Mathematika 61 (2015), 523–530](https://doi.org/10.1112/S0025579314000382), [author text, Definition 2.2 and Conjectures 3.2–3.4](https://michailsavvas.github.io/documents/Shutout.pdf). The exponential reading of §3 is explicit in the comparison to the weaker polynomial conjecture, despite the printed base quantifier allowing trivial bases. The proof supplies base two. The author's free-star test is checked after Breaker's reply; the proved immediate-win prevention is stronger. No assertion is made about the isolated numerical question in Conjecture 3.4.
- **Priority checks (2026-11-26):** Checked the journal record and author PDF, indexed citation/title/DOI searches, exact and alternative shutout/free-star names, MathDB/MathOverflow/arXiv indexes, both catalogue refs, matching issues and review branches. Checked [Beck's primary monograph](https://www.cs.umd.edu/users/gasarch/COURSES/752/S26/BOOKS/Combgamesttt.pdf), including the elementary size-dependent shutout criterion and its later lattice-game applications; the general local-criterion discussion concerns the distinct neighborhood conjecture. Checked [Leader, Hypergraph Games, Lemmas 13–14](https://tartarus.org/gareth/maths/notes/iii/Hypergraph_Games.pdf), which bounds the number of winning lines, not the maximum host degree. Checked [Chen–Chen–Yang, arXiv:2608.20644v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.20644v1), including its concluding remarks: its objective is a lower bound on the final minimum Breaker degree, and the paper does not give a shutout result. No prior full resolution was found in this bounded search.
- **Attribution:** The Shearer-region update on a move that affects a clique is from the Chen–Chen–Yang degree-game argument, with an initialization proof included. The new application kills the one-hit risk permanently and uses an extra step of safety to deal with the immediate win and feasibility of an actual edge claim.
- **Separate mathematical audit:** Re-derived the clique test by conditioning the independent-set sum on its unique possible clique element, and checked the monotonicity comparison including neighbors versus non-neighbors. Checked that the two virtual weight changes average to the old feasible point and are tested by the *same* linear inequality with off-clique coefficients fixed. Checked the actual reply: either live vertex has an unclaimed edge even after Maker's increment, since its Maker degree is at most one less than its host degree and it has no Breaker edge; killing another endpoint of the reply only decreases risk. Checked odd board size, threshold equality, low-degree vertices and the distinction between the two timing conventions. This is a separate argument audit, not a claim of external referee review.
- **Executable checks:** `python3 results/cioba-savvas-shutout-number/verify.py` independently enumerates the independent-set polynomial in all labeled graphs on five vertices for a three-value activity grid, comparing with deletion recurrence and testing every relevant two-endpoint update. It checked 123,093 positive states and 127,850 endpoint updates. It also executed 432 games on nine host graphs satisfying the theorem's degree test, with 8,736 legal Breaker replies and immediate-win checks. These are regression tests, not premises of the proof. No Lean formalization.
