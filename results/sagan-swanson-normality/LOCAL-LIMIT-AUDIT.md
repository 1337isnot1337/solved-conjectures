# Scope and independent audit

**Consolidation:** The interior-proportion central limit theorem was already proved in issue #92. The stronger uniform lattice local limit, proved in Section 5, is retained here. The old and newer materials concern one original conjecture and are credited only once.

The target is [Sagan–Swanson, published Conjecture 7.8](https://users.math.msu.edu/users/bsagan/Papers/Old/qStB-pub.pdf#page=32): all four families when the parameter ratio tends to a strictly interior number. Fixed-index results, bottom-heaviness, and [the prior Conjecture 7.9 result](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.08187) have different scopes.

Independent review checked the type-A and type-B symmetric-function formulas, exact occupancy conditioning, real tilting and uniform Fourier decay, the true first and second moments, and the covariance-to-conditional-variance calculation. The supplemental uniform local limit gives a peak profile, not a global large-deviation shape. No finite simulation is used for the theorem.

No Lean verification, external review or absolute priority claim is made.
