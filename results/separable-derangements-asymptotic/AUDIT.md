# Scope and independent audit

The exact [Conjecture 14](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.27583v1#S6) requires an asymptotic counting exponent strictly greater than 3/2. The proof rules out the full proposed interval. It remains compatible with convergence to zero, proved separately, and does not assert an exact rate. The source’s polynomial lower bound and the earlier occupation recurrences are credited.

Independent audit checked the two nonlinear occupation equations and saturation, the heat-kernel and summation estimates, conditioning on negative history, exact uniform size and displacement inequalities, the average over terminal sizes and target diagonals, the coefficient lower bound, and disjointness over both macro levels. The improved square-summability estimate can be obtained directly from symmetry by summing heat-kernel values at i+j, and was also independently checked.

No Lean, outside review or absolute priority claim is made.
