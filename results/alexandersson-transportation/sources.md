# Source and priority

Per Alexandersson states the conjecture for full-support tables of arbitrary positive integral margins. Both margin vectors must contain a nonunit entry. The primary research issue opened November 22, 2026. Its November 23 status audit records an earlier counterexample to a separate extension permitting forbidden cells. There a forced final row leaves a Birkhoff polytope. The November 24 update reports computations on one-parameter families and keeps the full-support question open. The present family allows every cell. [Author’s conjecture](https://www.symmetricfunctions.com/realRootedOpenProblems.htm) · [Primary research issue](https://github.com/PerAlexandersson/RealRooted/issues/2627) · [Interval-support counterexample](https://github.com/PerAlexandersson/RealRooted/issues/2627#issuecomment-7110080597) · [Prior computational update](https://github.com/PerAlexandersson/RealRooted/issues/2627#issuecomment-7144671629)

The related root-polytope literature identifies the Ehrhart numerator of the edge polytope of a connected bipartite graph with its interior polynomial. The present polytope projects by its first two rows onto the convex hull of the lattice points $(e_i,e_j)$ with all $i,j$ allowed except $i=j$ at a unit column. In graph terms this is $K_{m+1,m+1}$ with a matching of size $m$ deleted. We checked this alias as well as the original transportation terminology. Root nonreality for other graph polytopes and unit-margin examples precedes this question. [Kálmán–Postnikov](https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.04449) · [Ohsugi–Tsuchiya](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.12258)
