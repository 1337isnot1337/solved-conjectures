# Scope and independent audit

The literal generalized clause is [Gawron–Miska–Ulas Conjecture 5.2](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00605-017-1041-2). The exponent-5 and exponent-9 formulas were already proved by [Shen–Wang](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28718) and are not challenged here.

An independent audit reproduced both signed integer coefficients using the logarithmic derivative, separately reproduced the mod-4096 values for k=11,12,13 by packed multiplication of the original finite product, and checked the no-carry lift to every k≥11. The first counterexample alone refutes the all-k conjecture. Searches for the exact statement and indices did not find an earlier general disproof.

No priority, external review or Lean verification claim is made.
