The original question fixes a vertex order and counts the distinct positive earlier indegrees in each acyclic orientation. The primary source includes a restricted chordal ordering for which real-rootedness holds. The counterexample uses a different order. [Original conjecture](https://github.com/PerAlexandersson/RealRooted/issues/2641) · [Prior-result search](sources.md).

For the finite witness I independently computed the support of each hub by inserting each page in its three possible gaps. I tracked the hub’s earlier indegree and whether a page supplied value one. For the padding graph I inserted clique vertices and independent vertices one by one and accumulated support by set union. This second calculation reproduced all thirteen coefficients and the total $2\cdot3^{42}\cdot10!\cdot11^{18}$. I also enumerated the directed edges on four smaller graphs and retained exactly the acyclic orientations. [Independent support calculation](check_unions.py) · [Original subset-inversion calculation](check.py).

For the family with even $r$, extending a directed edge to an attached triangle has exactly three choices. Conditioning on its direction makes the page choices independent, and separates the large hub indegrees with probability tending to one. The shared-edge limiting polynomial has $r$ simple nonreal roots. First fix the page parameter large enough to separate them in disjoint disks. At this fixed degree add stars at a common vertex to supply all lower positive indegrees with probability tending to one. Root continuity then preserves a zero in each disk. The family stays connected and chordal with treewidth two. [Written argument](proof.md) · [Independent issue review](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/823#issuecomment-7196051430).
