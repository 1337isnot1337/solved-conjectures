# Source and independent review record

- Exact scope: all four infinite Vega families, all homomorphic preimages with arbitrary missing template edges, arbitrary product weights of total mass \(N\). The proof also applies to its stated broader crown-and-apex template with an acyclic cyclic three-colour core. The unrestricted Erdős #23 remains open.
- Primary template source: [Łuczak–Polcyn–Reiher, §4.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2002.01498#S4.SS1). The four variants are accounted for by empty fibers. The [published Balogh–Clemen–Lidický density tails](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.14179) are an explicit dependency outside the medium range.
- Discovery and review: [research issue #1097](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/1097) and [reviewed source a05d756](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/blob/a05d756/research/erdos-triangle-free-bipartization/VEGA-TEMPLATES.md), source blob 19b16fe7093c99d39d313cda6b7bcc30027cb98f. The separate independent AI mathematical review on 28 November 2026 found no gap. It checked the primary-source crown and inner cyclic order, the three cut comparisons, the apex recoloring, the two complementary wrong-way crown link sets, the two convex endpoint ranges, and the use of actual rather than completed edges on the preimages. The publication proof expands the reviewed circular-core step inline and keeps the reviewed constants.
- Related literature checked: [Erdős–Faudree–Pach–Spencer](https://combinatorica.hu/~p_erdos/1988-12.pdf), the [Brandt spectral paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.00093), [Ferudun's medium-band and finite-order paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28041), the [Erdős Problems record](https://www.erdosproblems.com/23), the 2026 [Caro–Wei localization paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00210), and searches for Vega and Andrásfai max-cut, sparse-half, and bipartization results. The earlier Heinig theorem is on the Andrásfai family, not this broader Vega family. The finite-order and sparse-half results do not give the same weighted Vega statement. No earlier matching result was found in the bounded check.
