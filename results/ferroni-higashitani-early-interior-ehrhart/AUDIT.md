# Scope and independent audit

The exact question is [Ferroni–Higashitani 5.12(a), arXiv:2307.10852v4 §5.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2307.10852v4#S5.SS2), also in [*Examples and counterexamples in Ehrhart theory*](https://doi.org/10.4171/EMSS/86). The earlier numbering is 5.11(a). [Ferroni’s construction](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.10513v2#S6) proves the distinct part (b) at later indices and expressly does not settle part (a).

Independent internal review verified lattice integrality, original IDP, the six coset shifts in all defining linear forms, the interior strictness at residue zero, and preservation of IDP under the refinement and dilation. The optional regular triangulation strengthening uses the original Cayley-box triangulation and the cited dilation theorem; the IDP proof is independent of it. The exact replay and independent direct fractional-coset counting agree for the three tested parameters, including the 170-dimensional witness. The indexed inequalities alone prove existence and the unbounded failure ratio.

The Cayley-box method is credited to Ferroni, and the prior part (b) result is not counted. Bounded searches by both question numbers, the three early interior counts and the superlattice method did not find an earlier solution of part (a). This is a named open question; no priority or Lean verification is claimed.
