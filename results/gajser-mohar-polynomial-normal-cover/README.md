# Gajser–Mohar’s polynomial normal-cover conjecture

**Proved.** The extremal order of a graph with a normal cover by
cliques of size at most $`c`$ and stable sets of size at most $`s`$
grows as $`\Theta_c(s^c)`$ for every fixed positive integer $`c`$.

This resolves the polynomial question at the end of §4 of Gajser
and Mohar's paper, including the optimal exponent. It does not
determine their two-parameter extremal function or sharp constants.
