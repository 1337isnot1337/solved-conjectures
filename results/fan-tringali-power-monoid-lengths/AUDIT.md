# Scope and independent audit

[Fan–Tringali, *Power monoids: A bridge between factorization theory and arithmetic combinatorics*, §5](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jalgebra.2018.07.010) asks for these length sets in both original monoids for all Dedekind-finite monoids containing an infinite-order element. Their transfer theorem reduces this to the reduced nonnegative-integer case. [Tringali’s 2026 reduced formulation](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.15754v3#Thmconjecture1) extends to every non-torsion monoid and also follows. The several equivalent or implied formulations are counted once.

Independent internal review checked each unit-domino quotient, the missing square, uniqueness in all marker slices, the width constraints at height zero, and the equivalence between maximal cliques and high atoms. The no-carry map preserves decompositions of every possible divisor, not just the represented set, since all summands contain the identity. Thus it preserves atomicity and multiplicity. The supplied exact checker passed for all labeled graphs through four vertices; independent exhaustive literal integer factorizations matched all graphs through three vertices. In the reduced realization all divisors remain in the copied pure powers, where irreducibles and atoms agree.

[Reinhart’s earlier partial results](https://doi.org/10.4064/aa251028-7-7) include all two-element length sets. We credit these, along with the earlier transfer theorems. Bounded exact-author, title, and method searches did not find an earlier full solution. No mathematical priority, Lean verification, or external refereeing is claimed.
