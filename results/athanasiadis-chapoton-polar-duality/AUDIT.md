# Scope and independent audit

The exact [Conjecture 6.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.26916v1#S6) was listed as open in the November [Evidence Press release](https://www.evidencepress.org/releases/preorder-polytope-flag-spheres/). The existing nonpolar Conjecture 5.4 theorem of [Wang et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16037) is different. Luo’s gauge and Stanley’s transfer are credited.

Independent internal review checked the gauge and the inverse for tied classes, preservation of chain sums and of integral lattices, convexity at every mutation step, and the strict-order enumeration of the numerator. All 355 labelled four-element preorder cases passed exact checks for the polar map. Additional tests compared 67 Ehrhart numerators and sampled 13,450 convexity inequalities; the proof itself is uniform. The Luo results are supplements, not extra counts.

No priority or external review claim is made. [Lean verification](FORMALIZATION.md)
