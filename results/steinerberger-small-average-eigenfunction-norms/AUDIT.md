# Scope and independent audit

The exact target is the lower-average L¹ proposal in [Steinerberger’s March 31 problem list, §43, pp. 43–44](https://faculty.washington.edu/steinerb/openproblems.pdf#page=43). The separate upper-average L∞ question is already handled by Han’s Theorem 1.2 and is not claimed. The universal near-linear lower proposal fails: on the round S³, the sharp selected-basis cumulative scale is Θ(N^(5/6)), and on S² it is Θ(N^(7/8)). Neither statement classifies the optimal power over all manifolds.

Independent review checked Fourier block construction, the SU(2) coherent-state matrix coefficient bound, realification, and the universal lower orders. The smooth lapped and variable-width packet improvements were reviewed separately. The S² proof was independently checked through the harmonic Hopf pullback, fixed torus weight, Schur normalization and interpolation. Earlier lapped transforms, individual beams and Han’s separate norm results are credited. The targeted searches located no earlier whole-basis small-L¹ construction with this claim.

No Lean verification, external peer review or absolute priority claim is made.
