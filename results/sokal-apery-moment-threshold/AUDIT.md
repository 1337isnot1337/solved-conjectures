# Scope, prior work and independent audit

[Sokal’s original threshold conjecture, IHP slide 27](https://rtca2023.github.io/pages_Paris/DocM6/Sokal.pdf) asserts the Stieltjes property for all parameters at least one and excludes every parameter strictly between zero and one. [Edgar](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.10733) settled the case one. [Knowledge Sense’s October 2026 primary preprint](https://github.com/KnowledgeSense/math/blob/91f1bdd2a10abeb28acc742ce3ee884874caf124/preprints/Apery-Polynomials-as-Stieltjes-Moment-Sequences-October-13-2026/build/source/apery-polynomials-as-stieltjes-moment-sequences.tex) covers the positive side. Its introduction expressly leaves the excluded interval and coefficientwise conjecture out of scope. The new claim here is the excluded interval, which completes the original threshold with those earlier results. The alternative elliptic-period proof for the positive side is not claimed as a new resolution.

Independent internal review checked the Legendre addition formula reduction, the inverse Joukowski transformation and branch choices, the isolated saddle logarithm with continuous remainder, the Abel cusp with nonzero coefficient, and the separate quartic minimum at one quarter. The Hamburger supplement checks the signed cut discontinuities and positive beta–Poisson mixtures. Its compact representing measure at one quarter has positive mass at negative points; the negative-parameter obstruction applies Neuschel’s earlier oscillatory theorem. The positive alternative was independently audited, including the quartic normalization, elliptic continuation, at-most-logarithmic singularities, and finite mixture mass.

Bounded exact-source searches did not locate an earlier resolution of the excluded interval or the exceptional Hamburger representation. The later Lean checkpoint documents a separate checked scope. [Lean verification](FORMALIZATION.md) The coefficientwise conjecture is a separate open problem.
