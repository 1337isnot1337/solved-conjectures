# Prior art

The exact target is [Erdős–Galvin–Hajnal, *On set-systems having large chromatic number and not containing prescribed subsystems* (1975), Problem 9/C, printed p. 482](https://www.renyi.hu/~p_erdos/1975-24.pdf#page=58). Their Definitions 10.3 and diagrams determine the four noninduced three-edge obstructions; they ask for simultaneous avoidance on **exactly $`\omega_1`$**, with chromatic number $`\aleph_1`$. The paragraphs just before the problem cite individual omissions under CH, not the four-way assertion.

| Source | Scope |
| --- | --- |
| [EGH 1975, §§10–11](https://www.renyi.hu/~p_erdos/1975-24.pdf#page=44) | Gives individual omissions and some combinations, including $`\mathcal T_3,\mathcal T_4`$. It poses the simultaneous four-configuration question separately. |
| [Todorčević, *Forcing with a coherent Souslin tree* (1983), §1 Remark (4), p. 715](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9947-1983-0716846-0) | Gives the rainbow partition model used for the negative direction, not the positive hypergraph construction. The 2-bounded reduction in this bundle applies the model to 9/C directly. |
| [Komjáth–Shelah, *Forcing constructions for uncountably chromatic graphs* (1988), Theorem 3](https://shelah.logic.at/files/95727/303.pdf#page=6) | A regressive-bound construction for an ordinary $`K_4`$-free graph and a claim about coloring its **edge** subgraphs. It does not impose star neighborhoods or require an uncountably chromatic hypergraph of its triangles. This is the closest forcing template found. |
| [Hajnal–Komjáth, *Obligatory subsystems of triple systems* (2008)](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10474-007-6231-2) | Works on obligatory configurations in linear triple systems and a separate-versus-joint omission question. Omitting $`\mathcal T_0`$ (linearity) already rules out this proof strategy on $`\omega_1`$: closing countable initial segments under the unique completion of every pair shows every linear 3-graph on $`\omega_1`$ is countably colorable. |
| [Komjáth, *An uncountably chromatic triple system* (2008)](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10474-008-7179-6) | A forcing construction for a **linear** system omitting two short circuits. It therefore cannot be a witness on $`\omega_1`$. |
| [Li, *A Resolution of Erdős Problems 593 and 1177* (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24882) | Classifies obligatory triples with no vertex-set size restriction, plus compares exact chromatic-cardinal spectra. This is not the simultaneous forbidden family on $`\omega_1`$. |
| [Hajnal–Komjáth, *Embedding graphs into colored graphs* (1988)](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9947-1988-0936809-5), [Nešetřil–Rödl, *Partitions of vertices* (1976)](https://dml.cz/handle/10338.dmlcz/105676) | General graph vertex-arrow results; not the joint local suspension exclusion and $`\omega_1`$ order in 9/C. |

The exact-text, author-title, local star-neighborhood, suspension-of-$`P_4`$, loose-triangle, and simultaneous-omission searches did not turn up a prior resolution of this formulation. The negative direction depends on the published rainbow consistency theorem, not on new model theory. The positive forcing and its double pressing-down argument are the contribution. This search is not an assertion that no unindexed manuscript exists.
