# Source and priority check

- Primary statement: Leyou Xu and Bo Zhou, [*Diameter vs. Laplacian Eigenvalue Distribution*](https://journals.uwyo.edu/index.php/ela/article/download/8757/6821), *Electronic Journal of Linear Algebra* 40 (2024), 774–787, [DOI 10.13001/ela.2024.8757](https://doi.org/10.13001/ela.2024.8757), Conjecture 1.1 on p. 775. The same statement is [arXiv:2401.03777v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.03777v2), Conjecture 5.1. It counts the eigenvalue **values** in a closed interval, with multiplicity, not indices of ordered eigenvalues.
- Actual parent result: Xu and Zhou's [2023 LAA paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.laa.2023.08.013) affirmed Ahanjideh–Akbari–Fakharan–Trevisan's conjecture, the $`c=0`$ slice of the later conjecture. Xu–Zhou's [Discrete Mathematics paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.14127) gave the $`c=1`$ slice. The 2024 primary paper has the further $`c=2,d-3,d-2`$ cases. The existing vertices-on-a-diametral-path and strict threshold-4 approach of that paper is credited; the new step is one inertia argument covering arbitrary numbers of surplus vertices in breadth-first layer completions.
- Later neighboring literature: [Zhen–Wong–Xu (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.laa.2026.05.003) cite the earlier diameter results, then address eigenvalue counts in terms of girth, not the full diameter conjecture. [Xu–Zhou on signless Laplacians](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.laa.2024.06.019) concerns a different matrix. [The source-author bibliography](https://faculty.scnu.edu.cn/bozhou) lists the established articles; no later full resolution emerged in the bibliography or arXiv author/title/identifier searches.
