# Independent audit and scope

[Conjecture 1.1 in the published article](https://journals.uwyo.edu/index.php/ela/article/download/8757/6821), p. 775, and [arXiv v2 Conjecture 5.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.03777v2#S5) give the identical closed interval and parameter range. The earlier theorem for c=0 resolves a different, narrower named conjecture; prior endpoint slices are credited in the literature note. Larger c can be covered whenever the surplus condition permits, but no extra named claim is made.

Independent audit checked the full breadth-first layer completion, zero-extension energy comparison, reduction by tridiagonal congruence, marking count and duplicate-mark accounting, treatment of the zero eigenvalue, and sharp infinite family. The rational-inertia checker independently covers all 32,767 ordered layer compositions through 15 vertices and 40 sharp examples. The proof is analytic and does not depend on those checks.

The exact title, generalized statement and source identifier were checked against both repository branches and the public discovery record. The bounded primary-source, MathDB, and indexed external searches located no earlier proof of the full conjecture. No Lean verification, external peer review, or absolute priority certification is claimed.
