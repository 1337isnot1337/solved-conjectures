# Scope and independent audit

The source is [Féray, Oberwolfach Reports 2/2026, pp. 146–147](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/53596?nt=1#page=66). Conjecture 1 is the uniform limit for the length of the cycle containing 1, divided by n. Conjecture 2 is the Poisson–Dirichlet limit for normalized ranked lengths. The report also states the stronger total variation conjecture for this exact word. The proof covers all three descriptions as one family. It does not cover arbitrary non-power words.

Independent review checked the word substitution, conditional conjugacy-class independence, parity-coset transfer, character bounds and Witten zeta, and both cycle-statistic implications. The sharp supplement was separately audited, including the standard character, sign twists, exceptional ranks and summation of the remaining Fourier tail. The class-product estimates and existing word-measure results are cited; bounded primary, code and public result searches located no earlier resolution for this exact word.

No Lean verification, external peer review or absolute priority claim is made.
