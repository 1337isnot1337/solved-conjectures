# Scope and independent audit

[Dukes’s Conjecture 5.6](https://doi.org/10.37236/3583) asks for strict interlacing with a shared zero across successive lengths. The exact meaningful range is n≥3; at n=2 the source’s own appendix lists x, which has no negative root, and at n=1 a negative number of passes is not defined. The proof is of the all-length nondegenerate conjecture; the printed degeneracies are not separately counted.

Independent audit checked the paired-peak tail, nearest-pole estimates, derivative criterion, reciprocal symmetry and induction. The exact rational sign solver independently certified every transition from 3→4 through 127→128. This is not a mere bounded verification. The separate general t-revstack real-rootedness conjecture is not claimed.

No Lean, external review or absolute priority claim is made.
