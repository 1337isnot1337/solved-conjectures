# Scope and independent audit

The target is [Zhu’s unweighted conjecture after (5.15), §5.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2202.03793v2#S5.SS2), also listed as [Deb P1.9](https://bishaldeb.com/positivity/themes/SMSCHTP/hankel.html#p19). The positive result in Zhu’s Proposition 5.7 includes a weight absent from the conjecture; earlier Jacobi–Stirling triangle positivity and log-convexity are also distinct.

Independent internal review verified the symbolic coefficient −4, the boundary cases and signs of the signed Bessel measure, and the square-root transform uniqueness argument. A separate numerical cross-check covered 260 moments including threshold and degenerate-order cases. The disproof and sharp threshold do not rely on those numerical tests. Searches did not locate an earlier resolution.

No priority, external review or Lean verification claim is made.
