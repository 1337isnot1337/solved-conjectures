# Source and independent audit

[Benfield–Lippard, arXiv:2404.08193v2, §10.1, Conjecture 10.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.08193v2#S10.Thmtheorem4) is the literal parity assertion for both statistics throughout the paper’s range of exponents at least 2. It does not assume the symmetry equation and is not a numerical case.

Independent internal review checked the stable exception identity, existence of finite gcd-one generators, and minimality of every Apéry representative. The two exact residue formulas are classical and are derived from the definition in the proof. The stronger two-adic divisibility follows by repeated squaring starting with the square of an odd integer; the odd modulus generator is invertible modulo the claimed power of two. The proof is analytic and has no finite-test dependency.

Exact author/title and Conjecture 10.4 searches, and searches for a published identical parity application of the one-odd-generator property, did not return an earlier full resolution. The neighboring Conjecture 10.5 had an earlier resolution and is credited. No priority or Lean verification is claimed.
