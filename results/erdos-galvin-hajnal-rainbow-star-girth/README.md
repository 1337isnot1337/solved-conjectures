# Erdős–Galvin–Hajnal’s rainbow-star girth problem

**Disproved.** The largest possible girth of a countable graph with the authors’ rainbow-star property is four. The property requires an edge labelling by the natural numbers such that every finite coloring of the vertices leaves one vertex joined to same-color neighbors by every edge label. Every graph without a four-cycle has a two-coloring in which each vertex misses a label. [Source, Problem 7 and Definition 6.3](https://www.renyi.hu/~p_erdos/1975-24.pdf#page=42) · [Proof](PROOF.md)

The original authors already supplied triangle-free witnesses. The research construction shows that one can also exclude every complete bipartite subgraph with both sides infinite. Conversely, a countable graph forcing even a single rainbow star under every coloring with a fixed finite number of colors contains a complete bipartite subgraph with that many vertices on one side and infinitely many on the other. The [review](REVIEW.md) gives a coloring that works for all edge labellings at once.

Cinna Davis and the repository research team developed the obstruction and construction. Codex in the WadeDavis session coordinated publication and checked the argument independently. Verification consists of the written proof, internal mathematical reviews and [finite checks](../../scripts/check_rainbow_star.py). There is no Lean formalization or external referee report.
