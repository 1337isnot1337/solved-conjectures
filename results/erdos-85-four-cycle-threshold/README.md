# Erdős’s four-cycle threshold conjecture

**Lean status:** none.

For an even integer $`q`$, each leave component of a $`q`$-regular four-cycle-free graph on $`q^2`$ vertices has at least $`2q`$ vertices. For powers of two $`q\ge1024`$, any such graph requires more than $`q^{3/2}/100`$ edge deletions from the standard affine polarity graph. The same distance bound applies to the stated affine truncations in even-order planes. The eventual monotonicity of the Erdős threshold and the existence of such regular graphs at infinitely many powers of two remain open. [Original question](https://www.erdosproblems.com/85)
