# Scope and independent audit

The exact proposed logarithmic estimate appears in [Steinerberger, *Some Open Problems*, §20, p. 22](https://faculty.washington.edu/steinerb/openproblems.pdf#page=22). The result treats every singleton start and every tie-break, not arbitrary initial multipoint seeds. The squared Fourier energy and uniform potential claims are distinct and are not refuted.

Independent review verified the dyadic carry, all phases and the sharp upper bound and alternating-binary lower bound. It also verified 12,960 Fourier magnitudes from exact rational minimization in five runs without the block characterization. That characterization was already given by [Pausinger](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10231-020-00990-7). The same number occurs in [an earlier discrepancy theorem](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00013-021-01688-9), and [classical star discrepancy](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00013-024-02087-6) also uses a related digit statistic, but neither is the truncated harmonic-weighted sum of absolute Fourier magnitudes. Searches did not find an earlier treatment of this exact conjecture. This written-proof audit does not claim external peer review or a priority guarantee. The subsequent [Lean statement and source review](LEAN_REVIEW.md) verifies a complete disproof of the original logarithmic Fourier conjecture using one admissible infinite sequence. The sharp limsup constant and the all-initialization and all-tie-breaking supplements remain outside that Lean scope.
