## Source and duplication checks

In Definition 2.6 the original authors permit exchanging the two roles of the prefix type. In the stronger assertion of Theorem 2.7 the chronological order of the two arguments of the type function is fixed and the next point of the earlier ladder must exceed each given bound. Their theorem proves sufficiency of bounded widths. Theorem 2.8 assumes club guessing. The existing solution of Question 6.1 classifies ordinary guessing in the indicated forcing models, and the existing strong-guessing supplement already obtains stationary sets with fixed matching full prefixes for bounded depth and lower reach. Exchanging the names of those two stationary sets realizes the normalized word, since either stationary set accumulates below club many later ordinals. [Lambie-Hanson and Uhrik, pp. 5–7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.01828#page=5) · [Existing strong-guessing theorem on main](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/blob/f32dec08/results/lambie-hanson-uhrik-disjoint-type-guessing/STRONG-GUESSING.md) · [Davis’s stationary-prefix proof on research](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/blob/4b6c6db6/work/1061-lambie-hanson-uhrik-disjoint-type-guessing/STATIONARY-RECTANGLES.md)

The threshold for the next point is a genuinely separate assertion, because the two fixed prefix fibers alone do not imply that any selected coordinate can be varied cofinally. Both permanent branches, #1061, #1062, the follow-up draft #1075, and the original authors’ other uses of the tail condition were compared. Searches under disjoint-type guessing, strong guessing, ladder prefixes, unbounded widths, reversed roles and cofinal next coordinates found the source bounded-width theorem and club-guessing consequences. This bounded search found no preceding classification with literal mixed orientations or the earliest cofinal coordinate in the role-exchange theorem. The graph-growth question is a separate target. ## Proof checks

- In the forward proof, unbounded widths allow selection of an unused color whose last prefix point clears the highest earlier cut. For any record at the model ordinal, all finitely many decisions and thresholds are below that ordinal or belong to the countable ground forcing. Elementarity therefore gives one common smaller ordinal copying every decision, including incompatible tags. The clipped bound tests the copy exactly as needed because it lies above every originally smaller flag bound.
- A high-threshold flag cannot hit an inner tail. A flag with low cutoff cannot hit a record whose final prefix point is at least the cut. Otherwise the same-tag proxy supplies exactly the finite data needed on the inner side. These arguments preserve the guards in the amalgam. Each vertex has a dense collection of forcing tags, so its eventual choice comes from the generic filter. The exact flag at the later vertex bounds every incoming oriented match at that color. This covers new sequences after countable forcing.
- In the role-exchange proof, the set of possible next coordinates belongs to the model at height $`\delta_d`$ and contains $`\delta_d`$, so it is unbounded. The resulting selection forces the earlier ladder’s point of index $`d`$ itself above the bound. Closing later ordinals under this selection gives an earlier vertex for every requested bound at each one of stationarily many later vertices. Only the entries of their fixed prefix have to lie below the model height. Separated words, positive depth with zero reach, and the smallest allowed widths are included. When $`d>0`$ the point of index $`d-1`$ is trapped below the last later prefix point in either assignment.
- For a literal word ending in 1, the selected width is smaller than the common model-chain length. Reflecting stationary fibers of length $`n+1`$ leaves unboundedly many actual values of the next point after choosing the first $`n`$ points in their prescribed gaps. For a word ending in 0 the reversed-role argument yields the literal chronological order. In either failing subfamily, relabelling the avoidance coloring by its original indices precludes witnesses in every other color.
