# Fernandes et al.’s two-token chromatic conjecture

**Disproved.** A non-complete graph on eleven vertices has
chromatic number six while its two-token graph has chromatic number five.
This refutes Conjecture A.1 of Fernandes et al. in full.
A complete-join construction gives arbitrarily large counterexamples
of both parities.
