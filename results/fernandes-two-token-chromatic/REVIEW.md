# Independent review receipt

A separate internal research worker independently transcribed
the cycle factors in the [pinned hand proof](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/blob/192d2d23938c23913f5a7ca70614411bbdae7517/research/fernandes-two-token-chromatic/PROOF.md).
The review confirmed that the rows partition all 55 unordered
pairs, that the 24 two-step pairs induce no triangle among all
165 triples, and that the coloring is proper on all 279 token
adjacencies. The fixed-token subgraph then proves equality in
both chromatic numbers. The reviewer compared the conclusion
to the exact Conjecture A.1 in the published paper.

The review also supplied the general [join extension](JOINS.md).
Its matching palettes and fixed-token lower bound were checked
against the same adjacency definition. The standalone verifier
checks mixed joins at orders 17, 22, 29, 44 and 58 as regression
tests for the written proof.
