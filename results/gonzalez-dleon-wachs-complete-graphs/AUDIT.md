# Scope and independent audit

The exact target is [Question 7.1 in González D’León–Wachs, arXiv:2608.08692v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.08692v2#S7.SS1). The existing two-variable case and general weighted inverse series are prior. This establishes the explicit equal-variable formula for every number of variables greater than two, but not Schur-log-concavity.

Independent review checked both rank conventions, Lagrange inversion, the Laguerre normalization and positive roots, the Gamma-rate convention, the coefficient convolution and the exact real-zero count. The exact weighted-bond recurrence independently agreed in 64 cases. A focused search located no prior general formula. This is an independent open-question answer, not a named conjecture count.

No Lean verification, external review or absolute priority claim is made.
