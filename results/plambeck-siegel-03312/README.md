# Plambeck–Siegel infinite quotient conjecture for 0.3312

**Status:** proved. The full misère quotient of 0.3312 is infinite, answering Kai Liang’s Open Problem 6.1 and the conjectured infinitude of the last of Plambeck and Siegel’s four outstanding quaternary quotients. Not Lean-formalized.

An exact finite transfer certificate determines the outcome of every position of at most three heaps of arbitrary sizes. It produces infinitely many pairwise inequivalent single heaps, distinguished by pairs of equal heaps. The full quotient has not been classified.
