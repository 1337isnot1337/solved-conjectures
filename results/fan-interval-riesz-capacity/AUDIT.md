# Scope and independent audit

The [published Conjecture 4.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.11186v1#S4.SS1) and its larger one-dimensional [Clark–Laugesen Conjecture 12](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.14809) are credited. Clark–Laugesen’s Proposition 3 and Theorem 2 cover earlier parameter endpoints and the length limit. Their later account describes Liu’s earlier result as maximizing the opposite ratio; that two-point result is not claimed here. The higher-dimensional conjecture is not addressed.

An independent internal audit checked the interval equilibrium, all parameter endpoints, sufficiency of the observed chord, the additive garbling and conditional Jensen with a singular quantile derivative. It also checked the distinction between intervals and the polar-appendage equality cases. I did not locate an earlier proof of this interval maximum in primary-source searches. No priority, external review or Lean verification claim is made.
