# Scope and independent audit

The exact [Conjecture 15](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.27583v1#S7) asks for a vanishing proportion and is also [Vatter’s Question 4.5](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.16355v3#S4). This does not include the separate asymptotic-equivalent Conjecture 14, addressed in its own package.

[Omega #15063](https://github.com/the-omega-institute/trureturing/issues/15063) previously formulated the same normalized occupation lower bound and established useful comparisons and skew-child disjointness, while explicitly leaving the actual bound and limit open. The present route uses two count moments and a spine walk, not their comparison subsolution.

Independent internal audit checked the exact finite-record limit, lattice characteristic function, square-root Fourier asymptotic, child-disjointness and second moment, tail-event argument, and the finite-level transfer. The proof covers both root signs and every sufficiently large length, not a sampling statement.

No Lean, outside review or absolute priority claim is made.
