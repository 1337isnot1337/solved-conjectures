# Scope and independent audit

The exact target is [Han arXiv:2609.14023v3, Problem 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.14023v3), a real orthonormal basis in every harmonic degree realizing sharp growth for every basis vector for all exponents 2<p≤∞. The original paper already proved separate one-basis theorems for the two exponent ranges. This question is one separate open-question answer, not a count of the prior individual results.

Independent review checked both isotropic families, projection-selection and simultaneous orthogonal completion, the zonal gradient-localization radius, the Gaussian-beam duality estimates and the matching critical exponent. The finite-witness extension is an independent generalization of the basis-selection step. The primary statement and bounded priority search were checked; no earlier exact simultaneous result was located.

No Lean verification, external peer review or absolute priority claim is made.
