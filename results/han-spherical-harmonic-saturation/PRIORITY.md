# Source and priority

- Exact source: Xiaolong Han, [arXiv:2609.14023v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.14023v3), dated 12 November 2026, §1 Problem 1. It asks for a basis in every harmonic degree with sharp $`L^p`$ rates for **every** basis vector and **all** $`2<p\le\infty`$ simultaneously. In §1 it explicitly leaves this question open despite proving separately simultaneous saturation within each of the two ranges.
- Attribution: The zonal reproducing kernels, highest-weight Gaussian beam normalizations, rotation averaging and the use of a correlation as a witness for large norms are laid out in Han's paper. The one-witness completion by rotations of orthogonal pairs is Han's Lemma 2.7. The argument here does not claim those prior results; it constructs an initial orthonormal set with two simultaneous witnesses by isotropy and completes it by avoiding the forbidden angles for both witnesses.
- Earlier work: Han's [2016 paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1404.5016) and earlier Bourgain work treat the low-$`p`$ range on $`\mathbb S^2`$, not the full simultaneous statement. In particular, the unrelated upper-average $`L^\infty`$ question is already covered by Han's Theorem 1.2 alone and is not a new result here.
- Targeted public search: exact source id, current paper title, author and phrase combinations for the simultaneous norm problem, including site-scoped Hexagon, TruReTuring and MathDB checks, returned the primary paper and discussions of its separate ranges but no earlier resolution of Problem 1. This is not a guarantee of priority.
