# Scope and independent audit

The exact targets are [Kovalev, arXiv:2610.31555, Questions 6.5 and 6.6](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.31555v1#S6). They are two formulations in one question family, not separate named conjectures. Kovalev's preceding bounds and single-linkage reduction, and the prior lower bound for consecutive-size retractions, are credited. A bounded source and follow-up search found no prior general linear result.

Independent derivations checked branch contraction, the derivative telescoping, continuity across collisions, the moving-cut estimate, and Hausdorff stability. The explicit general bound is 2+32(n−1); the asymptotic order is optimal uniformly over target sizes for every nonzero normed space. The separate shorter proof in the consecutive case and the normed-space theorem both extend to spaces with a Lipschitz Wasserstein barycenter.

No Lean verification, external review or absolute priority claim is made.
