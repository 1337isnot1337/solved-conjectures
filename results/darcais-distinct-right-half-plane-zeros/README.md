# Charlton et al.’s distinct D’Arcais zeros question

**Answered yes.** The D’Arcais polynomials have infinitely many distinct zeros in the open right half-plane. More strongly, a positive proportion of indices has such a zero at a distance of order the reciprocal of the index.

The proof passes to a scaled entire limit. A strict Hurwitz minor forces a zero in the open right half-plane, and local uniform convergence transfers it back to the polynomials.
