# Prior-art audit

The exact target is [Jing Zhang, *Some remarks on uncountable rainbow Ramsey theory*, Question 5.5](https://jingjzzhang.github.io/EvenMoreResultsRainbowRamsey.pdf#page=15), [arXiv:1809.00649](https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.00649), published in [Proc. Amer. Math. Soc. 151 (2023), 865–880](https://doi.org/10.1090/proc/15928). The current author-hosted PDF still poses the exact consistency and PFA questions. This bundle addresses both with one ZFC counterexample.

| Source | What it proves or asks |
| --- | --- |
| [Zhang, §5](https://jingjzzhang.github.io/EvenMoreResultsRainbowRamsey.pdf#page=14) | Remark 5.1 gives a finite-to-one triple colouring with no 4-point rainbow. That colouring is not 2-bounded. Remark 5.3 gives certain positive countable order types under fixed finite bounds. The assertions about every countable order type (5.4) and the uncountable order type (5.5) are distinct. |
| [Todorčević, *Partitioning pairs of countable ordinals* (1987)](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02392561); [Moore, *A solution to the L space problem*, Theorem 1.1](https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~justin/Ftp/Lspace.pdf#page=2) | The ordinary negative square-bracket colouring on **pairs** is the sole non-elementary input. Moore's stronger rectangle result is an alternative source. Neither input is a bounded-fibre colouring of triples. |
| [Todorčević, *Forcing positive partition relations* (1983)](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9947-1983-0716846-0); [Abraham–Cummings–Smyth (2007)](https://www.math.cmu.edu/users/jcumming/papers/polyx_revised_dec2006.pdf) | Uncountable **pair** rainbow results, which have a genuine forcing-axiom boundary. This is not the question about triple colourings. |
| [Garti–Zhang, *Stationary and closed rainbow subsets* (2021)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.05935); [Jakob–Zhang, *A Topological Rainbow Ramsey Theorem* (2026), §9](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.03828v1) | Related pair-rainbow results with stationary or closed targets and higher hosts. Neither resolves the triple question. |
| [Todorčević–Zhang, *Strong colorings based on oscillations* (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05790); [Zhang's 2025 abstract, *Stepping Up to Higher Dimensions*](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/67825186f17ab571c8a6cd3d/t/68be6dc24dd1d61e7702729c/1757310402456/YST25_Book_of_Abstracts.pdf) | Strong ordinary colourings on pairs and higher arities. The accessible statements concern realizing colours on large subsets, not a 2-bounded triple collision. No access to an unpublished manuscript is implied. |
| [Livne's polychromatic-colouring seminar (2013)](https://math.biu.ac.il/en/node/864) | The listed negative results use pairs under extra hypotheses, or infinite arity. They do not state the finite arity-three ZFC conclusion. |

Searches for the paper, Question 5.5, normal 2-bounded triple colourings, uncountable polychromatic triples, and square-bracket or higher-arity transfers did not turn up a prior answer to this exact question. This is a bounded public-source audit, not a claim that no unpublished argument exists. The new inference is the assignment of at most one prescribed collision to each ordered top pair, so the strong pair colour is used as a matching instruction without enlarging any fibre beyond two.
