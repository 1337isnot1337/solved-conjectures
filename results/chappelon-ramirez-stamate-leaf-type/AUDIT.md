# Scope and independent audit

[Chappelon, Ramírez Alfonsín, and Stamate, Conjecture 4.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.15006v2#S4.Thmconjecture4) gives the specified residue-by-residue type bounds for leaves at all genera. The construction's excess over half the genus refutes all those ceilings for infinitely many genera; it is not an isolated numerical exception. Other conjectures in the article are not addressed.

Independent internal review checked additive closure and the absence of any effective generator, then counted the pseudo-Frobenius gaps in both intervals. For the general bound, a decomposition of the Frobenius number plus the multiplicity leaves a top gap that is not pseudo-Frobenius, excluding a whole residue. The number of exceptional offset pairs bounds the square-root deficit. The interval basis sizes increase by zero or one per step, so perturbing by at most two unused offsets achieves every large genus with the matching order of deficit. The exact finite checker is supplemental.

Exact author, conjecture number, additive-basis and optimal two-thirds searches did not locate an earlier resolution of the stated leaf claim or the matching square-root bounds. The standard residue and reflection bounds are credited as standard. No priority, Lean verification, or outside validation is claimed.
