# Steinke–Zakynthinou’s agnostic VC learning conjecture

[Manuscript (PDF)](paper.pdf) · [LaTeX source](paper.tex) · [Scope audit](SCOPE.md)

**Literal iid statement.** Lean proves that VC-one classes of disjoint finite supports defeat every proposed constant if the actual independently seeded output in Definition 3 is a measurable random variable, even after completing the iid experiment. The examples have trace Borel hypothesis spaces and distribution-uniform convergence in outer probability. The statement for standard Borel hypothesis spaces with completed iid laws remains open here. [Disproof and scope](SCOPE.md#completion-of-an-iid-law) · [Lean theorem](../../lean/Conjectures/AgnosticVC/ConditionalJointObstruction.lean)

With independent fair labels, the benchmark also forces iid CMI at least $`n/32`$ at empirical tolerance $`1/8`$, allowing improper outputs. The written proof derives the information inequality directly from the iid experiment. The existing Lean proof takes the generalization inequality as an explicit premise. [Fair-label proof](../../research/steinke-zakynthinou-agnostic-vc/completed-finite-groups.md#fair-labels-and-improper-learners) · [Lean theorem](../../lean/Conjectures/AgnosticVC/FairLabelObstruction.lean)

**Positive result under measurable selection.** For a binary hypothesis class of VC dimension at most $`d`$, with $`1\le d\le n`$, one randomized proper learner achieves both rates under the measurable-selection condition below. Its choice is independent of the data distribution. [Lean theorem](../../lean/Conjectures/AgnosticVC/Theorem.lean) · [Proof](PROOF.md)

| Guarantee for the same learner | Verified bound |
| --- | --- |
| Expected fraction of mistakes on **every** labeled training sample | At most the best empirical error in the class plus $`583\sqrt{d/n}`$ |
| Information the **entire returned hypothesis** reveals about the fair selector, conditional on an iid pool | At most $`19{,}712d`$ nats |

The first estimate compares with the infimum of ordinary zero–one losses. The second uses the joint distribution of the selector and the named hypothesis. Lean identifies its discrete information formula with Mathlib’s KL divergence from the product of their marginals when output singletons are measurable. For any other prescribed output measurable space, Lean proves that its KL is bounded above by the same complete-name information. [Arbitrary output measurable spaces](../../lean/Conjectures/AgnosticVC/CoarseKL.lean) · [Empirical loss and infimum](../../lean/Conjectures/AgnosticVC/EmpiricalInfimum.lean) · [Joint and marginal laws](../../lean/Conjectures/AgnosticVC/OriginalInformation.lean) · [KL theorem](../../lean/Conjectures/AgnosticVC/KLRates.lean)

**Lean status:** Partial. The verified rates retain the stated measurability hypotheses.

## Scope

The [scope audit](SCOPE.md) compares the original definitions with the formal hypotheses and gives a VC-one obstruction to unrestricted Borel selection.

**Companion paper, Conjecture 4.18.** The companion paper defines distribution-free CMI as the supremum over deterministic paired supersamples. Lean proves both bounds for this statement for every positive sample size, with common constant $`19{,}712`$ in nats and with the proper output class. For $`n<d`$ a constant proper learner suffices. The COLT bounty states its information requirement as CMI under every iid distribution, which requires a measurable learner and conditional law. [Framework paper, Definition 1.1 and Conjecture 4.18](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2001.09122v3) · [Exact Lean endpoint](../../lean/Conjectures/AgnosticVC/FixedPairing.lean)

- **Every hypothesis set and every fixed paired pool.** The information bound holds for each pairing of the $`2n`$ labeled positions, including repeated features and label noise. Compare both choices in a row to the same deleted sample, then sum the deletion charges on the selected block. [Fixed-pairing theorem](../../lean/Conjectures/AgnosticVC/FixedPairing.lean) · [Proof](PROOF.md#4-information-in-the-returned-hypothesis)
- **Every iid law for which the information is defined.** Almost-everywhere measurability of the exact pool statistic suffices. Lean proves that the statistic has finite range and hence is integrable. This covers every countably supported data law on a feature space with measurable singletons, even for uncountable hypothesis classes. [Population theorem](../../lean/Conjectures/AgnosticVC/MeasurableInformation.lean)
- **One measurable Markov learner for every iid law.** The construction uses the first hypothesis realizing each trace in any chosen global well-order. The same two numerical constants work for every choice of order. When these representatives satisfy the stated measurability condition, Lean proves the learner is a Markov kernel on the original hypothesis space. Lean proves the condition for every countable class of measurable predictors and for arbitrary classes on countable instance spaces with measurable singletons. It also suffices to put a countable subclass of measurable predictors first in the order, provided that it realizes every finite trace of the whole class. [Kernel and selection condition](../../lean/Conjectures/AgnosticVC/Kernel.lean)

A further sufficient condition uses the empirical risk minimizer constructed by Steinke and Zakynthinou in Lemma 4.15. If evaluation is jointly measurable and the first minimizer in a fixed global well-order is measurable at every sample size, the same randomized learner satisfies both bounds. [Formal reduction](../../lean/Conjectures/AgnosticVC/OrderedERM.lean) · [Proof and source comparison](SCOPE.md#relation-to-the-authors-empirical-risk-minimizer)

For a fixed labeled law, it suffices to test the ordered minimizer on iid features with independent fair labels, after completing that one experiment at each sample size. The learner and its constants are independent of the law. In the opposite direction, one VC-one class on the unit interval prevents every proper randomized learner on a completed iid law from attaining empirical excess below one on every sample. That class also violates VC uniform convergence. These results leave the prize interpretation open. [Positive theorem](../../lean/Conjectures/AgnosticVC/IidOrderedRates.lean) · [Counterexample](../../lean/Conjectures/AgnosticVC/LabeledNullChain.lean)

A separate example on standard Borel spaces proves that the actual randomized learner can fail completed-iid measurability for every choice of tie-breaking order, at every sample size of at least two. This class admits a Borel learner that exactly minimizes empirical errors at every size. Its existence alone gives no information bound. [Circle example](../../lean/Conjectures/AgnosticVC/OrbitOutputAllSizes.lean) · [Borel empirical minimizer](../../lean/Conjectures/AgnosticVC/OrbitMeasurableERM.lean)

A learner using local reconstructions does attain both rates on that circle class. Lean also proves the general extension for classes whose finite traces can be realized by countably many measurable proper maps using at most $`k`$ input rows. This gives actual iid CMI at most $`197{,}120d+32k`$ nats together with the same empirical bound on every sample. [General reconstruction theorem](../../lean/Conjectures/AgnosticVC/LocalReconstructionMeasurable.lean) · [Circle rates](../../lean/Conjectures/AgnosticVC/OrbitIidRates.lean)

Lean rules out globally Borel proper kernels even for a VC-one class on standard Borel spaces with Borel finite-trace feasibility. The written analysis verifies the usual uniform-convergence rate and gives a proper learner with both rates under completed iid laws for this type of class. [Borel trace feasibility and randomized selection](../../research/steinke-zakynthinou-agnostic-vc/borel-traces-and-randomized-selection.md)

Lean verifies a counterexample to the empirical requirement for completed-iid learners on VC-one classes with finite positive supports. The stronger statement only requires measurability of the real-valued expected empirical loss on one completed iid law. The classes have distribution-uniform convergence in outer probability by the written proof. Their hypothesis parameter spaces carry the trace Borel sigma-algebra. [Finite-support counterexample](../../research/steinke-zakynthinou-agnostic-vc/completed-finite-groups.md) · [Lean disproof](../../lean/Conjectures/AgnosticVC/CompletedFiniteGroups.lean)

The conditional joint-law theorem permits arbitrary pointwise probability laws on the hypothesis space and almost-everywhere agreement of the conditional kernel. The result still holds when the conditional law uses the ambient standard Borel space of all output codes. The proper subclass carries the trace Borel sigma-algebra. [Lean joint-law disproof](../../lean/Conjectures/AgnosticVC/ConditionalJointObstruction.lean)

A fixed VC-one class of two-point supports suffices when the scalar expected loss must be universally measurable on the sample space. The same class has a Lean-verified measurable envelope for uniform iid deviation with expectation at most $`2/\sqrt n+2/n`$ for every labeled law and positive sample size. [Fixed-class disproof](../../lean/Conjectures/AgnosticVC/CompletedFiniteGroups.lean) · [Uniform-deviation bound](../../lean/Conjectures/AgnosticVC/FiniteSupportUniform.lean)

With measurability under each completed iid law, Lean proves both rates for bounded-size, bounded-overlap supports. The same proper learner gives iid CMI at most $`20s`$ nats and expected empirical excess at most $`m/n+8/\sqrt n`$, where $`m`$ bounds support size and $`s`$ bounds pointwise overlap. [Lean theorem](../../lean/Conjectures/AgnosticVC/NoisySupportIid.lean)

For supports of unrestricted size, the same information bound holds with empirical excess at most $`(16B^2+9)\sqrt{d/n}`$ if the sharp outer uniform-convergence premise holds with constant $`B`$. Bounded overlap $`s=O(d)`$ then yields both conjectured rates. [Lean theorem](../../lean/Conjectures/AgnosticVC/NoisySupportSharpIid.lean)

For jointly Borel evaluation on standard Borel spaces, a written proof obtains both rates for every marginal dominated by a single reference law chosen in advance. The learner uses a countable subclass that misses at most $`2d`$ sample positions almost surely under that law, with a proper empirical minimizer on the exceptional inputs. [Dominated-marginal theorem](../../research/steinke-zakynthinou-agnostic-vc/dominated-marginals.md)

## Proof and verification

[![Lean build, axiom audit and kernel replay](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/actions/workflows/lean-agnostic-vc.yml/badge.svg?branch=main)](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/actions/workflows/lean-agnostic-vc.yml)

The learner gives each training mistake a random weight and regularizes the entropy of predictions on subsets of several sizes. Controlling the change in its output law when one example is removed bounds the information revealed by the selector. [Weighted loss estimate](../../lean/Conjectures/AgnosticVC/ProjectionWeightIntegral.lean) · [Deletion bound and rates](../../lean/Conjectures/AgnosticVC/Rates.lean)

To typeset the paper, run `tectonic paper.tex` in this directory. From the repository root, run `python3 scripts/verify_agnostic_vc.py --fetch-cache` with the pinned Lean toolchain available. It builds the theorem, elaborates all local proof files with warnings as errors, audits their declarations and replays their compiled proofs with the pinned kernel checker. The `verify.py` diagnostic separately checks selected finite-dimensional estimates. [Verification receipt](verification.json) · [Tracking issue](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/240)

The authors advertised **US$500** for a proof and **US$300** for a disproof of Conjecture 6. The measurable theorem is a partial result on that unrestricted question. The repository records no award. The real-threshold case has a separate result and prize.
