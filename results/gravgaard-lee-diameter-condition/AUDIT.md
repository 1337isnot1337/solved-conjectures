# Scope and independent audit

The precise question follows [Gravgaard–Lee, Theorem 2.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.12513v2#S2.SS1), not any of the numbered two-summand conjectures. The proof removes the separation restriction, including the total-diameter equality case.

The independent audit checked the exact Astels hypotheses and merge inequality, the stuck-merger contradiction, closure thickness, the existence of a genuine donor in the endpoint repair, and validity of the resulting infinite expansions. The exact equality example demonstrates that the threshold is not a vacuous boundary case.

No Lean verification, external review or absolute priority claim is made.
