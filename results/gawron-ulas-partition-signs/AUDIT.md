# Scope and independent audit

The exact [Conjecture 4.3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.05221) concerns an unordered partition product with parts congruent to 1 modulo 3. Ordered compositions studied by the same authors and the separate factorial-part Conjecture 4.4 are not counted here.

Independent review checked the dominated cusp limits, radial upper bounds and strict oblique gain, both for step 3 and all larger odd steps. It also checked root-filter uniqueness and the reduction from eventual sign periodicity or eventual one sign. Searches did not locate an earlier resolution of the exact original conjecture.

No priority, external review or Lean verification claim is made.
