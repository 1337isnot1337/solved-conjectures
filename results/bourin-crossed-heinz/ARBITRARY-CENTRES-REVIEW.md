# Review and source comparison for local validity at arbitrary positive centres

The theorem concerns Bourin’s 2009 Question 4, restricted to positive definite pairs of matrices within the stated operator-norm distance of one another. Their common condition number may be arbitrarily large. The bound works for all exponents in the open interval, all finite dimensions and all unitarily invariant norms. The earlier spectral-band theorem in this repository allows arbitrary separation inside its spectral interval. The current argument gives a second sufficient condition and identifies all equality ranks inside it. The full question remains open. [Original Question 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0805.1954#page=6) · [Theorem and proof](ARBITRARY-CENTRES.md) · [Tracking issue](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/issues/214)

**Mathematical review.** A separate Codex mathematical reviewer in the WadeDavis session read the complete proof. The review covered rescaling, the two resolvent signs, the scalar integral and its derivative at one, the simultaneous rectangular divided-difference formula including repeated eigenvalues, the paired coercivity bound and the relative Frobenius estimate. In the variational argument the reviewer checked both isometries, the numerical absorption coefficient at the closed threshold and all equality conditions. Zero difference on a maximizing subspace reduces the centre and both inputs, so the equality converse holds. The proof has no Lean formalization or external peer review.

**Prior results.** Sababheh and Bourin's first preprint version, Theorem 5.1, fixes a positive definite matrix with simple spectrum and a compact subinterval of exponents; its neighbourhood depends on both. Their Section 4 also records the ordered crossed-sum factorization used here. The subsequent version proves the two-dimensional assertion. Tian and Xu give the all-dimension operator and Frobenius cases. Kittaneh and Ricard obtain all unitarily invariant norms in the central exponent interval. None of these statements supplies the present uniform, explicit neighbourhood of arbitrary positive definite centres with repeated eigenvalues. The rectangular resolvent comparison and absorption of the subspace and isometry errors were derived in this repository. A bounded search for arbitrary or repeated-spectrum centres, local validity, positive matrix pairs near equality, spectral gaps, condition numbers and local stability found no matching quantitative theorem. Searches by both preprint identifiers and by the original problem distinguished the version with higher-dimensional local results. [Sababheh and Bourin, version 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.04567v1#S5) · [Version 2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04567v2) · [Tian and Xu](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22363v2) · [Kittaneh and Ricard](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.laa.2025.02.004)

**Exact checks.** A standard-library verifier runs 225 rational resolvent comparisons at three centre spectra and throughout the possible projection ranks. The centres include repeated eigenvalues. It checks the relative bound before integration, both ordered terms in the resolvent decomposition, the subspace leakage estimate, 80 pairs of complementary divided differences including coincident endpoints, and absorption at the exact boundary. These checks corroborate a dimension-independent written proof. [Reproducible check](verify_arbitrary_centres.py)
