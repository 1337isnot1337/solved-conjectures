# Scope and independent audit

[Hajdu–Sárközy, *On multiplicative decompositions of polynomial sequences, III*, Conjecture 1.2](https://math.unideb.hu/sites/default/files/inline-files/poly3_revised.pdf) asks for total multiplicative primitivity after changing a set of size little-o of the number of shifted powers, at every exponent at least two. The shift is one and the source includes the value one. The proof allows any fixed nonzero integer shift; that one-value convention and any later finite modifications do not affect the error hypothesis.

Independent internal review checked both signs in the crossed determinant, the integer power gap, the fixed-multiplier logarithmic common-neighbor bound, and the separate geometric summations for small and large row size. Counting errors is by distinct values, while upper-bounding genuine edges counts representations; injectivity for a fixed multiplier is what permits the transition. Supplemental exact diagnostics checked both signs and multiple exponents; they are not used in the proof.

The authors use a precursor two-sided geometric-rectangle analysis in §6. [Yip’s crossed-product argument, §3.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.14450#S3.SS3), credits Gyarmati, and [Yip’s 2026 article](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00493-025-00193-9) proves the perturbation theorem for exponents at least three. The contribution here is keeping the exact difference in skinny rectangles and summing across all boxes to settle the outstanding squares. Exact title, statement and recent-citation searches did not identify an earlier full square-case resolution. No priority or Lean verification is claimed.
