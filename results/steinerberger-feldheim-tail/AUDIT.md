# Source and independent review

The exact two existential bounds are in [§1.3 of Steinerberger’s 2018 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.00663) and [§24 of his March 31, 2026 problem list](https://faculty.washington.edu/steinerb/openproblems.pdf). They compare opposite parts of the same sum-tail event for i.i.d. absolutely continuous nonnegative variables of noncompact support, at some positive threshold. One proposed coefficient is normalized by the median, the other by the mean. The supplied distribution has a finite mean, unique median, and a positive denominator at every positive threshold.

Independent internal review checked the exact probability-ratio integral and the power-series and Gaussian upper bounds on the two parameter ranges. It checked both normalization inequalities strictly and distinguished an existential linear lower bound from a limsup or eventual growth claim. The smooth perturbation uses monotone likelihood ratio to bound the survival function from below, pointwise density domination for the numerator, and convergence of the two normalizations. No numerical check is needed for the proof.

The earlier [Feldheim–Feldheim paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.03004) proves a qualitative limsup theorem, and Steinerberger’s initial paper proves a different conditioning bound. Neither is contradicted. Exact ratios and both normalization clauses were screened using title/author, cited versions, and quadratic-hazard searches; no earlier full counterexample for these statements was located. This is not a priority claim, Lean verification, or outside refereeing.

The independent review of the [existential linear lower bound](LINEAR-EXISTENCE.md) checked the two-step quantile telescoping, possible plateau in the survival function, the two disjoint tail rectangles, and both normalizations. Searches for the exact follow-up question and distribution-free bounds at a median multiple located the earlier qualitative theorem and the distinct older bound at or below one median, not this result. No priority claim is made.

The later improved quantitative proof treats all nonatomic laws. Independent review checked both quantile rectangles, the bound below twice the median, and the convergent geometric computation for three times the mean. The original false constants and the distinction from eventual growth are unchanged.
