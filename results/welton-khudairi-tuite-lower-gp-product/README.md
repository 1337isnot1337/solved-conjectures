# Welton–Khudairi–Tuite lower general position product conjecture

**False.** For every integer $M\ge7$, two connected factors can each have lower general position number at least $M$ while their Cartesian product has a six-vertex maximal general position set. The conjectured inequality fails by an unbounded amount, even when one factor is complete and the other has diameter two.

- [Source and proof audit](AUDIT.md)
- [Exact graph checker](verify.py), run with the Python standard library using `python3 verify.py`

This is Conjecture 3 of the journal article and Conjecture 2.10 of the preprint. The separate conjecture that every graph has a terminal set is not resolved. The proof does not depend on the checker or on Lean; no external review is claimed.
