# Scope and independent audit

The exact target is the symbolic reciprocal equation of [Conjecture 8.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.26916v1#S8). The November [Evidence Press release](https://www.evidencepress.org/releases/preorder-polytope-flag-spheres/) left it open. A reported counterexample to Conjecture 8.1 on the three-element corolla is separate and does not affect this theorem.

Independent review checked both geometric averages, the Möbius computation and the final formal substitution. Exact independent checks of the reciprocal-support identity passed all 355 labelled four-element preorders. The proof does not rely on those finite checks.

No priority, external review or Lean verification claim is made.
