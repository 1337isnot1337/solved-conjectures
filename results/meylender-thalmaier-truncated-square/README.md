# Meylender–Thalmaier’s truncated-square conjecture

**Strict inequality proved; proposed optimizer disproved.** Proves the strict systolic bound for every truncated-square product and disproves the proposed optimizer.

**Lean status:** partial. Lean proves the exact Lebesgue areas and maximal four-shape covering scales of the parallelogram and rational pentagon, and the strict comparisons with the source’s trapezoid formula. The first comparison also holds near the boundary for every first truncation leg. Identifying this scale with symplectic capacity and proving the full strict bound for every convex body remain open in Lean. [Checked statements and receipt](FORMALIZATION.md).
