The approximants (1+z/N)^N+1 converge locally uniformly to exp(z)+1, and for fixed degree d and N>d their (N−d)th derivative has precisely the repeated negative root −N. Every limiting Taylor coefficient is positive. The identity L₁(f)=(f′)²−ff″ gives −exp(z), whose coefficients are all negative, contradicting eventual positivity.
