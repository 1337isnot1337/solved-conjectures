# Scope and independent audit

The target is [Roy’s Conjecture 3.2, Table 2](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.03153v4#S3.SS1), for the ordered inputs in the paper. [Gajdzica, Theorem 5.2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.16267) supplies earlier strictness when both inputs are at least 12.

Independent review checked every injection case, reconstructed all 35 initial counts and all 32 residue-seed differences from the non-one parts, verified the numerical table, and checked the propagation of both equality and strictness. Conjecture 3.3 and the distinct m-ary conjecture are not claimed.

No priority, external review or Lean verification claim is made.
