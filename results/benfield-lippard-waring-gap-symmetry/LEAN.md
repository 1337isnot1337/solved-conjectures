# Lean formalization

**Complete for the original conjecture (10.1) and intended finite-domain reflection statement (10.2).** This supersedes the earlier partial certificate.

Brennan Benfield and Oliver Lippard, [Integers That Are Not the Sum of Positive Powers, arXiv:2404.08193v2, Conjecture 10.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.08193v2#S10.Thmtheorem1), assert the maximum/cardinality equality for the stabilized exception set at power-of-two exponents. This formalization follows the repository's [existing proof](PROOF.md) (Davis / GPT-6.2 Astra attribution), particularly its independent L-shaped Apéry certificate. The original [bounded prior-resolution search and priority caveat](AUDIT.md) remain unchanged.

## Exact statement and scope

The checked source proves the strict reverse of the conjectured equality at both exponents 64 and 128. It gives the largest stable exception F, a duplicate-free finite list of **all** stable exceptions, and the strict inequality F+1 < 2 times the length of that list. It also produces a pair of positive reflected gaps in the intended reflection domain.

`Represents` uses a list of exactly j positive integers whose e-th powers sum to j+n; `StableException` is positivity and eventual nonrepresentability. `stable_iff` identifies this set with the complement of the additive monoid generated by **all** positive power offsets. `stabilized_matches` identifies the eventual definition with the paper's stabilized set whenever a uniform stabilization index is given. `exact_monoid64` and `exact_monoid128` prove exact four-generator truncation, not a finite cutoff. The reduced-pair certificate is checked by lattice cancellation, residue uniqueness, and a direct double-gap argument. `stableList_nodup`, `stableList_mem`, and `stableList_length` establish the actual finite cardinality. The two `stable_list_counterexample` endpoints give the complete source-matched disproofs; `not_conjecture10_1` and `not_reflection_conjecture` refute the respective assertions.

The proof of strict inequality does not require a Lean proof of the exact numerical genus formulas in the optional written checker. Those supplementary numerical formulas are not claimed to equal the actual genus in Lean. There is no claim about 10.4 or the asymptotic ratio. The two exponents and the reflection formulation count as one repository result.
