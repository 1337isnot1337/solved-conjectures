# Source and independent audit

We matched the conjectural equality of maximum and twice cardinality minus one in [arXiv:2404.08193v2, §10.1, Conjecture 10.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.08193v2#S10.Thmtheorem1). The proof refutes the full universal conjecture, not merely a generalization, finite-offset truncation, or stronger conjecture. Conjecture 10.2 has the same status under its intended finite reflection domain; it is not counted a second time.

Independent internal review verified padding with ones, reduction to prime bases, the modular packet bounds that remove every higher prime, and all residue classes in the gluing step. It checked both Herzog’s three-generator criterion and the independent L-shaped Apéry argument. The crossed relations reduce every pair, and the terminal shape has exactly one representative per residue, so the explicit positive defects are certified for the exact stable monoids. The [standard-library checker](check.py) passed at both exponents. At the time of that written audit, no Lean or outside review was claimed. The subsequent [Lean audit](LEAN.md) independently checks the full source-matched disproof; outside review remains unclaimed.

The [published Omega Institute result](https://the-omega-institute.github.io/trureturing-mdbook/Problems/benfield-lippard-2025-exception-set-oscillation.html) concerns Conjecture 10.5, not the symmetry conjecture. Bounded author/title, exact number, and semigroup formulation searches did not find an earlier resolution of the stated symmetry claim. Priority is not asserted.
