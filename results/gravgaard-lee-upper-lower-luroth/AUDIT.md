# Scope and independent audit

The exact target is [Gravgaard–Lee, arXiv:2506.12513v2, Conjecture 2.6](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.12513v2#S2.SS1). Theorem 2.5 assumes an upper bound larger by two. Astels's prior continued-fraction theorem concerns a different expansion. The authors' subsequent thesis repeats the same Lüroth conjecture. A bounded exact-statement and follow-up search located no prior full solution.

Two independently derived proofs were cross-audited. The first uses directed gaps with equality allowed in one bridge inequality; its endpoint step excludes all finite tails. It credits Moreira's earlier oriented gap technique in its exact scope. The second uses a linked interval refinement and each time explicitly repairs a possible zero tail. Both establish the ordinary half-open interval, not just its closure or density.

No Lean verification, external review or absolute priority claim is made.
