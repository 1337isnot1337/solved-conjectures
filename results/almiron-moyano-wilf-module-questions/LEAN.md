# Lean verification of the Wilf-module questions

Lean verifies Question 4.8 and all six clauses of Question 4.13 for normalized modules and positive integer thresholds. Zero counts as one of the minimal module generators. The development also checks the sharp threshold for modules with exactly two generators over a two-generated numerical semigroup. [Almirón and Moyano-Fernández, Questions 4.8 and 4.13](https://arxiv.org/html/2012.01358v3#S4) · [Repository proof](PROOF.md) · [Threshold classification](MODULE-THRESHOLDS.md).

| Statement | What Lean proves |
| --- | --- |
| Question 4.8 | For every multiplicity $m\ge5$, the exhibited numerical semigroup has Wilf threshold two. The maximum Wilf number among **all** its gap modules is exactly $4-m$. [Family and exact counts](../../lean/Conjectures/AlmironMoyanoWilfGapFamily.lean). |
| Question 4.13(1)–(2) | For a fixed numerical semigroup the least uniform threshold over normalized modules equals the multiplicity. Translating the naturals by a positive integer gives an unnormalized module with no finite threshold. [Uniform threshold](../../lean/Conjectures/AlmironMoyanoWilf.lean) · [Translated modules](../../lean/Conjectures/AlmironMoyanoWilfClassification.lean). |
| Question 4.13(3)–(4) | The sharp uniform threshold at any admissible generator count is the multiplicity. For a fixed semigroup it is the maximum of the exact thresholds over the admissible gap antichains. Every multiplicity $m\ge4$ admits two semigroups with respective two-generator module thresholds $m$ and $3$. [Uniform construction](../../lean/Conjectures/AlmironMoyanoWilfGeneratorCount.lean) · [Finite maximum](../../lean/Conjectures/AlmironMoyanoWilfFixedThreshold.lean) · [Comparison](../../lean/Conjectures/AlmironMoyanoWilfClassification.lean). |
| Question 4.13(5)–(6) | Lean checks the individual and uniform gap-count criteria at a given generator count. Every normalized module satisfies the individual inequality at its own minimal generator count exactly when the semigroup is all the naturals. [Individual criterion](../../lean/Conjectures/AlmironMoyanoWilfAntichains.lean) · [Uniform criterion](../../lean/Conjectures/AlmironMoyanoWilfClassification.lean) · [All-module characterization](../../lean/Conjectures/AlmironMoyanoWilf.lean). |

## The two-generator supplement

For coprime $2\le a<b$, Lean proves the least positive threshold for **every** normalized module with exactly two minimal generators over $\langle a,b\rangle$. It is $1$ for $\langle2,3\rangle$, $2$ for $a=2$ and odd $b\ge5$, and $3$ for $a\ge3$. The proof identifies those modules with the ones generated by zero and a single gap. [Threshold for actual modules](../../lean/Conjectures/AlmironMoyanoWilfClassification.lean).

For the upper bound at three, Lean computes the conductor and the number of gaps filled by adjoining a gap. If $g+ua+vb=ab$ with $u,v>0$ and $C=(a-1)(b-1)$, the new conductor is $C-\min(ua,vb)$ and exactly $uv$ original gaps are filled. The Frobenius gap witnesses failure at two for $a\ge3$. At multiplicity two, the proof calculates the conductor and left count of every gap module directly. [Gap coordinates](../../lean/Conjectures/AlmironMoyanoWilfTwoGeneratorGaps.lean) · [Conductor and count](../../lean/Conjectures/AlmironMoyanoWilfTwoGeneratorModules.lean) · [Inequalities](../../lean/Conjectures/AlmironMoyanoWilfTwoGeneratorThreshold.lean) · [Multiplicity two](../../lean/Conjectures/AlmironMoyanoWilfMultiplicityTwo.lean).

## Reproduction

The accepted closure reuses the original Wilf proof and the gap-antichain work on the research branch. [Earlier classification commit](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/commit/b1332652837e5b0fad563c28c9d67ece48994ae8).

From the repository root with the pinned Lean and Mathlib dependencies installed, run the following to compile the ten modules with warnings treated as errors, audit every owned declaration and replay the stock kernel.

```sh
python3 scripts/verify_lean_target.py --root Conjectures.AlmironMoyanoWilfClassification \
  --require AlmironMoyanoWilf.question_4_8_false \
  --require AlmironMoyanoWilf.gapFamily_wilf_maximum \
  --require AlmironMoyanoWilf.question_4_13_uniform_threshold \
  --require AlmironMoyanoWilf.generator_refined_uniform_threshold \
  --require AlmironMoyanoWilf.fixedThreshold_isLeast \
  --require AlmironMoyanoWilf.minimal_generator_count_le \
  --require AlmironMoyanoWilf.fixed_at_generator_count_iff \
  --require AlmironMoyanoWilf.wilf_at_generator_count_iff \
  --require AlmironMoyanoWilf.question_4_13_at_generator_count \
  --require AlmironMoyanoWilf.fixed_two_generator_threshold \
  --require AlmironMoyanoWilf.different_fixed_thresholds \
  --require AlmironMoyanoWilf.shifted_tail_no_threshold \
  --out /tmp/wilf-module-replay
```

The scoped audit passed for 427 declarations, including 386 theorems and 119 private declarations. All ten modules passed independent stock-kernel replay. [Verification receipt](verification/lean-receipt.json).
