# Scope and independent audit

[Hajdu–Sárközy, *On multiplicative decompositions of polynomial sequences, II*, Conjecture 1, p.198](https://www.impan.pl/shop/en/publication/transaction/download/product/112684#page=8) is the bivariate shifted-product conjecture for all exponents greater than one. Their Remark 2 settles the non-coprime case. The new conclusion is for the full remaining coprime range. Their Problem 2′ follows and is not counted twice. This is a different original statement from the univariate perturbation conjecture in Part III.

Independent internal review checked the integer crossed-products bound when different fixed rows use unequal scales, and the single-color bipartite rectangle bound. Arbitrary four different colors are not assumed to forbid a four-cycle. For a fixed cutoff there are finitely many ordered color pairs; the upper tail is summable when the exponents are unequal, and the equal exponents reduce to a single color. The review verified the order of limits and that overlaps only improve the upper bounds on distinct values. Ancillary tests included 3,615 literal repeated-column gaps, of which 1,561 had unequal scales.

Acknowledgments include the original authors’ geometric-box method, Yip and Gyarmati’s crossed-products gap, and the existing non-coprime cases. Exact formula, title and subsequent citations did not yield an earlier full resolution. No priority, external review, or Lean verification claim is made.
