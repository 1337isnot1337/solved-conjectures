On finite ground sets, the order representations here give precisely finite Boolean combinations of permutation graphs. To pass from order signs to permutation graphs, choose one reference order and use its pairwise comparisons with each remaining order to obtain the relative signs. Conversely each permutation graph is represented by a pair of linear orders. Adenwalla, Braunfeld, Sylvester and Zamaraev proved polynomial chromatic bounds in terms of clique number for each fixed finite Boolean function on permutation graphs. The present proof concerns an uncountable induced restriction and complementary products under BPFA. A bounded search under Boolean combinations of permutation graphs, Boolean order dimension, uncountable complementary products and the fixed-line basis located no earlier statement of the resulting uncountable canonical form or product obstruction. The finite-minimum assertion for finite ground sets is outside the new product theorem.
