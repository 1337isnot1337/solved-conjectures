# Review, sources, and scope

Reviewed 27 November 2026. The original question asks whether ZFC proves the existence of a graph on the first uncountable cardinal with two uncountably chromatic complementary factors and a countably chromatic categorical product. The [finite-dimensional theorem](ORDER-DIMENSION.md) settles a restriction to a class of ordered graphs and gives a necessary condition on induced subgraphs of arbitrary witnesses. The original existence question is open. There is no Lean formalization or external referee report.

## Attribution and prior results

Daniel T. Soukup recorded the problem in 2015. Lajos Soukup's October 2026 paper gives the cardinality theorem, the comparison construction under diamond and a ccc forcing of size the first uncountable cardinal that adds a witness. Cinna Davis used a special coherent tree and a tree with no stationary antichains after a single Cohen real to instantiate the comparison construction. Davis also proved the replication of the graph on disjoint uncountable fibers and recorded the PFA obstruction for two Countryman orders. The original research history is retained on the research branch. [2015 handout, Problem 8.1](https://danieltsoukup.github.io/academic/norwich_handout.pdf#page=3) · [2026 preprint, v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.08027v1) · [Davis's original manuscript](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/commit/481bc25b)

The present repository argument rules out every finite realizer under BPFA. Its ZFC step applies to every linear extension of every comparability representation of an uncountable induced subgraph. The argument uses Davis's replication theorem together with Krueger and Moore's fixed Countryman basis under BPFA. Reversing the orders independently and synchronizing their comparisons requires finitely many chain indices. A countably infinite realizer is outside this conclusion. The elementary finite and countably chromatic cases of the equality corollary use finite-poset chain decomposition and compactness. [Krueger–Moore, Lemma 12.3 and Corollary 12.5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.03581#page=21) · [Moore's PFA basis](https://doi.org/10.4007/annals.2006.163.669) · [Dilworth's chain decomposition](https://mathweb.ucsd.edu/~asuk/dilworth.pdf)

The bounded prior-result check covered the 2015 problem and the 1988, 2017 and 2026 uncountable graph product papers, searches using the arXiv IDs, complementary products, permutation graphs, finite order dimension, real and Aronszajn linear extensions, the Countryman basis, BPFA and its earlier PFA version. The 1988 and 2017 constructions allow two different graphs. Searches on both repository branches for the authors, identifiers and objects found Davis's research note. None of the sources inspected establishes the finite-dimensional BPFA obstruction. This is a bounded search. [Soukup, 1988](https://dml.cz/handle/10338.dmlcz/106592) · [Rinot, 2017](https://ems.press/journals/jems/articles/14452) · [Countryman basis and single-Cohen separation](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.05582v1#S5)

For the [component lemma](COMPONENTS.md), the arbitrary square cover by countably many graphs of functions goes back to Sierpiński. The full matching-step partition describes what one symmetric color already forces before choosing any enumeration. Searches for bipartite relations, matching pairs, simultaneous token moves, graph powers and Sierpiński square covers found no statement of that partition in the inspected sources. ## Independent mathematical review

A second repository reviewer independently checked the finite-dimensional obstruction. On a well-ordered suborder, the two cofinal fibers can be interleaved through all countable stages. On a real suborder, removing the endpoints of the countably many adjacent pairs still leaves an uncountable set in each fiber. An incomparable pair in that set has an intervening point in the original fiber. Two shared rational cuts let a vertex in a different fiber force the forbidden transitivity. Each step of the finite thinning retains the earlier signed embeddings, and all final chain labels fit in a countable set. The review confirmed that a graph of agreement of two orders is the comparability graph of their intersection. The stationary replication statement uses only countable unions of nonstationary or countable sets.

The same reviewer checked the disjoint matching moves at consecutive angles, the same-component distance reduction, and the countable vertex cover including single-edge components. A second direct computation built all equality classes from the defining two-by-two matchings. Run `python3 verify_components.py` from this directory. It checked 65,536 labelled four-by-four relations, 786,432 pair-state memberships and 183,563 partition blocks. This tests the finite structural lemma. The proofs of the infinite-cardinal and forcing assertions are written arguments.
