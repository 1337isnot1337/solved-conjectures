# Scope and independent audit

General attainment resolves the existence question. General uniqueness is false, and the source’s specific cubic attains exactly two uniquely modulo translation and nonzero scaling. The earlier Example 12.2 quadratic-distance argument and initial cubic witness are credited.

Independent internal review checked the cluster limit, root matching across diverging components, the sharp constant-perturbation lemma for cubics, normalization and equality for the original example. The example satisfies the literal question regardless of whether it is read specifically or generally. It is counted as a named open question, not another conjecture.
