# Scope and independent audit

The target is [Heim–Neuhauser, Conjecture 2](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11139-026-01435-0), for the ordinary sigma-one polynomials. Their integer-parameter result and finite-index result do not imply this statement for all real parameters. [Bringmann–Kane–Rolen–Tripp](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.08874) establish a prior real-parameter log-concavity threshold depending on the parameter; that is not uniform in the parameters here. Neither the earlier integer Chern–Fu–Tang result nor the separately proved sigma-two result settles this target. No prior full resolution was located in the author's statement and cited/citing searches. We make no priority assertion.

The independent internal review checked the centered Fourier identity and its tilt cancellation, divisor Lambert-series bounds, monotonicity of the normalized second moment used for variance comparison, and the signs of the rational enclosures. In each box the centered skewness is bounded above, the damping exponents are bounded below, and the exponential and square-root factors are bounded above. The finite bridge uses nonnegative shifted coefficients with a positive constant term. Its packed integer products have disjoint digit bins by the explicit coefficient bit bound. After factorial cancellation, the two compared expressions are precisely the root and Turán differences; the ratio induction covers the remaining root inequalities.

Exact replays passed for the 990 bounded saddle-parameter boxes and the final analytic regime, the root comparisons for indices 6–25, and the Turán differences for indices 26–500. The fractional Chern–Fu–Tang exception is explicitly credited to the prior literature and is not counted as new. Internal review and replay do not constitute Lean verification or external refereeing.
