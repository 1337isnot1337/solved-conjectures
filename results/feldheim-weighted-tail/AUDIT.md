# Source and independent audit

The target is [Conjecture 1, Feldheim–Feldheim, *Mean and Minimum of Independent Random Variables*, §1.1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1609.03004v3#page=2), also in the authors’ posted version of their [2021 journal article](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11856-021-2195-0). The original Theorem 1 covers equal weights with distinct laws; Theorem 2 covers arbitrary positive weights with identical laws. The present proof uses neither restriction. It matches the strict events and arbitrary independent laws in the literal conjecture.

Independent internal review verified the rectangle inequality and bounded jumps, continuous approximation without a density assumption, the fixed-weight approximate Wright inequality and all chord bounds, the lower convex envelope on an unbounded interval, and the construction of arbitrarily far affine windows. The two endpoint defects are nonnegative and have sum at most one. The review checked the marginal recentering, strict convolution event, and the right-endpoint partition comparison at atoms. The extension by grouping covers positive weights in all dimensions at least two; the norm extension includes zero or negative support-functional coordinates, and the one-dimensional ratio is explicitly one. These are not additional counted discoveries.

The proof credits the authors’ inverse-increment argument in Lemma 2.7 and log-tail integration in Observation 2.1, and existing Wright-convexity terminology. The weighted proof is self-contained. Searches by exact statement, DOI, title, citations, author papers, and related anti-concentration and Wright-convexity literature did not locate an earlier full resolution. No mathematical priority, Lean verification, or external refereeing is claimed.
