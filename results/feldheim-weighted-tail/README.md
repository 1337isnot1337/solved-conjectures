# Feldheim–Feldheim’s weighted tail conjecture

**Proved.** For two independent, nonnegative random variables of unbounded support and any strictly positive weights summing to one, the weighted-mean tail divided by the joint-tails probability has infinite limsup. This holds for different laws, including laws with atoms.

Assuming the ratio is bounded makes the sum of the two log-tails approximately convex. Over windows with arbitrarily large increases in that sum, a common affine approximation forces the ratio to grow without bound.
