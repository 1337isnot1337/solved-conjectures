# Bounded prior-resolution review of Chen–Khare–Sahi Conjecture 4

Reviewed 27 November 2026 UTC by Codex in the WadeDavis research session, independently of the certificate construction. The search found no earlier disproof of equation (4.5) in the sources below. The authors themselves had already described a related coefficient changing sign on the same interval, used the same pairs of partition shapes in a different example, and disproved semiring membership for reversed parameters.

## Statement and candidate

Hong Chen, Apoorva Khare and Siddhartha Sahi ask whether, for every ordered pair of parameters $`0\leq\sigma<\tau\leq\infty`$ and partitions $`\lambda\succeq\mu`$, the difference of the two Jack polynomials at $`\tau`$, each normalized at the all-ones vector, belongs to the Jack semiring generated at $`\sigma`$. This semiring allows every finite product of normalized Jack basis factors and normalized dominance differences, with nonnegative real coefficients. The reciprocal parameter convention gives Schur polynomials at $`\sigma=1`$. The arXiv record lists v1 on 24 September 2025 and latest v2 on 13 February 2026. The author-hosted February PDF agrees with v2. V1 calls the target a semiring in prose but prints the cone symbol in (4.3) and includes equal parameters. V2 prints the semiring symbol and the strict ordering in (4.5). [ArXiv version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.19649) · [v1 §4.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.19649v1#S4.SS2) · [v2 §4.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.19649v2#S4.SS2) · [Author-hosted paper](https://math.iisc.ac.in/~khare/papers/Jack.pdf#page=20).

The candidate uses five variables, degree five, $`\sigma=1,\ \tau=3/2`$ and the dominance cover $`(3,2)\succ(3,1,1)`$. Its coefficient of the **ordinary** Schur polynomial $`s_{(1^5)}`$ is $`1/4522`$. The broad version concerns the same shapes for every $`n\geq5`$ and $`1<\tau<2`$. In this range the ordinary and stable degree-five calculations coincide under specialization to $`n`$ variables. The substantive obstruction uses vanishing on the all-ones vector, positive leading monomials to exclude summands with two or more nonzero differences, and the column Schur coefficient to bound the remaining summands. A positive column coefficient alone does not certify exclusion from a semiring with products. The secondary witness screened was degree seven in eight variables, $`(3,3,1)\succ(3,2,2)`$, $`\sigma=1,\ \tau=11/10`$.

Thus source literature already supplies the sign interval at $`\sigma=1`$. Negative basis coefficients are permitted in dominance-difference generators.
