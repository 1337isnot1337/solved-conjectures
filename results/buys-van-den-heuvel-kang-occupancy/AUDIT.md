# Source, scope and priority audit

**Target.** Conjecture 12 on page 10 of Buys, van den Heuvel and Kang, arXiv:2503.10002v2 (14 November 2026), is the asymptotic lower bound with leading constant one on the expected size of a uniformly random independent set of a triangle-free graph of average degree $`d`$. Their separately named Conjecture 12 redux asks for the pointwise Lambert-function lower bound for every $`0<\lambda\le1`$. The original variance-and-entropy argument resolves the former and gives its counterpart as $`\lambda d\to\infty`$. The separate [pointwise proof](POINTWISE.md) proves the full finite-parameter redux by a differential comparison. The two nested conclusions share a single catalogue row. [Primary statements](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.10002v2#page=10) · [Version history](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.10002v2)

**Significance.** A lower bound on the number of independent sets and a lower bound on their average size ask different questions. Here the full leading coefficient gives the average-degree analogue of the sharp maximum-degree estimate. An expected size is also a lower bound on the maximum independent-set size, recovering the leading term of Shearer’s theorem by sampling. The authors discuss this consequence and distinguish the older constant-factor estimate on page 10. Their Theorem 5 supplies the partition-function input. [Original discussion and Theorem 5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.10002v2)

## Prior results and duplicate check, 27 November 2026

- Davies, Jenssen, Perkins and Roberts obtain the sharp occupancy estimate in terms of the **maximum** degree. Their conditioned neighborhood calculation supplies the local star law. Maximum degree can greatly exceed average degree. [Original theorem](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.01043)
- Theorem 1.4 of Davies, Sandhu and Tan gives a degree-sequence estimate under a restriction on fugacity involving the fourth power of maximum degree. The authors explain why this range does not establish Conjecture 12. Davies, Sandhu, Seo and Tan extend the range to a constant times inverse maximum degree, still insufficient at fugacity one for arbitrarily large degrees. [Theorem 1.4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.13396#page=4) · [Multivariate local occupancy](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05149)
- Zhang and Xu settle a scalar variance question with a maximum-degree parameter. The bound here uses the instantaneous occupancy in its denominator and leads to a relation between free energy and occupancy for each triangle-free graph. The low-degree and heterogeneous covariance calculations are already distinguished in the repository’s weighted note. [Zhang and Xu](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01717v3) · [Weighted note](https://github.com/1337isnot1337/solved-conjectures/blob/a028ccae5b30ff9856db665d75b4ffc6b73d7c4d/research/davies-sandhu-tan-variance/WEIGHTED-COVARIANCE.md)
- Maximum-degree guarantees for algorithms finding a large independent set and distributional existence results for locally sparse graphs impose different requirements from the uniform hard-core law and arbitrary average degree. [Davies and Tan](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02483) · [Davies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04654)
- Search terms included the three author names, the source identifier, both Conjecture 12 names, hard-core occupancy and variance, mean independent-set size, triangle-free graphs of given average degree, and the entropy integral.
