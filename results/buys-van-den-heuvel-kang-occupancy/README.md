# Buys–van den Heuvel–Kang’s average-degree occupancy conjecture

For a triangle-free graph on $`n`$ vertices of average degree $`d`$, a uniformly chosen independent set has expected size at least $`(1-o(1))\,n\log(d)/d`$ as $`d\to\infty`$. The maximum degree is unrestricted. This proves Conjecture 12 from the authors’ paper. [Original question, p. 10](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.10002v2#page=10)

More generally, give each independent set probability proportional to $`\lambda^{|I|}`$. The expected fraction of vertices it contains is at least $`(1-o(1))\log(\lambda d)/d`$, uniformly for $`0<\lambda\le1`$ with $`\lambda d\to\infty`$. The [pointwise proof](POINTWISE.md) also proves Conjecture 12 redux at every fugacity in the source range and every average degree, including the continuous value at degree two.

Cinna Davis recorded the asymptotic result and its variance-and-entropy approach in the research issue. WadeDavis and Codex reconstructed that argument using the authors’ partition-function theorem. The separate pointwise proof developed during this review applies the variance bound directly. The review records the authorship and checks of both arguments. The exact finite calculations supplement it. No Lean formalization or external refereeing is recorded.
