# Scope and independent audit

The exact [Conjecture 7.1](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00025-023-01966-8#Sec7) concerns the sorted enumeration of the entire exceptional set; the source already handles the individual layers. It supplies the exceptional set, not the all-base regularity result.

Independent review checked both exact floor identities, the repeated-digit input and the unbounded logarithmic remainder. It verified that powers of two transfer via their kernels and that the non-erasing morphic image in other bases satisfies Durand’s hypotheses. Searches by the exact authors, title, formulation and theorem did not locate an earlier proof.

No priority, external review or Lean verification claim is made.
